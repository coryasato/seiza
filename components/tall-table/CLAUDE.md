# tall-table

A GPUI table that reads rows a page at a time from a delegate and draws only where the viewport is, at row counts past what f32 pixels can scroll. A component (root `CLAUDE.md`, "Tiers"), crate `seiza-tall-table`.

---

This is the table's context. Why it's built this way is in `docs/DECISIONS.md`; what it offers its users, in `README.md`. Its work is a milestone in its own `PLAN.md` (the one list of its features). Each feature's own cost (added wasm, frame work) is measured in a bare host and recorded in `perf/results/`; budgets are the apps' that build it: Tycho, which adopts every feature, or the observatory, which only consumes it and adds a feature level when both its sides are built (root `CLAUDE.md`, "Tiers"). The repo-wide rules in the root `CLAUDE.md` apply here too. Paths below are relative to this directory.

Current state only: say what to do and point to the decision behind it (`docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Table and paging" and "Component API"); don't retell its measurements.

## Rules

These add to the repo-wide rules.

1. **No app knowledge and no `seiza` dependency.** The crate (`seiza-tall-table`) depends on `gpui-kit` alone; the bridge and the bare host beside it (below) are host-side crates, never its dependencies. Report what happened through `TallTableEvent`s; the host turns them into panel rows, marks, and published probes. What only a host knows comes in as settings: `Paging`, `ActivityOptions` (its clock, its window, and when to record), `probe_frames`, `probe_cells`.
2. **A frame never waits on data.** Rows whose page isn't loaded draw as placeholders; nothing in render awaits the delegate.
3. **Don't swap in gpui-kit's `DataTable`** without re-reading `docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Table and paging": its `uniform_list` places rows in f32 pixels and loses precision past ~16 M px.
4. **Events that mark a frame are emitted in render.** `Filled` (and `live`'s `Refreshed`, `follow`'s `TailDrawn`) is emitted while the frame renders, which GPUI delivers in the task that presents it (`App::update` flushes effects after the draw and present), so a host's `mark_after_current_task` lands right after the frame. Don't move it to a deferred or spawned emit. Tycho's `just tycho table --browsers` checks the marks land in the presenting task (`shared-web/perf/`'s `presentedMark`).
5. **A change to the paging is soaked before its code review.** A change to `pages.rs`, the state's paging (`load_wanted`, `page_done`, `set_rows`, `close`, the scroll and drag settles, `follow`'s tail pin (`pin_to_tail`; not `pin`, the columns' feature)), or `copy`'s reads runs `just tall-table soak` (minutes, not a run to ask about) before `/code-review`, and again if the review's fixes touch the paging. A failure is handled as the random test's are: a scenario test and the fix, or an exclusion until the fix (`src/state/random.rs`). Why: `docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Fixtures".

## Architecture

- **`src/state.rs`**: `TallTableState<D>`, the entity: the scroll model and page cache, hover and tooltip, the glyph measurements, and the drawing of the header and body. Every row height, padding, text size, and horizontal estimate is read through `sizes()` (the `Sizes` in effect; `padded` puts the cells' padding on a cell, the base's `px_2` without `density`), and the viewport's rows are fitted by `fit_viewport` (from `set_body`, and with `density` from `set_density`). Its public setters (`jump_to`, `set_rows`, `set_columns`, `retry_failed`, `close`, with `live` `invalidate_rows`, with `follow` `follow` and `let_go`, with `select` `select` and `set_selectable`, with `copy` `copy`, `copying`, `cancel_copy`, and `set_copy_options`, and with `sort` `set_sort` and `set_sort_icons`; with `live`, its and `set_rows`' reads are planned once at the end of the update that made them, `load_after_update`; `close` runs once, and a closed table's `set_rows` leaves the cache as it was) and readers; with `live`, a re-read whose load is ready when sent lands in that turn (`answered`), not on a spawned task; `TallTableEvent`, `TallTableStats`, `Paging` (1024-row pages, prefetch 2, 64 MiB, 3 in flight, a 250 ms scroll settle, an 80 ms drag settle), `ActivityOptions`; the keys (`init`, `actions!` under `seiza_tall_table`).
- **`src/state/follow.rs`** (`follow`): following, a child of `state`: the flag (`follow`, `let_go` (the host's: off with the view where it is, nothing read, moved, or re-planned), `is_following`; `reader_moved` sets or clears it by each vertical move's `Reach`, before the move, from the top it lands on: down keeps it or reaches the end, a landing (End, a jump, a drag's release) is at the end or not, up and Home let go; a vertical drag holds it, and a wheel more sideways than vertical is sideways; following starting forgets the tail drawn), the tail pin (`pin_to_tail`, after `set_rows`, a page landing (`pin_and_want`, which re-plans), a resize that keeps the bottom, and a drag's release: the view moved down to `PageCache::loaded_through` from its top once every row in view is loaded, never up, not a scroll), the visible pages bottom first (`tail_first`, in `wanted`), and `TailDrawn` (`note_tail_drawn`, in render, from a filled frame's end). Its `Control` (tests only) is the paging fixture's `follow` negative controls (`docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Features").
- **`src/state/resize.rs`** (`resize`): resizing, a child of `state`: the bands (`render_bands`, over the header's cells: each visible resizable column's right edge, 4 px on each side of a 1 px hairline, `table_row_border`, `border` hovered or dragged, the column-resize cursor), the drag (`Resizing`, the state's `resizing`, never a `Drag`: `press_band`, `resize_to` (width at the press + the pointer's move, clamped; back at the press's x, the set width it had; a kind's width outside the min or max held until the pointer brings it inside), `end_resize`; followed by the body's `canvas` handlers, the cursor held window-wide there), the sideways scroll held during it (`held_content`, read by the state's `content_width`: the content counts as reaching at least the view's right edge, so every clamp holds the scroll and the thumb stays at the view; `scroll_by` drops sideways input; a move re-lays out without bumping the layout generation; a release that changed the width runs `lay_out`, which clamps and reports the layout once, and one that didn't redoes nothing), the double-click fit (`fit_column`: the header's name shaped, a numeric column's exact text by character count at the number advance rounded up to a whole pixel, any other's shown text (and the name) cut to `max_width / 2` characters before it's copied, one-lined, and shaped with the state's `text_width`, which the tooltip's cut test shares, over the visible loaded rows), `column_width`, and `ColumnWidthsChanged` at a release or a fit that changed a set width (`docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Features").
- **`src/state/select.rs`** (`select`): selecting, a child of `state`, its state one field (`SelectState`: the selection, the kinds on, a drag, whether the last jump selected, focus at the last render): every change but a drag's own through `replace_selection`, which ends a drag selecting; `select` clamps a host's cells past the end and does nothing for a kind that's off (a debug assertion); the presses (`press_select`, from the state's root on a left press: `target_at` says a cell (the hover's `cell_at`), a row's number, a header, or the corner; Ctrl and a double-click's second press select nothing), the drag (`selecting`, never a `Drag`: it moves only the head's coordinates the press picked and the range spans, `cell_clamped` keeping the pointer to the visible cells; followed by the body's `canvas` handlers, with a window-wide wheel that scrolls the body through `on_scroll_wheel` and moves the head), the keys (`init`, its actions under `seiza_tall_table`, bound in `TallTableSelect` after the base's; `context` and `on_actions`, which the element calls; `key_step`: a plain move collapses from the anchor, Shift moves the head along the range's direction, the ends stop, `scroll_into_view` the least distance through `scroll_by`, an extended head only along the range's direction), `jump_select` and `drawn_mark` (a jump that selected draws no mark), `cut_selection` (`set_rows`), the drawing (`selected_lines` for the gutter's and the header's tint, `render_selection`: the tint and the active cell's edge as quads, one `canvas` a region (the pinned one's only with `pin`, the tint split at the run's edge into a piece a region, each edged), each region's child after `custom-cells`' and before the text), and `Selected`. Its `Control` (tests only) is the selection fixture's negative controls (`docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Features").
- **`src/state/copy.rs`** (`copy`, which turns on `select`): copying, a child of `state`, its state one field (`CopyState`: the host's `CopyOptions`, the last id, the copy running). Cmd+C and Ctrl+C (`CopySelection`, bound by `copy::init` in `TallTableSelect`, after the selection's; with nothing selected the key propagates; past `key_max_cells` cells, `Declined` and nothing read) and `copy` start it: `Started` emitted in the task that asked, then refused at once (`Failed(TooLarge)`) if its separators alone pass `max_bytes` (`least_bytes`). The turns (`copy_turn`): up to `TURN_CELLS` cells from the turn's page (one read for the copy and landed, or the cache's, `PageCache::holding`, as drawn), one `Text` a turn, `Copied` at the last row, `Failed(TooLarge)` when the text passes the cap or the first page's bytes a row project past twice it; the first turn runs in `copy`, the rest spawned (`copy_wake`, also called by every plan, so a page the cache loads for the reader wakes a copy waiting on it). The reads (`copy_wants`: the pages from the turn's, `background_limit` of them, it hasn't in hand, isn't reading, and the cache neither holds nor is reading for a wanted page (`PageCache::loading`: the copy waits for that read, even one short of rows that grew since, then takes the page or reads it); `start_copy_reads` sends what `plan` granted (`take_copy_grant`), the state's `load_wanted` splitting the plan's fetch list at `copy_at` and sending the cache's reads through `fetch_page`, so the copy's go before prefetch's; `copy_read_done`: every answer frees its place in the cache, whatever became of the copy), never cached; planned as a landing page's are, once the update ends (`copy_load`, a `defer`: after the events it emitted reach their subscribers, so a host that can't write the copy cancels it at `Started` before a read is sent; with `live`, not while a scrollbar drag moves, once it rests; every turn and every end plans, so a place a copy frees is used), and a page landing during a drag wakes a waiting copy too. The cap is checked a row at a time, its early projection from a page's worth of rows; a copy keeps the cap it started with. A page the turn passed (taken from the cache while the copy's own read was out) is let go: its read cancelled, its landed page dropped (`drop_passed`); a copy that ends cancels its reads still out. `copy` and the keys start nothing once the table is closed (`copy_ranges`). The cancels (`end_copy`, `drop_copy`: its reads cancelled through the delegate, draining as the cache's do): a second copy, `set_columns`, `set_rows` shrinking under its last row (`cut_copy`), Escape (`escape_copy`, before `select`'s, the selection kept), `cancel_copy`, a read that fails (`Failed(Read)`, and `ReadFailed` with `read_failure`), and `close` (no event). Its tests (`copy/tests.rs`) drive the element in a window over a source whose reads answer at once or when a test says (`docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Features"). Its `Control` (tests only) is the random test's `copy` controls and `copy.json`'s, the latter through `Writer` (how a turn writes a value, counts bytes, ends the text, and the key declines; outside the tests a stand-in at the file's end, each method the rule itself, inlined, so the build's code is as without it).
- **`src/state/sort.rs`** (`sort`): sorting, a child of `state`, its state one field (`SortState`: the sort, the host's icons if all three loaded, the arrow pressed and hovered). `set_sort` refuses on a closed table, and a column past the end or not sortable itself (no event, the source not told; a debug assertion outside the tests), then asks the source (`TallTableDelegate::sort`), and on `Ok` `apply_sort`: a copy cancelled, `PageCache::forget_all` (every page forgotten, its reads cancelled through the delegate, draining as any cancelled read; skipped on a closed table), flashes cleared, hover and the jump's mark cleared, `sort_selection` (`select`: a `Rows` or `Cells` selection cleared, a `Columns` one kept with its rows at 0), the view at the top (direction still, the scroll settle dropped; the sideways scroll kept), `sort_lets_go` (`follow`: following let go unless every row fits, the tail drawn forgotten), `Sorted`, and the reads planned once the update ends (`sort_load`, a `defer` through `load_or_settle`). The arrows (`render_arrows`, a layer over the header's cells under `resize`'s bands): each sortable column wide enough for its padding, the gap, and the box (`arrow_room`; the header cell's right padding takes the room, so its name ends at the gap), the glyph (`ARROWS`) or the host's SVG in one fixed box (`box_width`: the widest glyph's advance, read with the font in `Glyphs`, at least the icon size, which with `density` scales with the text, as the icon drawn does), dim unless sorted or hovered (hover kept in the state, so a frame knows it); its target from 4 px left of the box to `resize`'s band, a press there stopping `select`'s and a release on it running the cycle (`click_sort`, `sort::next`; a refusal emits `SortRefused`), a release off it forgetting the press. `set_sort_icons` loads each path once through the asset source (in the tests, `SortState::assets`), the glyphs for all three if any fails. `set_columns` keeps the sort by the sorted column's name (`sorted_name`, `keep_sort`: cleared whatever the source answers, untold on a closed table) and forgets the arrow pressed and hovered, which `render_arrows` also forgets once their arrow isn't drawn. `room` is what `layout_columns` and `resize`'s fit add to a sortable name; `arrow_box` is each arrow's box, where `render_arrows` draws it and what the layout probe reports (`ColumnProbe::arrow`). Its tests-only `drawn` (each arrow, its color, its target) and `Control` (the cycle fixture's `descending-first`) are `fixtures/sort.rs`'s (`docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Features").
- **`src/state/pin.rs`** (`pin`): pinning, a child of `state`, its state one field (`PinState`: the host's count, the run in effect). `set_pinned` keeps the count (kept across `set_columns`); `repin` computes the run in effect (`pin::in_effect`) wherever the columns or the view's width change (`set_pinned`, `set_body`, every `lay_out`, which calls it, and `resize`'s release, which may change no width), held through a `resize` drag, and on a change clears the hover, bumps the layout generation, and emits `Pinned`; `place` is the run as drawn (`Pin`, its edge from the columns' widths, a drag's too, clamped to the cells' width). The drawing: `split_layer` and `regions` (a layer over the columns split at the run's edge into two clipped regions, the pinned one only while a column is pinned, each child placed by `Pin::layer_left`): the header's cells, `sort`'s arrows (a scrolled column's not drawn while its target is under the run), `resize`'s scrolled bands (a pinned column's band is clipped to the cells only, over them, so the last one's half past the edge draws and takes the press), and the body's cells, each region with its own `custom-cells` canvas (`pinned_quads`) and `select` canvas, over the one layer of row backgrounds; a failed row's message across both, in the cells layer; `edge_line`, the 1 px `border` line at the edge through the header (over the bands) and the body. Its `Control` (tests only) is `pin.json`'s negative controls and the window test's unclipped paint; `drawn` and `line` (tests only) are what the body drew (`docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Features").

- **`src/column.rs`**: `TallTableColumn` (with `custom-cells`, its `fill`, `bar`, and `text_ramp`; with `live` too, its `flash`; with `resize` or `density`, its `width`, its set width kept on it; with `resize`, its `min_width` (`None` for the table's least, `Sizes::least`), `max_width`, and `resizable`; with `sort`, its `sortable`, off unless set; with `format`, its `format`, kept on the laid-out column for a numeric kind, `Glyphs::lay_out`) and `ColumnKind`, and the layout (`layout_columns`, at a `Sizes`): widths by kind, at least the header's width (with `sort`, a sortable header's arrow and gap added to its name's estimate), an integer column fitting its widest value at the font's widest number advance (`Glyphs`, read once per font and size, `digits_width`); with `resize` or `density`, a set width in place of all that, clamped (a kind's width never is; with `density` alone, to the table's least and 1200 px). - **`src/pages.rs`**: `PageCache`, pure bookkeeping (tested natively): which pages are loaded, in flight, draining (cancelled but still answering, counted against `max_in_flight`), failed; what to fetch (visible pages first: no prefetch starts while a visible page is loading), cancel, and evict (LRU past the budget); a shrink forgets every page wholly past the new end (`set_rows` returns their reads to cancel). With `live`, a loaded page's re-read state (`Live`: stale, its read in flight with the generation it started at, a failed re-read, a landed one not yet drawn, the end of the rows the drawn page and the read in flight hold), a short last page that `set_rows` grows kept stale (`row` answers `Pending` past the rows it holds), each invalidation's generation, and `take_refreshed` for render; with `custom-cells` too, each page's rows invalidated since its drawn read started (`RowSet`s: before the re-read in flight, and during it), while a column flashes (`track_changes`, `changes`); the order re-reads go in and their `max_in_flight − 1` limit are `plan`'s (`docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Features"). With `copy`, a copy's reads in flight by page and request (never cached), counted against `max_in_flight` and, with `live`'s re-reads, against `background_limit` (`(max_in_flight − 1).max(1)`); `plan` grants the pages the copy asked for (`set_copy_wants`) after the visible pages and their re-reads, none while a visible page has nothing to show, before prefetch (`Plan::copy`, `copy_at`), and prefetch (a first read or a re-read) skips a page the copy reads or was just granted; a cancelled one drains as the cache's do (`copy_cancelled`), and any answer frees its place (`copy_answered`); `holding` is a loaded page with the rows a copy wants (with `live`, as drawn), `loading` a read of it the cache has out (short of the copy's rows or not: the copy waits for it). With `live`, a visible grown page whose re-read failed doesn't hold prefetch or a copy back, as a failed page doesn't. With `follow`, `loaded_through`: where the run of rows drawn with their values from a row on ends. With `sort`, `forget_all`: every page forgotten as a shrink forgets the pages past its end, its reads returned in page order to cancel. ## GPUI Kit gotchas (check before debugging)

- **`use gpui_kit::*` brings gpui-kit's `test` attribute**, which shadows `#[test]` ("recursion limit reached while expanding `#[test]`"). A test module imports what it needs by name, not `super::*`.
- **A `fn` pointer compared with `==` warns** (`unpredictable_function_pointer_comparisons`), in a derived `PartialEq` too: `Bar` compares its `Stop` with `std::ptr::fn_addr_eq`. A `Vec` of them (`Ramp`) doesn't warn.
- **A key context only counts on the focused element's path.** Key dispatch walks from the focused element up, so a context on a child of the element that tracks focus never matches: `select`'s context is the element's own (`TallTable TallTableSelect`). Bindings matching at the same depth go latest-bound first, so `select::init` runs after the base's bindings and its keys win while a kind is on.
- **A scrollbar release can arrive before the next paint.** The drag's mouse-move and mouse-up handlers are registered every frame in the body's `canvas`, not only once a drag starts: a quick click on the track missed its release in WebKit, and the next pointer move scrolled the table.

## Perf script gotchas

- **A page's own rAF loop slows `live`'s latency:** with `frameRecorder` installed, invalidate → refreshed frame read ~20 ms where it reads ~7 without, so `live.ts` measures latency first with nothing on the page, then installs the recorder and traces frame work in a second window.
- **GPUI delivers a frame's events after its draw and present,** in the same task: a script reading the probe at a frame's GPU calls reads the frame before's. `shared-web/perf/`'s `presentedRecorder` reads `__seizaFrameSample` in the presenting task's microtask checkpoint instead; it wraps the GPU calls, so `follow.ts` installs it only for the window that reads it and stops it before the timed and traced ones. And `follow`'s tail pin can move the view after the last frame and before an input, so a wheel up's frame can sit below the frame before it: `follow.ts` finds the wheel's frame by the following flag, not by its top.
- **A scripted drag moves the pointer every 8 ms, not once a frame,** as the sideways fling sends its wheels: under the throttled profile, each move's delivery waits on a frame that costs more than 16 ms, so moves sent a frame apart left half the traced frames empty and the trace's p50 read their idle time (`docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Features").
- **A drag-select moves a row a move:** the table redraws only when the selection changes, so moves that stay in one cell draw nothing, and the trace's p50 reads those idle frames (`docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Features"). And between `perf`'s segments the select-all is cleared before Home and pressed again after (not before the drags, which run with nothing selected): Home moves the active cell, which the select-all left at row 0, so it scrolls nothing.
- **A move to the page's corner never reaches the canvas:** gpui-pre-web's key textarea sits at (1, 1), over it, so a script moving the pointer "away" there leaves GPUI's hover where it was (an arrow stayed hovered). Move it into the body instead.
- **wasm-bindgen answers a closure's `None` as `undefined`,** not `null`: a hook that means `null` returns a `JsValue` (`__tallTableExact` does), or a script's NULL tests never match.
- **Firefox's screenshot can show the frame before the one the probe reports** (~100 ms behind, seen after Escape): the smoke check's tint samples poll up to 1 s for what they expect (`settled`), so a redraw that never came still fails.
- **The wheel is the body's:** over the header, where a resize drag holds the pointer (its band follows the edge), a wheel scrolls nothing, with or without `resize`. A script that wheels during a drag moves the pointer straight down into the body first (the width follows the pointer's x alone).
- **A copy that outlasts a scroll doesn't belong in the scroll's window:** `copy.ts` ends the traced window at the scroll's rest and reads the copy's end afterwards from its written mark; a window that waited for the copy read its tail's idle frames as the scroll's.
- **A copy's scripts read the clipboard through a real paste** (`harness.ts`'s `paste`: a textarea over the page, Cmd+V, the canvas focused again), never `navigator.clipboard.readText`, which Firefox and WebKit don't grant a script. Chromium's context is granted `clipboard-read` and `clipboard-write` (headless refuses every async write without it); WebKit on macOS writes the system pasteboard, so a run overwrites what was copied there. With reads held (`?delay`), prefetch's two reads go out only once the visible page lands, so a copy started at the filled mark has one place for a round: the smoke check waits out the prefetch before timing a held copy.
- **Playwright resolves a wheel before the page handles it:** at a throttled dense table's ~90 ms frames the view kept scrolling through queued wheels for seconds after the last one resolved, so a filled frame mid-scroll passed a rest's test and a Home pressed then was undone. `perf.ts`'s rests and the smoke check's density run wait for the view filled and no frame presented for 300 ms (`harness.ts`'s `restedWhere`, with `workRecorder` installed), and `perf.ts` ends each reading 300 ms after the last presented frame, so the quiet spell isn't counted.
- **The host's scripts serve its release build themselves** (`perf/harness.ts`: `vite preview` of `host/web/dist` on port 4350, through `shared-web/perf/`'s `serveApp`). Per-frame work comes from Chromium's trace (`shared-web/perf/trace.ts`'s `frameWork`), each segment traced on its own (a trace of every segment at once runs to hundreds of MB); `workRecorder` cross-checks it. Late frames come from `frameRecorder`'s rAFs: Chromium's compositor frame reporter doesn't see GPUI's canvas frames.

**The fixtures** (`fixtures/README.md`) pin what a DOM contender must match. A change that alters the table's page requests, its cell text, or its page SQL fails them: update the fixture in the same change (and so every DOM contender's tests). `cells.json`'s drawn cells come from the observatory's GPUI contender (`just observatory cell-fixture`; `--check` compares); its `rounding` cases, `paging.json`, and `sql.json` (whose test is the DuckDB engine's, where the SQL is written) are edited by hand. `paint.json`'s cases and stops are by hand and its expectations come from Chromium (`just tall-table paint-fixture`; `--check` compares). `selection.json`, `sort.json`, `pin.json`, `density.json`, and `copy.json`'s cases are by hand (`density.json`'s `number_px` from the bare host in Chromium: `just tall-table density-fixture`; `--check` compares); `sort.json`'s orders are tested in the bare host (`host/crate/src/rows.rs`), whose in-memory order the scripts sort. `just observatory fixtures` runs the first three and `copy.json`'s native test (the TanStack contender's test runs `selection.json` and `cells.json`'s `resize` against its wide level's ports and names `copy.json` skipped).

The view runs in Tycho's checks (`just tycho table`, `jump`, `cells`, `errors`, and the suite's table and jump parts) and in the bare host's (`just tall-table smoke`, `perf`).
