- **Pinning costs about nothing a frame.** In the wide shape, one column pinned reads +0.03–0.06 ms (≤ 0.9%) at the reference run. The ranges overlap the base's or touch it (the scroll's 7.03 against 7.03). The re-run after the base reads the same to 0.04 ms. That's the second region: a second clipped layer for the header and the cells, and the edge line. The narrow shape, two columns pinned, reads at or under the base (5.98 and 6.01 against 6.05): it scrolls only vertically, so its scrolled region starts where its pinned one ends and draws the same cells. The 0.04–0.07 ms under the base is no saving; it's within what a session's place moves.
- **Built with nothing pinned, it's the base:** every `--pin 0` segment within 0.04 ms of the base in both profiles, the ranges overlapping. The code paid for in every frame (each site through the mappings, the run in effect read) is too small to see. Whatever pinning costs is in the drawing.
- **Throttled, within the spread:** the pinned segments within −1.3% to +1.7% of the base, the ranges overlapping in every one.
- **The wide throttled fling's late frames:** base 2.99% (median; 1.49–6.77), pinned 1.96% (0.48–4.52), `--pin 0` 2.49% (0.49–8.21). The open question's gap (`PLAN.md`) didn't show in this build. Every other segment's late frames match the base's (the throttled scrolls' 32–59% are the base's too).
- **Visible cells:** the same count pinned and not (narrow 280, wide 308, the sideways fling 336): the pinned columns are the ones that were there, and the scrolled ones are drawn right of them.
