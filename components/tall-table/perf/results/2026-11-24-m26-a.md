## What changed

- **The table:** `TallTableState::select_in_view(selection, cx)`, public behind `select`: `set_selection`'s setting, then the selection's head scrolled into view the least distance through the keys' own path, along what its kind spans; again for the selection in effect, never for one cleared or refused; with `popover`, a popover the active cell left closed.
- **Tycho builds it:** its Row tab's Previous row and Next row (`apps/tycho/crate/src/workbench.rs`, `select_cell`).

## Experiments and controls

- **The popover's close fails its test without it:** with the close removed, `select_in_view_closes_it_off_its_cell` failed at "the row after"; restored, it passes.
- **The guard for a kind that's off has no test:** `set_selection` debug-asserts on that kind first, so a test build never reaches it; its reason is written beside it.
