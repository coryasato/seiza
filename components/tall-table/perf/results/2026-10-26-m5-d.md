## Frame work: `resize` built against the base (D1)

The bare host's `resize` build makes every column resizable, so its header draws a band at each visible column's edge every frame; nobody drags in these segments. Work p50 (p95), ms, from Chromium's trace; spread is (max − min) / median of the ten runs' p50s:

| Shape, segment | Reference: base | Reference: `resize` | Re-run, after the base | Throttled: base | Throttled: `resize` |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| narrow scroll | 6.05 (7.51) | 6.12 (7.46) | 6.13 (7.51) | 21.05 (30.19) | 21.59 (30.70) |
| narrow fling | 3.96 (8.19) | 4.02 (8.25) | 4.00 (8.21) | 12.55 (26.87) | 12.12 (26.01) |
| wide scroll | 6.98 (8.48) | 7.13 (8.66) | 7.11 (8.61) | 25.81 (37.31) | 26.09 (38.06) |
| wide fling | 4.05 (8.71) | 4.17 (8.80) | 4.14 (8.65) | 12.34 (15.48) | 12.98 (28.90) |
| wide sideways | 6.50 (7.28) | 6.63 (7.40) | 6.63 (7.40) | 23.62 (28.65) | 24.26 (28.82) |

- **Reference: +0.05–0.15 ms a frame (+1–2%), past the spread.** The base's spreads are 0.7–3.0%, and in the re-run (the `resize` build after the base, so not the order) the ranges of the ten runs don't overlap the base's in the scroll and the sideways fling: narrow scroll 6.03–6.07 against 6.11–6.18, wide scroll 6.94–7.02 against 7.08–7.21, wide sideways 6.49–6.58 against 6.59–6.68. What it is: the bands, two styled divs per visible column (11–12 here) with a hitbox, a group hover, and a listener each, laid out every frame. It's the feature's own cost while its columns are resizable, not compiled-in code idling; a host with `resizable(false)` on every column draws no band (not measured).
- **Throttled: within the spread** in every segment's p50 (spreads 1.9–12.1%); busy time within 3% but for the narrow fling (8% lower with `resize`).
- **The wide throttled fling's late frames:** base 1.98% (median; runs 1.5–8.4), `resize` 5.09% (1.0–12.0). The runs' p95 splits between ~15 ms and ~30 ms in both builds, and `resize`'s land in the slower mode more often (7 of 10, the base's 3). It's `follow`'s open question from M4 part D, now in a second feature's build whose only work on that path is the bands' ~0.1 ms a frame. `PLAN.md`'s open question is widened to say so.

## The resize drag (D1, D2)

The first float column's edge out 200 px and back, twice, a move every 8 ms (about 2 s). Every move re-lays out the columns.

| Shape | Profile | Work p50 | p95 | Recorder p50 | Late | Busy | Beside: sideways fling p50 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| narrow | reference | 4.82 ms | 5.41 | 4.50 | 0% | 747 ms | — |
| wide | reference | 5.54 | 6.14 | 5.23 | 0% | 838 | 6.63 |
| narrow | throttled | 15.54 | 18.09 | 14.89 | 5.47% | 1,905 | — |
| wide | throttled | (17.36, bimodal) | 21.02 | 17.89 | 4.05% | 1,668 | 24.26 |

Re-run at reference: narrow 4.82 (p95 5.42), wide 5.50 (6.07).

- **D2, no:** in the wide shape at reference, the drag's work per frame is 5.54 ms p50 (spread 1.1%), *under* the sideways fling's 6.63: re-laying out 500 columns a move costs less than drawing a new set of columns does. No part for the drag. Logged in `docs/DECISIONS.md`.
- **No late frames at reference;** throttled, 4–5%: a drag frame costs ~15–18 ms at 4× CPU, at the edge of a 16.7 ms frame.
- **The wide throttled drag's trace p50 is bimodal:** 6 runs read 17.1–18.7 ms, 4 read 0.8–1.1 (spread 103%). A drag frame there costs ~18 ms, just over a frame, so Chromium draws about every other one: 69–104 of the ~150 frames the trace begins draw, and when fewer than half do, the p50 of every traced frame is an idle one. `workRecorder`, which counts only frames that drew, reads 17.7–18.2 in all ten (spread 2.4%), and is the number given for it. Part C's pacing (a move every 8 ms) fixed the case where moves arrived too slowly; this is the drawing rate itself. The wide throttled sideways fling (~24 ms a frame, past a frame by more) doesn't hit it. Logged in `docs/DECISIONS.md`.
