## Frame work: a select-all standing, against the base (D1)

- **No cost measured, in either order.** Every segment is within the spread (reference 1.2–7.0%, throttled 1.6–6.9%), and the scroll and sideways fling read up to 1.5% *lower* with the select-all standing, in both orders (narrow scroll: base 5.99–6.06, `select` 5.95–6.02 and 5.92–6.02 in the re-run). A standing selection is two elements (the tint and the active cell's edge) and a range test per visible row and column a frame, however many rows it spans; unlike `resize`'s bands (+1–2%), nothing is drawn per visible column. Busy time within 2%.
- **The wide throttled fling's late frames:** base 2.75% (median; runs 0.5–8.4), `select` 4.09% (1.5–6.8), ranges overlapping. `PLAN.md`'s open question, in a third feature's build, closer to the base than `follow`'s or `resize`'s.

## The drag-select (D1)

From a cell near the top left to the last fully visible row and column and back, a row a move (the column along the diagonal), a move every 8 ms, about 2 s: every move changes the selection, so every frame redraws the block.

| Shape | Profile | Work p50 | p95 | Recorder p50 | Late | Busy | Beside: scroll, sideways fling p50 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| narrow | reference | 4.99 ms | 5.17 | 4.67 | 0% | 762 ms | 5.96 |
| wide | reference | 5.65 | 5.94 | 5.36 | 0% | 846 | 6.95, 6.46 |
| narrow | throttled | 16.44 | 17.59 | 15.66 | 8.39% (4.0–17.6) | 1,872 | 20.73 |
| wide | throttled | (18.66, bimodal) | 20.09 | 18.25 | 5.04% (0–14.6) | 1,773 | 25.46, 23.71 |

Re-run at reference: narrow 4.97 (p95 5.18), wide 5.60 (5.83).

- **Under the scroll and the sideways fling** in every shape and profile: a block redrawn at a new size each frame costs less than drawing a new set of rows or columns. It reads as `resize`'s drag does (4.82 and 5.54 ms at reference, 15.54 and ~17.7 throttled).
- **No late frames at reference;** throttled, 5–8%: a drag frame costs ~16–18 ms at 4× CPU, at the edge of a 16.7 ms frame.
- **The wide throttled drag's trace p50 is bimodal,** as `resize`'s drag was: 6 runs read 18.6–19.3 ms, 4 read 0.80–0.94 (spread 99%). A frame costs just over one, so Chromium draws about every other one, and when fewer than half of the traced frames draw, their p50 is an idle one. `workRecorder`, which counts only frames that drew, reads 18.0–18.4 in all ten (spread 2.2%), and is the number given for it (`docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Features", M5 part D).
