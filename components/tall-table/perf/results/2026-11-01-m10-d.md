Measured on Apple M1, Darwin 25.6.0 arm64, machine `local`, Chromium headless=new, 1440×900 at DPR 2, cold, median of 10, reference and throttled (CPU 4×, Fast 4G) (root CLAUDE.md, "Measurement protocol"), on the final build (`1744bfc`, no change to the table in this part). The machine was quiet (browsers closed): load average 6.1 at the start of the base's run (just after the size run's builds), 1.5–2.9 for the rest.

Raw: `2026-11-01-m10-d-size.json` (`just tall-table size`); `2026-11-01-m10-d-frames-base.json` (the base), `2026-11-01-m10-d-frames-control.json` (the `density` build at `--density 30,14`: the feature built at the base's numbers, the kind widths kept), `2026-11-01-m10-d-frames-dense.json` (the host's default, 18,11,56), `2026-11-01-m10-d-frames-wide-26-13-96.json`, `-22-12-80.json`, and `-20-11-68.json` (the wide shape at three settings between), in that order; `2026-11-01-m10-d-rerun-frames-control-reference.json` (the control's reference run again, after the rest, for the order).

## A denser table's cost: the cells on screen (D1)

The wide shape, reference, median of 10. Cells is the visible cells' p50; work is the recorder's (each frame that drew), p50 and p95; presented is frames a second and their share of rAFs (the base presents on 98–99% while keeping up).

| Setting (row, text, width) | Cells | Scroll: work p50 / p95 | Scroll: presented | Fling: work p50 / p95 | Fling: presented | Sideways: cells | Sideways: work p50 / p95 | Sideways: presented |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 30,14,120 (the control) | 308 | 6.77 / 8.23 | 58.7/s (98%) | 7.58 / 8.45 | 59.5/s (99%) | 336 | 6.44 / 7.23 | 59.2/s (98%) |
| 26,13,96 | 462 | 8.93 / 10.34 | 58.1/s (97%) | 10.00 / 11.06 | 59.2/s (98%) | 480 | 8.14 / 9.17 | 59.0/s (98%) |
| 22,12,80 | 663 | 11.77 / 16.86 | 54.5/s (91%) | 13.45 / 14.92 | 47.7/s (79%) | 646 | 10.90 / 11.81 | 59.5/s (99%) |
| 20,11,68 | 840 | 17.99 / 20.06 | 30.3/s (50%) | 16.27 / 18.09 | 40.7/s (68%) | 840 | 12.86 / 13.82 | 59.1/s (99%) |
| 18,11,56 (the host's default) | 1,128 | 20.96 / 22.69 | 30.1/s (50%) | 17.95 / 21.24 | 39.6/s (66%) | 1,128 | 15.64 / 16.95 | 58.7/s (99%) |

The ranges: at 663 cells the scroll's presented frames ran 48.2–56.4 a second and the fling's 43.6–58.8 (the edge, where a run holds or doesn't); at 840 the scroll's 30.1–47.2 (one run held most of its frames). Every other setting's range is within 2 a second of its median.

- **Work grows ~14 µs a cell** while the table keeps up: the scroll's p50 6.77 → 8.93 → 11.77 ms over 308 → 462 → 663 cells, a straight line within 0.1 ms. Past it, a frame draws two steps of input, so the per-frame number grows faster (17.99 at 840) and isn't a cost per cell.
- **The reference run holds the display's rate to ~460 cells in every segment.** At ~660 the vertical scroll and fling start dropping frames (91% and 79% of rAFs), once the frames' p95 reaches the 16.7 ms frame (16.86 and 14.92); by 840 the scroll presents every other rAF, 30 a second, and stays there at 1,128. The late share reads 0–0.65% at every setting (the spike's lesson: it reads rAF intervals).
- **The sideways fling holds 59 a second to 1,128 cells,** its p95 under the frame until 1,128 (16.95, presenting on 99% of rAFs), and its work a frame below the vertical scroll's at the same cells (15.64 against 20.96 ms). Why wasn't measured: a vertical step brings in whole rows of new cells, a sideways one a few columns, which would fit.
- **The narrow shape at the default** (517 cells): scroll 9.38 ms p50 at 58.0/s (97%), fling 58.8/s (98%): it keeps up, as the wide shape's 462 does.

So the curve the observatory's dial reads, at this machine's reference run: the rate holds to ~460 cells, falls off between ~660 and ~840 vertically, and halves past; the base's ~310 is well inside it.

**Throttled (4× CPU):** the base already falls behind (32 frames a second in the wide scroll, 63% late), and a denser setting presents fewer in proportion to its work: the wide scroll 32.0/s at 308 cells, 22.2 at 462, 16.4 at 663, 13.9 at 840, 11.9 at 1,128 (work p50 27.97, 42.78, 58.67, 67.64, 80.04 ms); the sideways fling 38.9, 30.5, 22.9, 18.6, 14.9. Every throttled segment reached its rest at every setting. The flings' trace p50 under 1.1 ms where the table presented on half the rAFs is the empty rAFs, as the spike found; the recorder's is the frame's work.

## Reproducing it

```sh
just tall-table size --label m10-d-size
just tall-table build && just tall-table perf --label m10-d-frames-base
just tall-table build density && just tall-table perf --density 30,14 --label m10-d-frames-control
just tall-table perf --label m10-d-frames-dense
just tall-table perf --shape wide --density 26,13,96 --label m10-d-frames-wide-26-13-96
just tall-table perf --shape wide --density 22,12,80 --label m10-d-frames-wide-22-12-80
just tall-table perf --shape wide --density 20,11,68 --label m10-d-frames-wide-20-11-68
just tall-table perf --density 30,14 --profile reference --label m10-d-rerun-frames-control-reference
just tall-table build density,select,resize,sort,pin && just tall-table smoke
```
