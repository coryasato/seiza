# M7 part C: `copy`'s bridge and scripts (2026-10-27)

Measured on Apple M1, Darwin 25.6.0 arm64, machine `local`, Playwright 1.63 (Chromium headless=new, Firefox, WebKit), on the bare host's release build with `copy` (`just tall-table build copy`). The smoke check is pass or fail; `copy.ts` ran once per shape and profile to show it measures (one run, not the protocol's median of 10: that's part D's, on the final build).

Raw: `2026-10-27-m7-c-copy-trial.json` (`just tall-table copy --runs 1`).

## What changed

- The bridge's writer, `clipboard::Clipboard` (`copy`): a `ClipboardItem` opened at `Started`, the pieces joined in a Blob at `Copied`, rejected at `Failed` or the host's word that the table closed; `writeText` without `ClipboardItem`; the copy handed back to cancel without `navigator.clipboard`, or when the browser refuses while it runs. The outcome on `__tallTableCopy` in the bare host.
- The table plans a copy's reads once the update that started it ends (`copy_load`), so a host cancelling at `Started` sends no read. Found by the smoke check's insecure run, which read one page before the cancel (`a_host_cancelling_at_started_sends_no_read`, which fails with the plan back in the update).
- The bare host: Cmd+Shift+C (the host's `copy`), `?copy-max-bytes=` and `?copy-key-cells=`, the source's read counts with `copy` too, and `?perf` hooks (exact text, heap, `select`, `close`). `serveApp`'s `lan` option (`shared-web/perf/serve.ts`) serves the build at the LAN address too.
- Sizes: the `copy` build's wasm 2704.4 KiB brotli in the build's own asset report; the cost against the base and `select` is D1's.

## Experiments and controls

- `a_host_cancelling_at_started_sends_no_read` fails with `copy_load` planning in the update (its first assertion, a `load 3`), and passes with the plan deferred.
- Two first runs of the held copies took 8.0 s and 80.0 s, not 6 and 60: prefetch's two reads go out only once the visible page lands, so a copy started at the filled mark had one place for its first round. The smoke check now waits out the prefetch.
- The first trial's window for the scroll during a copy waited for the copy's end, so it read the copy's tail as the scroll's: a rest of 5.4 s against 2.2 throttled, and per-frame p50 0.6 ms from the idle frames after the scroll settled (the code review). Ended at the scroll's rest, it reads 2.24 against 2.21 s. The table above is the corrected run.
- Pasting into the textarea takes GPUI's focus, and focusing the canvas doesn't give it back (gpui-pre-web's key target is another element): the paste helper puts the focus back where it was, and the select hook focuses the table.

## Reproducing it
