# M2 follow-up: `custom-cells`' follow-ups (2026-10-25)

Measured on Apple M1, Darwin 25.6.0 arm64, machine `local`, Chromium headless=new, 1440×900 at DPR 2, cold, median of 10, reference only (the spec's check: the wide shape at `?cells=heat`, against `custom-cells` as M2 measured it). Each run started at a 1-minute load average under 3 (2.66 for `text`, 1.92 for `heat`).

Raw: `2026-10-25-m2-follow-up-size.json` (`just tall-table size --against HEAD`, HEAD `ebdb552`), `2026-10-25-m2-follow-up-frames-{text,heat}.json` (`just tall-table perf --shape wide --profile reference --cells …`).

## What changed

- **The table** (`custom-cells`): `Bar::below(stop)`, a bar left of its zero in its own color, and `Bar` `#[non_exhaustive]`; text over a fill with no text ramp's color takes `foreground` or `background`, whichever's Oklab lightness is farther from the color on screen (a translucent fill over the row's background, the jumped-to row's tint over it; a NULL keeps its muted color); `Domain { lo, mid, hi }` and `unit(v)`; the probe's bar carries its color.
- **The bare host:** every float column's range is a `Domain`, its bars' zero `unit(0.0)`, its bars `below` in `chart_bearish`. The values drawn are the same as before.
- **Size:** `custom-cells` now adds 10.0 KiB after wasm-opt, 3.3 KiB brotli. The last size run before these changes (`2026-10-25-m3-d-review-size.json`, on gpui-kit 0.8.0) read 7.9 and 2.5, so the follow-ups add 2.1 KiB after wasm-opt and ~0.8 brotli (brotli moves ±0.5 between builds of the same code: `2026-10-24-m2.md`). With `live` too, the pair reads 34.2 KiB after wasm-opt (32.2 before) and 12.2 brotli (11.3).
- **The base is unchanged:** 9,680.5 KiB after wasm-opt both at `ebdb552` in a worktree and in the working tree (brotli 2,689.4 against 2,688.4); no `paint` or `flash` symbols in the base.

## Frame work

Work per frame is the trace's (ms, median of 10 runs' p50 / p95). Spread: (max − min) / median of the runs' p50s. 308 cells a frame (scroll, fling), 336 (sideways); at `heat` every float cell is filled, so every cell picks its text's contrast.

| M2, heat | 7.63 / 9.03 | 1.2% | 7.29 / 8.09 | 0.8% | 4.24 / 9.21 | 0% |

- **The contrast costs ~nothing measurable:** heat over text adds 0.65 ms in the scroll and 0.78 sideways; M2 read 0.63 and 0.79. The 0.02 ms difference is a quarter of one run-to-run spread (~0.09 ms at heat). An opaque fill's lightness is the `l` its mix already computed, so a heat cell's pick is two subtractions and a compare.
- **No late frames;** outside the frames, every build reads as before (the fling's page-making is the same ~2 s busy in both).
- Not measured: the throttled profile (the spec's check is the reference run), and `bars` (its new cost, a branch per bar, is smaller than the contrast's).

## Experiments and controls

- **The translucent fixture case first claimed more than it showed.** Its `why` said a fully translucent `table_active` fill over the dark row would pick dark text if read alone; checked in Chromium, it picks the same token either way (its own lightness 0.43, nearer the dark background than the light foreground). Only the half-translucent case flips (own lightness 0.74: dark text; on screen over the dark row: light). The note moved there, and a third control (`composite_ignored_fails`) holds it.
- **Negative controls:** `below` ignored misses exactly the 3 bars drawn in `below`; the nearer token picked misses all 10 text cases; the fill's own lightness read alone misses only `active` at 0.5 in the dark theme (an even row's and the marked row's).
- **The marked row's tint over the fill is checked only as part of the whole:** at its alpha (0.2), it flips only fills the script rejects as near ties (within 0.02), so no case tells it apart on its own.
- **The smoke check's first bar check was wrong:** it wanted both bar colors in both shapes, but the narrow shape's floats are all at or above zero. It now wants them in the wide shape only.
