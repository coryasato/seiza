## What changed

- **The table:** Cargo feature `find`: `TallTableDelegate::find`, `FindQuery` (with `cells`, `matches`, `new`), `FindLoad`, `FoundCell`, `FindOutcome`; `set_find`, `find_next`, `find_previous`, `find_term`, `current_match`; `TallTableEvent::FindStarted` and `Found`; the matches' tints and the current match's `ring` edge in the cells' text layer; `FindProbe` on the probe, `found` (`FoundProbe`) and `current` on each cell (`src/find.rs`, `src/state/find.rs`; `docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Features", 2026-11-18 and 2026-11-19). `select`'s sideways bring-into-view moved to the state (`column_into_view`), built with `select` or `find`.
- **The bridge:** each step's outcome on a global (`Publish::found`, `js::found`), the find on the probe, each cell's `found` and `current`.
- **The bare host:** `?find=<term>`; with `?perf`, `__tallTableFind(term)`, `__tallTableFindNext()`, `__tallTableFindPrevious()`, and `__tallTableFound`; its source finds over its rows in windows of 16,384 cells, at least a millisecond apart (`?delay` each, if longer).
- **The fixtures:** `find.json` (new): 11 match cases, 14 range cases, 14 steps over a grid, three controls (`case-sensitive`, `cut-ranges`, `no-wrap`), each failing it. No contender runs it.

## The random test's controls

31 controls, each run under `SEED`s 0–6 in the all-on build (48 cases a seed), `find` among its features, its `Find` steps placed last (up to 24 a case):

| Controls | Caught |
|---|---|
| `find`'s: `CaseSensitive` (invariant 24, the match rule's control, set for the thread) | 7 of 7 |
| `find`'s: `KeepFindOnSort` (22) | 3 of 7 (`SEED`s 0, 2, 4) |
| The 29 earlier controls, with the `Find` steps placed | 192 of 203 |

The earlier controls miss at the same `SEED`s as at M19's close ([`2026-11-18-m19-b.md`](2026-11-18-m19-b.md): `GapOutsideParent` at 1, 3, 5, 6; `IgnoreLoading` at 5, 6; `HoldingWithoutEnd` at 3, 4; `EndWithoutPlan` and `KeepOnReread` at 4; `HitIgnoresRun` at 6): the `Find` steps cost and give none. `KeepFindOnSort` needs a sort within a find's 20 ms on the test clock, which placed steps rarely make; `SEED` 0, which `just tall-table test` runs, catches it, and `a_sort_cancels_and_forgets` holds the rule in every run. The random test itself passes at every `SEED` 0–6.

**What it found:** `pairs`' `follow` + `find` build failed `SEED` 0 at invariant 4 (`Replace`, two `Find`s, `Wait(13)`: 4,864 bytes past a 1,216-byte budget after a landing, a page unwanted). Not the table's: a find landing inside the `Wait` moved the view between two of its page landings, and the check compares the cache with the pages wanted before and after the step; a jump to the same row in a step of its own passes. The check now skips a step a find landed in, as it skips following's pin (`docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Features", 2026-11-19). Swept again after it (`2026-11-19-m20-b-rerun.txt`): every control caught at the same `SEED`s, 202 of 217 both times, and the random test passing at every `SEED` 0–6.

## Experiments and controls

- **Every rule mutated:** 45 of `find`'s rules (the match's fold and its sigma, a range's bytes and their merge, a whole cell for a match the drawn text hides, the current match drawn whatever it holds, a step's gap each way, the wrap's `until` and its absence from the first row, the query's order; a step while one is out and on a closed table; hidden columns and the drawn order; the reader's selection, the landing's, a `Rows` start, a `Columns` range; an answer past the rows; a late answer's request; the landing in view, sideways, selected, following let go; each cancel's event and its call to the source; each setter's cancel and what it forgets; `close`'s deferred event; the colors, the edge, the ranges after the cut, the probe's), each alone in the all-on build, each failing at least one window test or fixture. One passed at first: a cancelled find's late answer taken by any find out, which every cancel test missed (none had a new step out when the old answer came); `a_late_answer_isnt_the_next_steps` now catches it.
- **The fixture's controls** (`case-sensitive`, `cut-ranges`, `no-wrap`) each fail `find.json`.
- **`pairs`** (`find` the 15th feature: 118 clippy builds, the pairs with code gated on both, 118 rustdoc builds): clippy and rustdoc passed; the tests failed one pair (`follow` + `find`, above), then passed whole after the check's fix (26 min).
- **The soak** (`just tall-table soak`, `find` among its features): 4 `SEED`s × 500 cases, passed, 99–104 s a `SEED`, on the final table code (the reruns changed only the tests).
- **The bare host in Chromium** (`m20-b-find-host.ts`, a `find` release build): in both shapes, with and without `?delay=20`, `?find=` sets the term before the first frame (narrow `a`: 71 drawn cells marked, 71 matching by the rule; wide `12`: 44 and 44), each probe range is the term in the shown text, `__tallTableFindNext` lands (row 0) and `__tallTableFindPrevious` wraps to the last row, the probe's current match follows, a step with `?delay` is pending (`started`, `out`), a term with no match ends `none` (narrow), a new term mid-scan ends the step `cancelled` (wide: 785 M cells, the whole scan minutes), a number is refused and `null` clears the term; no console problem. Its first run found the host's scan running whole in one task (below).

**The bare host's scan:** with no `?delay`, its windows waited `Duration::ZERO` between them, which GPUI's web timer answers without yielding: a term with no match ran the wide shape's 785 M cells in one task, 295 s with no frame drawn and no cancel taken (~0.37 µs a cell, each the host's one-cell page). A window now waits at least 1 ms and holds 16,384 cells (~6 ms of the wide shape's floats), so frames and a cancel go between them.

## Reproducing it

```sh

just tall-table pairs
just tall-table soak
SEED=<n> cargo test -p seiza-tall-table --features custom-cells,live,follow,resize,select,copy,sort,pin,density,format,reorder,clicks,group-headers,hide,find state::random   # n = 0…6
python3 components/tall-table/perf/results/raw/m20-b-mutate.py
just tall-table build find && node components/tall-table/perf/results/raw/m20-b-find-host.ts
```
