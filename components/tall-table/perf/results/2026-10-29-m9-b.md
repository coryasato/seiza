Measured on Apple M1, Darwin 25.6.0 arm64, machine `local`, by `just tall-table size --against a0cc05f` (M9's parent, M8 part D's commit) on part B's final working tree, after the code review's fixes. Raw: `2026-10-29-m9-b-size.json`. Frame work and the browsers come in parts C and D.
