# M3 part D: the flash, and `live`'s cost (2026-10-25)

Measured on Apple M1, Darwin 25.6.0 arm64, machine `local`, Chromium headless=new, 1440×900 at DPR 2, cold, median of 10, reference and throttled (CPU 4×, Fast 4G) (root CLAUDE.md, "Measurement protocol"), on the final build. Load average 2.0–8.4 over the runs (the first run's 8.4 eased to 2–4 by the second).

Raw: `2026-10-25-m3-d-size.json` (`just tall-table size --against e54d02c`); `2026-10-25-m3-d-live-<rate>[-flash][-throttled].json` (`just tall-table live`, 12 files); `2026-10-25-m3-d-frames-live-cells.json` and `2026-10-25-m3-d-frames-base.json` (`just tall-table perf`, the feature's build with the feed off, and the base); `2026-10-25-m3-d-flash-heat.png` and `2026-10-25-m3-d-flash-bars.png` (the order).

## What changed

- **The table** (`live` with `custom-cells`): `TallTableColumn::flash(ramp, duration)`; each page's changed rows kept by generation while a column flashes; a landing re-read compared with the page it replaces (rise 1, fall 0, other 0.5; NaN equals NaN); the flash drawn as a quad over the fill, faded by opacity, frames asked for only while one is on screen, held with a timer under `App::reduce_motion`; `PaintProbe::flash`.
- **The fixture:** `paint.json`'s `flash` section (12 cases), its expectations written by `paint-fixture.ts` from the rule in TS and Chromium's mix.
- **The bare host:** `?flash`; the bridge's `flash` field; `smoke` checks drawn cells change under the feed and flashes draw with `?flash`; `live.ts` measures latency first, then frame work and late frames in a second window; `size.ts` measures a two-feature build against each feature alone.

- **The rates saturate early:** the feed sends a batch about every 29 ms (its 16 ms timer, on the web), each changing distinct visible rows; at 10k and 50k every visible row changes in every batch, and at 1k about half. One page holds them, so it's one re-read a batch at every rate: re-reads a second and the frames' cost hardly move from 1k to 50k. "Work p50" counts every frame Chromium began (300 in 5 s), drawn or not, so without the flash its p50 mixes idle frames (≈1 ms) and redraws (≈5–6 ms reference, 17–21 throttled); busy time is the cleaner total.
- **Reference: the table keeps up at every rate,** no late frames, with or without the flash. The flash draws every frame (+45–55 ms of main thread a second, about 15%), and moves latency from ~8 to ~12 ms p50: a re-read landing between frames now waits for the next frame, where without the flash the table drew as soon as it landed. Its p95 is lower (18 against 24 ms): no batch waits more than about a frame.
- **Throttled: the flash saturates the main thread** (~995 ms busy a second): every frame redraws all 280 cells at 17–21 ms of work, 10.5–12.6% of frames are late, and re-reads fall from ~30 to ~14 a second (the feed's timer gets fewer turns). Without the flash the table keeps up (0–1.2% late). A host on a slow machine with a market watch's rates would want fewer flashing columns or a shorter flash; the table offers no throttle of its own today.
- **Spreads** are 1.8–9.2%, several over the 6% gate; the flash-against-none differences (latency +4 ms, busy +50 ms/s reference, +350 ms/s throttled, late frames 0 → 11%) are far outside them.

## After the code review

Fixed after the numbers above were taken: a landing re-read now also compares the rows invalidated while it was out (a source that reads when it answers already holds their change; before, that change never flashed), each page's changed rows are merged row sets (overlapping ranges had piled up and been compared more than once) cleared when no column flashes, the jump's edged highlight is drawn over a flash on a column with no fill, the fade is computed in f64 (in f32 the 900 ms case drew alpha 26 where the fixture's JS writes 25), a fill's box comes from the same helper as a flash's, and a landing with nothing to compare reads no clock. New test: `a_change_the_landing_read_already_holds_flashes_then`. Tests, lint, `paint-fixture --check`, and `smoke` (all three browsers, the feed with and without `?flash`) pass on the fixed build. The bare host's source answers in the turn that asks, so it has no read out while rows change.

## Checks

- **D1:** `just tall-table smoke` on the `live` + `custom-cells` build: every shape and paint style, and the narrow shape at `?live=1000`, with and without `?flash`, in Chromium (WebGPU), Firefox (WebGL2), and WebKit (WebGPU): refreshed marks 0.9–1.7 ms after the frame's last GPU call, in its task; 92 drawn cells changed under the feed; 92 of 280 drawn cells flashing with `?flash` (every visible float), none without.
- **D2:** `src/state/fixtures/flash.rs`: `a_reread_flashes_what_changed_and_fades` (rise at 1, fall at 0, text at 0.5; unchanged, NaN→NaN, and a column without `flash` unpainted and unrecorded: 3 live flashes; alpha 255 → 191 at 250 ms; none and the map empty at 1 s; frames asked for while one is drawn, none after); `a_row_changed_during_a_read_flashes_when_the_next_lands`; `a_change_the_landing_read_already_holds_flashes_then` (after the code review); `reduced_motion_holds_the_flash_then_a_timer_clears_it` (held at 255 at 500 ms, no frames, no redraw until the timer at 1 s). `flash.rs`'s unit tests (`changes`, `fades`).
- **D3:** `flash_fixture` passes (12 cases, light and dark); `flash_fade_backwards_fails` and `flash_fall_as_rise_fails` fail it; `just tall-table paint-fixture --check` passes.
- **Tests and lint:** `just tall-table test` passes off, with each feature, and with both (65 table tests and 8 host with both, after the code review); `just tall-table lint` passes.

## Reproducing it

```sh
just tall-table size --against e54d02c --label m3-d-size
just tall-table build live,custom-cells
just tall-table smoke
just tall-table live --rate 1000 [--flash] [--profile throttled] --label m3-d-live-1k[-flash][-throttled]   # and 10000, 50000
just tall-table perf --label m3-d-frames-live-cells
just tall-table build && just tall-table perf --label m3-d-frames-base
```
