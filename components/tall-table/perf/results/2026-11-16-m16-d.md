- **A band's and an arrow's stop each, not one for the header, is kept:** over `resize,sort`, `clicks` reads −0.09 to +0.14 ms a segment (the wide sorted fling the most, the wide sorted scroll the least), every range overlapping, the drag at the display's rate in both.
