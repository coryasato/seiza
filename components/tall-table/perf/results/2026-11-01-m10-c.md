# M10 part C: the scripts and the browsers (2026-11-01)

Measured on Apple M1, macOS 26.6.2 arm64 (Darwin 25.6.0), machine `local`, Chromium headless=new, 1440×900 at DPR 2, cold, **one run each** (a check that the scripts measure `density`, not the protocol's 10; the medians are part D's), reference and throttled (CPU 4×, Fast 4G), both shapes, the `density` build.

Raw: `2026-11-01-m10-c-perf-dense.json` (`just tall-table perf --runs 1`, the host's default density: 18 px rows, 11 px text, 56 px floats), `2026-11-01-m10-c-perf-control.json` (the same with `--density 30,14`, the base's numbers).

## What changed

Nothing in the table. The scripts: the host's `__tallTableDensity` hook; `perf.ts`'s `--density`, its presented frames, and its rests; the smoke check's density runs; `density-fixture.ts`, which pins `fixtures/density.json`'s `number_px` from Chromium. No bytes measured: the hook is behind `?perf` in the `density` build, whose size is part D's.

## C1: the browsers

`just tall-table smoke` (Chromium, Firefox, WebKit, both shapes) passes on three builds:

- **The base:** saves each browser's and shape's first screen (`perf/results/raw/smoke-base/`, gitignored), each beside the commit and a hash of the source changes it was drawn from; two runs wrote byte-identical screens. A record edited to another tree's is refused (its control).
- **`density`:** at the default, the layout probe's row 18 px and every float 56 px; rows 1–6's middles alternating and each row's last pixel its border at 18 px; the text's ink inside its row and inside its cell's 6 px padding (ink in all 11 narrow columns, 23 wide); scrolled to row 40 and at rest (no frame for 300 ms), `__tallTableDensity(30, 14)` (30 px rows and 14 px text; the floats keep the host's 56 px, so not the base's layout) and back to 18, 11 keeping the top at 40.00 both ways. At `?density=30,14`, the first screen identical to the base's in every browser and shape, pixel for pixel.
- **`density,select,resize,sort,pin`:** every feature's own checks at the dense default. `resize`: a float 56 → 136 px (budget 6 → 18), a fit, the last column narrowed to the least at 11 px text, 31 px. `select`, `sort` (glyphs, icons, opened sorted): unchanged checks, passing. `pin`: the narrow shape's columns all fit at the default, so the check narrows the viewport to 912 px for 150 px of sideways scroll; the edge line, the held pinned pixels, the thumb, and the band drag pass.

Negative controls: the dense checks with a padding 2 px wider and rows sampled at 30 px failed (the stripes; the narrow shape's left-aligned text outside the padding). The wide shape's right-aligned floats alone didn't catch the wider padding: their ink ends inside the 1.5 px slack.

`number_px` from Chromium (`just tall-table density-fixture`): the integer column `spkid` (20 characters) is 185, 231, 145, 182, 317, and 79 px wide at 14, 17.5, 11, 13.75, 24, and 6 px text, so `number_px` is 0.6 em at each, the provisional values exactly. `--check` passes; `density_fixture` and its controls pass with them.

Every segment reached its rest in both runs, the throttled dense ones included (the spike's 30 s wait timed out there). The presented frames show what the late share hides: the dense wide scroll at the reference run is 0% late and presents 30 frames a second, on half the rAFs, as the spike found; its trace p50 (0.20 ms) reads the rAFs that drew nothing, and the recorder's (20.97 ms) is the frame's work. The control presents on 95–99% of rAFs in every reference segment, where the spike's base presented on ~85% (124 of 145): the spike counted rAFs over the whole window, its idle tail included, and the share now counts to the last frame presented.

These are the runs after the code review. The first pair waited out the rest's 300 ms quiet spell inside every window, so the trace's frames, the late share, and the placeholders counted its idle rAFs: the throttled late shares read lower (the dense narrow scroll's 55.95%, now 74.19%). Every reading now ends 300 ms after the segment's last presented frame, as before the quiet spell.

## Reproducing it

```sh
just tall-table build && just tall-table smoke          # the base's screens
just tall-table build density && just tall-table smoke  # the density runs and the control
just tall-table density-fixture --check
just tall-table perf --runs 1 --label m10-c-perf-dense
just tall-table perf --runs 1 --density 30,14 --label m10-c-perf-control
just tall-table build density,select,resize,sort,pin && just tall-table smoke
```
