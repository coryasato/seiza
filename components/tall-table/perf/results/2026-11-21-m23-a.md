# M23 part A: the fade's spike (2026-11-21)

Measured on Apple M1, macOS 26.6.2 (Darwin 25.6.0 arm64), machine `local`, Chromium headless at 1440×900, DPR 2 (screenshots at DPR 1 too), the bare host's release build with `popover,select`. A spike, not a protocol run: the page's work samples (`shared-web/perf/`'s `workRecorder`), not a trace's, median over about 181 frames a run, three rounds interleaved, no load reading taken. It chose a way to fade; the feature's own cost is read at the milestone's close.

Raw: none kept. The spike's code (a switch in `src/cells_text.rs`'s `paint`, a `?fade=<mode>` parameter in the bare host, a script) was thrown away with the part.

## What changed

- Nothing shipped. The spike drew the hovered cell's cut text without its "…", fading from 64 px to 16 px short of the content box's right end, three ways: each glyph painted once at its middle's alpha; each glyph painted again in every 4 px strip it crosses, a content mask and an alpha a strip; the same with strips a device pixel wide.

## Experiments and controls

**The look:** the host opened with `?popover-long=300` (a text column of 300-character values, every cell cut), the pointer on one cell, screenshots of that cell and its neighbors at DPR 1 and 2. The three fades couldn't be told apart at reading size: a glyph is about 7 px of the 48 px ramp, so a glyph's alpha is seven steps, a letter each.

**The frames:** the same page with every cut cell in view faded (about 28 a frame, many times what the feature draws: a frame fades two cells at most), 180 wheels of 40 px, 16 ms apart.

| The way | Round 1 | Round 2 | Round 3 | Against none |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| None (the "…", the control) | 6.93 ms | 6.98 ms | 6.81 ms | |
| A glyph's alpha | 7.09 | 7.01 | 6.84 | +0.03 to +0.16 |
| Strips 4 px wide | 7.69 | 7.89 | 8.34 | +0.8 to +1.5 |
| Strips a device pixel wide | 11.37 | 10.91 | 10.61 | +3.8 to +4.4 |

The glyph's alpha is inside the control's own range in two rounds of three; it also reshapes each faded cell's uncut text, which the control doesn't. Chosen: the glyph's alpha (`docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Features", 2026-11-21).

## Reproducing it

The spike is gone; the build's fade is measured at the close with `just tall-table perf`.
