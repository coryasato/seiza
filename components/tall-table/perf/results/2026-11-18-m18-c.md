## In the browsers (C2)

`just tall-table smoke`'s replace run, in every build, both shapes: the source cut to every other row and `replace_rows` called in one update (`__tallTableReplace(2, at)`) at the top, row 400,000, a row past the new last top (clamped to the end), and the end; each view filled at its target with 783,762 rows; then the whole source replaced back at the row whose view ends at the strided last row × 2, and the two views' last rows' first cells (the probe's `lastCell`) equal. With a build that publishes the drawn cells, every cell of the rows both views draw too; with `sort`, a column sorted first (the narrow shape's `H`, the wide's first), kept through each replace, and the strided top in its order.
