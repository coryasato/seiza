# M18 part B: `replace_rows` built and tested (2026-11-18)

Measured on Apple M1, Darwin 25.6.0 arm64, machine `local`. Bytes only: the bare host's release build (`cargo build -p`, wasm-bindgen, wasm-opt with the apps' flags, brotli 11), and the random test's controls natively. No frame work was measured: the reset runs once a call, and no per-frame code changed (the root's research, "`replace_rows`", "Frame-work risk"); the call's time to its first filled frame is `sort`'s, read against the parent in part C.

Raw: `2026-11-18-m18-b.json` (`just tall-table size --against 55a9597 --label m18-b`: the base, each feature's build, the symbols, and the base against M18's parent); the control counts and the mutation script in the gitignored `raw/` (`2026-11-18-m18-b-control-counts*.txt`, `m18-b-counts.sh`, `m18-b-mutate.py`).

## What changed

- **The table:** `replace_rows(rows, at)` and `ViewAt` in the base (`src/state/replace.rs`), `sort`'s reset moved there (`forget_all` and the reset's hooks out of `sort`'s `cfg`), the sort told again to a new source, `set_columns`' reset run at once but for following (`docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Features", 2026-11-18).
- **The bare host:** `__tallTableReplace(stride, at)` in the base with `?perf`, over a source that can answer every `stride`-th row.

## The random test's controls

26 controls, each run under `SEED`s 0–6 in the all-on build (48 cases a seed), on the final build and before its code review's fixes, the same:

| Controls | Caught |
|---|---|
| The reset's: `ResetKeepsPages`, `ResetKeepsReads` (invariant 5 or 7), `sort`'s `Untold` (20) | 21 of 21 |
| The 25 earlier controls, with the replaces placed | 166 of 175 |
| The same, without the replaces (a switch, removed, before the review's fixes) | 170 of 175 |

The replaces cost four catches: `HoldingWithoutEnd` at seeds 3 and 4, `CloseKeepsReads` at 3, `KeepOnReread` at 4. The misses that were there without them (`GapOutsideParent` at 1, 3, 6; `IgnoreLoading` at 5, 6) stay. `SEED` 0, which `just tall-table test` runs, catches every control.

**The replaces' stream:** placed last from a constant of their own, SHA-512's words tried in turn at `SEED` 0: the third, fourth, and sixth lost `HoldingWithoutEnd`'s only catching case, the fifth `HitIgnoresRun`'s; the seventh catches every control, and is the one kept.

## Experiments and controls

- **The paging fixture's `replace` control** (`keep-pages`, in every build) fails its scenarios in the base build, where no `sort` scenario runs.
- **Every rule mutated:** twenty of the call's rules (what the reset clears and keeps, the landing rows, following, the sort's tell and its refusal, the pressed arrow, `set_columns`' reset and its let-go, the reads held for the tell and planned after the events, a closed table's pages), each mutated alone in the all-on build, each failing at least one window test or the paging fixture. Two first passed: the tail drawn kept (the test replaced with a larger count, so the tail grew anyway: it now replaces with the same count) and the flashes kept (the mutation left its `cfg` behind and didn't build).
- **The soak** (`just tall-table soak`: 4 `SEED`s × 500 cases, every feature with steps in the random test): passed before the code review and again after its fixes, ~12 min each.
