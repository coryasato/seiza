# M23 part B: a long value's cell (2026-11-21)

Measured on Apple M1, macOS 26.6.2 (Darwin 25.6.0 arm64), machine `local`: frame work in the wide shape with a column of 10,000-character texts on screen, before and after the change and against the base (Chromium 153 headless=new, 1440×900 at DPR 2, cold, median of 10, the reference run alone: the change is to what a frame copies and shapes, not to the paging); the wasm's bytes, the bare host's and each app's that builds the table; the reference screens in three browsers. The after runs are on the code as reviewed (`/code-review`, seven nits, fixed). Load average 3–8 before each frame run (builds between them), 3–5 after.

Raw: `2026-11-21-m23-b-frames-long-before.json` (the `popover` build at `4561bee`, `--popover 10000`), `-frames-base.json` (the base build with the change), `-frames-long.json` (the `popover` build with the change, `--popover 10000`), `-size.json` (`size --against 4561bee`). In the gitignored `raw/`: the reference screens (`smoke-screens/`), and the long column's screens with the one-off script that took them (`m23-b-long-screens.ts`, `m23-b-long-screens/`).

## What changed

- **A text longer than its cell could show is drawn from its start** (`TallTableState::bounded_text`): as many characters as the content box and an em hold at an eighth of the text size each (1.75 px at 14 px), taken before the value is copied, its line breaks replaced, and shaped. The start is used only where it proves the cut (shaped wider than the box, and GPUI's `should_truncate_line` cutting inside it); any other text is drawn from its whole value. A text cut in the middle is left as it was. `CellProbe::shown` is still the whole text (`docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Features", 2026-11-21).
- **The base's wasm:** +1.0 KiB after wasm-opt, +1.3 KiB brotli (below).

## The same pixels (B1)

- **The reference screens:** `just tall-table smoke --screens 4561bee` on the base build: 12 screens (Chromium, Firefox, WebKit; narrow and wide; first and scrolled) pixel for pixel.
- **The long column itself,** which the reference screens don't draw: a `popover` build opened with `?popover-long=10000`, the build at `4561bee` against the build with the change, 12 screens (the same browsers, shapes, and views) the same to the byte.
- **Natively:** `a_long_text_is_drawn_from_its_start` (`src/state/cells_text_tests.rs`): texts a character under the bound, at it, over it, and at 10,000 characters (with spaces and punctuation at the cut, with line breaks, in characters of more than a byte), each drawn (`DrawnText`: the cut's text, its place, its box) as the control draws it from its whole value; four cells counted drawn from their start, and none where the start doesn't prove its cut (the advance widened to an em). `just tall-table test` and `lint` pass, the cell fixtures untouched; `just observatory fixtures` passes; `just tycho table` and `cells` pass.

## Experiments and controls

- **The native test's control** draws every cell from its whole value (`unbounded_text`); the bounded cells' `DrawnText`s equal it.
- **The bound too short and unchecked** (an em a character, the check forced true), by hand: the test fails, the long cells drawn as their start with no "…". With the check back and the same advance, every cell falls back to its whole value (kept in the test).
- **Not reached:** a text of glyphs with no advance (combining marks) in a browser. GPUI's test font gives every character 0.6 em, so the fallback is tested by widening the advance, not by such a text, and the bare host has none.
