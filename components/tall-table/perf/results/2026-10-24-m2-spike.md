# M2 part A: how a cell's paint is drawn, a spike (2026-10-24)

Measured on Apple M1, Darwin 25.6.0 arm64, machine `local`, Chromium headless=new, 1440×900 at DPR 2, cold, the reference profile only, the wide shape only: an A/B, not a protocol run. Interleaved rounds (each configuration 2 runs a round, in turn), so the machine's drift lands on every configuration alike. Load average 3.6 before, 2.4–3.6 during the main A/B; 5.2 → 3.0 during the follow-up.

Raw: `2026-10-24-m2-spike.json` (every run, by round and configuration; `perf.ts` with the spike's URL switches).

## What was measured

The spike, since thrown away (part A builds no shipped code): a `paint` on the page answering a fill (0..1 through a two-stop ramp, `background` → `chart_1`, mixed in Oklab per cell) and a bar (0..1 of the cell's inner width, from a zero point at the middle), and two ways to draw them:
- **elements:** the fill as `.bg()` on the cell's existing element, a bar as a child `div` under the text;
- **quads:** both as `window.paint_quad` calls in one `canvas` under the cells layer.

The host painted every float cell of the wide shape: `?cells=heat` (fills) or `?cells=bars`, against `?cells=text` (no paint, the control). The screenshots of the two drawings were byte-identical for heat and for bars. The wasm with both drawings and the ramp linked: 2,679.5 KiB brotli against the base's 2,677.1 (+2.4 KiB, the spike's switches included; part B measures the real feature).

## The drawing A/B

4 rounds, 8 runs per configuration. Work per frame, the trace's (ms, median of the runs' p50s). Spread is the gate's, (max − min) / median of the runs' p50s, which must stay under 6% (root `docs/DECISIONS.md`, "An interleaved A/B is gated on its own spread"):

| Configuration | scroll p50 | spread | scroll p95 | hfling p50 | spread | hfling p95 | fling p50 | fling p95 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| text (control) | 7.13 | 5.6% | 8.62 | 6.63 | 3.0% | 7.55 | 4.48 | 8.82 |
| heat, elements | 7.75 | 3.6% | 9.18 | 7.23 | **11.9%** | 8.05 | 4.28 | 9.40 |
| heat, quads | 7.69 | **7.7%** | 9.04 | 7.41 | 3.5% | 8.19 | 4.28 | 9.21 |
| bars, elements | 8.27 | 2.8% | 9.66 | 7.92 | 4.2% | 8.77 | 4.22 | 9.82 |
| bars, quads | 7.87 | 2.4% | 9.39 | 7.44 | 2.7% | 8.32 | 4.15 | 9.33 |

No late frames in any run; 308 cells a frame (scroll, fling), 336 (hfling). The fling's p50s spread 7–23% in every configuration (its frames are ~45% placeholders, which paint nothing), so no comparison is read from the fling.

- **A bar** (passes the gate in both segments): as a quad it adds 0.74 ms in the scroll and 0.81 sideways over the control, against 1.14 and 1.29 as a child element, about a third cheaper. A child `div` is one more layout node per cell per frame; a quad isn't laid out.
- **A fill** (fails the gate: heat on the element sideways at 11.9%, heat as quads in the scroll at 7.7%): +0.62 on the element against +0.56 as a quad in the scroll, +0.60 against +0.78 sideways. Read only as "close"; no winner.
- **Bars as quads cost 10–12% of the base's frame work** (0.74 of 7.13 ms, 0.81 of 6.63) with every visible cell painted; fills about 8–9%, either way.
- **Not measured:** a cell with both a fill and a bar. In the spike a bar quad painted before the cells, so a fill on the cell's element would have covered it (the decision this note backs draws both as quads, the fill first).

## Experiments and controls

- **The ramp's math, against one fixed fill color** (3 rounds, 6 runs each: text, heat with the ramp, heat with one color): scroll 7.12 / 7.70 / 7.59 ms, hfling 6.34 / 7.29 / 7.12. **Spread 9–19%: the load rose to 5.2, so this run fails the 6% gate** and is a hint only: the Oklab mix may be ~0.1–0.2 ms of heat's ~0.6, the rest the fill's quad itself. Not enough to cache a ramp's colors in a table, which would also quantize what the fixture pins.
- **The control** (text) matches M1's wide reference baseline (scroll 7.06, hfling 6.56 ms there), so the spike's switches cost nothing with paint off.

## Reproducing it

The spike's code wasn't kept. To repeat it, rebuild the two drawings behind `?cells=` and `?draw=` in the host and run, per configuration, in rounds:

```sh
just tall-table build
just tall-table perf --shape wide --profile reference --runs 2 --label <configuration>-r<round>
```
