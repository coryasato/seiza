# M20 follow-up: `find`'s scan cost, the term lowered once (2026-11-19)

Measured on Apple M1, macOS 26.6.2, machine `local`, Chromium headless=new (Playwright 1.63.0), 1440×900 at DPR 2, reference profile, cold, 5 runs a build, the two builds interleaved (A, B, B, A, …). gpui-kit 0.10.0. Load average 9.3 as it started (falling: a `pairs` run had just ended); both builds ran under it in turn.

Raw: `2026-11-19-m20-scan.json` (each run's script and task time a cell, and its wall time).

## What changed

- `FindQuery::matches` lowered the term at every cell (an allocation and a fold a cell); the query now lowers it once, as it's built, and `term` is read through `FindQuery::term()`. A source calls `matches` once a cell, as the bare host's `MemoryRows::find` does.

## Against HEAD

The bare host built with `find`, both builds from this tree, the only difference `matches`' body (`matches(exact, &lower(&self.term))`, HEAD's, against `matches(exact, &self.lower)`). Each run: the wide shape, the term `0.7600149863517591`, whose first match is row 100,000's last cell, so a step on from the top tests 50,000,500 cells; Chromium's `ScriptDuration` and `TaskDuration` (CDP `Performance.getMetrics`) read before the step and after its `Found`, divided by the cells. The windows' waits are idle, so script time is the scan's work.

| Build | Script a cell, median (range) | Task a cell, median (range) | Wall, median |
|---|---|---|---|
| HEAD (lowered a cell) | 473.4 ns (470.1–477.3) | 475.8 ns (472.2–479.9) | 54.24 s |
| The term lowered once | 406.4 ns (402.4–408.8) | 408.9 ns (404.0–411.3) | 50.90 s |

−67 ns a cell (−14%), each build's runs within 1.5% of its median. A window of `FIND_WINDOW`'s 16,384 cells is now ~6.7 ms of script (7.8 at HEAD); its comment says ~0.41 µs a cell and about 7 ms.

The old figure (~0.37 µs a cell, a scan of all 785 M in 295 s) was a different reading: wall time of one task that never yielded, before the windows waited. It isn't comparable with these.

## Experiments and controls

- **The windows wait ~10 ms each, not `FIND_YIELD`'s 1 ms.** Wall minus script over the 3,052 windows: (50.90 − 20.32) s / 3,052 = 10.0 ms in the fixed build, 10.0 ms at HEAD. So ~60% of a scan's wall time is waiting between windows. Not the table's cost (the bare host's source chooses its wait), and not looked into here.

## Reproducing it

A one-off script (not kept): the term chosen natively from `MemoryRows::exact` (row 100,000, column 499, no earlier cell matching), then each build's `host/web/dist` served apart and opened in turn, the step taken through `__tallTableFindNext` and its end read on `__tallTableFound`, the metrics through a CDP session.
