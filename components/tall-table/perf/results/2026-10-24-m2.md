# M2: `custom-cells`, built and measured (2026-10-24)

Measured on Apple M1, Darwin 25.6.0 arm64, machine `local`, Chromium headless=new, 1440×900 at DPR 2, cold, median of 10, reference and throttled (CPU 4×, Fast 4G) (root CLAUDE.md, "Measurement protocol"). The wide shape only, as the spec asks: the base's release build, then the feature's (`just tall-table build custom-cells`) at `?cells=text` (paint compiled in, none drawn), `heat`, and `bars`, one after another, each started at a 1-minute load average under 3 (1.9–2.8 at each start).

Raw: `2026-10-24-m2-size.json` (`just tall-table size --against c3da129`), `2026-10-24-m2-frames-{base,text,heat,bars}.json` (`just tall-table perf --shape wide [--cells …]`), `2026-10-24-m2-jump.png` (a jump onto heat cells).

## What changed

- **The table** (`custom-cells`, off by default): `CellPaint` from the page (`TallTablePage::paint`), each column's `fill` and `text_ramp` (`Ramp`, theme tokens mixed in Oklab) and `bar` (`Bar`, from a zero point); fills and bars as quads in one `canvas` under the cells' text; the drawn paint in `CellProbe` (`PaintProbe`). A column with no ramp or bar asks its page nothing in a release build.
- **The bridge and the host** forward the feature; the host paints every float column through its range (`?cells=text|heat|bars|color`).
- **`fixtures/paint.json`** and its native tests; `just tall-table paint-fixture` writes its colors from Chromium.
- **The scripts:** `size` builds the base and the feature's build, checks the feature's symbols, and builds the base at another commit; `perf --cells`; `smoke` at each paint style, with a fill sampled on screen.

- **Compiled in, unused, it costs nothing:** `text` against the base, reference, 0.00 / +0.01 / +0.01 ms (scroll, sideways, fling), within the spread; throttled within 0.2 ms at p50. The throttled fling's p95 (24.90 against 15.03) is one run's tail in a segment that's 82–85% placeholder frames, whose p50s spread 5–7%; no other segment moved.
- **With every visible cell painted** (against `text`, reference): a fill adds 0.63 ms in the scroll and 0.79 sideways (9% and 12%), a bar 0.73 and 0.77 (10% and 12%). The spike's quads read +0.56 / +0.78 (fill) and +0.74 / +0.81 (bar) (`2026-10-24-m2-spike.md`): the build matches it. No late frames at the reference run.
- **Throttled,** painting every cell adds ~2.5–4.4 ms a frame; late frames in the scroll rise from 59% to 67–68%, sideways from 42% to 56%. The base already drops frames there (M1); the paint makes it worse in proportion. A host painting some columns pays for those.
- **The fling** barely moves (+0.18 ms at `heat`, 0 at `bars`): ~45% of its frames are placeholders, which paint nothing.
- **Outside the frames,** every build reads the same (the scroll's 35 ms, the fling's ~820 ms of page-making at the reference run): the paint is all in-frame.

## The jump's highlight

`2026-10-24-m2-jump.png`: a jump to row 1,567,521 (`?jump=1567520`, near the end, so the row isn't at the top) onto heat cells. The base's highlight, a tint under the cells, was hidden by the fills, and the same tint drawn over them vanished on the blue ramp; drawn over a filled cell with a 1 px top and bottom edge in `table_active_border`, the row reads across every fill. Only filled cells get it (`docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Features").

## Experiments and controls

- **`paint.json`** (`just tall-table test`): the table drew 34 of 36 fixture colors (18 cases, light and dark) exactly as Chromium's `color-mix(in oklab, …)` and 2 within one step. Controls: every color two steps off (shifted away from what was drawn) fails, every bar 2 device pixels off fails, and the ramps mixed in sRGB fail. A theme switch changed the drawn paint with no page read.
- **`just tall-table smoke`:** both shapes at `heat`, `bars`, and `color` load, fill, and scroll in Chromium (WebGPU), Firefox (WebGL2), and WebKit (WebGPU), every drawn float cell carrying its paint; at `heat` the darkest fill fully on screen, sampled from a screenshot, matched the probe's color exactly in all three.
- **A probe costs frames:** the code review caught that with `?perf` the painted styles had also published every drawn cell each frame, which `text` and the base didn't; caught before these runs. Cell probing is its own parameter now (`?probe-cells`), used only by the smoke check.

## Reproducing it

```sh
just tall-table size --against c3da129 --label m2-size
just tall-table build custom-cells && just tall-table smoke
just tall-table build && just tall-table perf --shape wide --label m2-frames-base
just tall-table build custom-cells
just tall-table perf --shape wide --cells text --label m2-frames-text   # and heat, bars
```
