# M12 part C: format in the browsers, its cost, and the close (2026-11-05)

Measured on Apple M1, Darwin 25.6.0 arm64, machine `local`, Chromium 153 headless=new, 1440×900 at DPR 2, cold (root CLAUDE.md, "Measurement protocol"): the reference run a median of 10, the throttled run (CPU 4×, Fast 4G) once each, as the milestone's check asked. Measured on this part's final code (after its code review's fixes). Load average 3.6–5.4 over the runs (other work on the machine; not a quiet one).

Raw: `2026-11-05-m12-c-size.json` (`just tall-table size`); `2026-11-05-m12-c-frames-base-reference.json` and `-throttled.json` (the base), `2026-11-05-m12-c-frames-format-reference.json` and `-throttled.json` (the `format` build, the host's formats: every float column at 2 decimals, 3 on every fourth row, a `+` on a rise, grouped; the narrow shape's `spkid` grouped), `2026-11-05-m12-c-frames-format-off-reference.json` and `-throttled.json` (the same build at `?format=off`, nothing formatted: the code's cost apart from the drawing's). Run in this order: the base, the formatted, and the control at the reference run, then the formatted, the control, and the base throttled.

## What changed

- **The layout probe carries the table's advances** (`TallTableLayout::advances`, with `format`): each character a format writes and its advance at the text in effect, `Glyphs`' own, which the fit sums; the bridge publishes them as `advances` on the layout. The fixture's script and the smoke check read them there.
- The bare host probes its cells with `format` (`?perf&probe-cells`, as `custom-cells` and `sort` do): code in the host's `format` build only.
- `perf/format-fixture.ts` (`just tall-table format-fixture`), the smoke check's format runs (`perf/smoke.ts`, on `perf/harness.ts`'s TS formatter: half to even on the exact value), and `perf.ts`'s `--format off`.

- **Formatted costs no more than the base, and less where it shortens the text.** The narrow shape (5 of its 11 columns formatted) is within its range of the base's. The wide shape (every one of its 500 columns a float) is 0.4–0.8 ms a frame cheaper: −17% scrolling, −10% in the fling, −9% sideways. The likely reason, not isolated here: the base writes a float in as many characters as its 120 px column's budget holds (12), the format in 5 or 6 (`+0.12`, `+0.123`), and a formatted text that fits is drawn whole, with no cut test. The format's own work a cell (the rounding, the advances summed) costs less than the glyphs it saves.
- **The control:** in the wide shape, within the base's range in every segment. In the narrow shape it reads 0.10–0.14 ms (2–3%) over the base in the scroll and the fling, its range starting where the base's ends. One session each, on a machine that wasn't quiet: not separated from noise here. The formatted build, which runs the same code and more, reads at the base in those segments.
- Placeholders, cells, and intervals are the same in all three builds (the fling's ~45% placeholder frames are the base's).

## In the browsers (C1)

`just tall-table format-fixture --check`: the table's advances at 14 px in Chromium (its layout probe's) are the digits and `+` 8.4, `-` 5.586, `.` and `,` 3.808, `e` 7.686, the values part B read from the font natively, so `cells.json` is unchanged; `%`, a suffix's, is shaped whole by the table and stays the native test's 12.978.

`just tall-table smoke` after a base build's smoke saved its first screens, on the `format` build and again on a `format,resize` build, in Chromium, Firefox, and WebKit, every check passing:

- The smoke check's formatter, first: 26 of `cells.json`'s 33 `format` cases written and fitted as the fixture says (the 7 skipped by design: a float without fixed decimals, NULL, NaN, the infinities; any other it can't write fails).
- Formatted: the narrow shape's first screen 28 `spkid` cells grouped, 21 at 3 decimals, 67 at 2; after a wheel down (from a frame at the new rows) 28, 23, 69. The wide shape's 77 at 3 and 231 at 2 both times. Every one the source's exact value formatted, half to even, and drawn as the format because its advances fit the content box less a pixel; no fallback at the kind widths.
- Narrowed (`format,resize`), a float column dragged from 120 to 56 px and its cells read from a frame laid out at 56: the narrow shape's `H`, all 28 fell back; the wide shape's `c001`, 21 still fitting at 2 decimals and 7 falling back. Each fallback is the automatic format: its exact text within the column's budget, else another float's text, never a `+` or `,`.
- `?format=off`: every float and `spkid` cell drawn as the base draws it (116 in the narrow shape, 308 in the wide), and on the `format` build the first screen the base build's, pixel for pixel, in both shapes and every browser.
