# The state's feature children (`src/state/`)

Each feature's half of `TallTableState` (a child module of `src/state.rs`), and the state's tests. Claude Code loads this file when a file under `src/state/` is read. The table's rules and the map of its folders are in `components/tall-table/CLAUDE.md`, the repo's in the root `CLAUDE.md`; both apply here. `src/CLAUDE.md` (the state entity and the pure modules) loads with it. Paths are relative to `components/tall-table/`.

## Architecture

Each feature's child keeps its state in one field of the state (`SelectState`, `CopyState`, `SortState`, `PinState`, `DensityState`, `ClicksState`, `GroupState`, `reorder`'s `order`, `resize`'s `resizing`, `follow`'s flag; `hide`'s flag is the column's, in `column_info`), and its module's doc comment says the rest: `follow.rs`, `resize.rs`, `select.rs`, `copy.rs` (its window tests in `copy/tests.rs`), `sort.rs`, `pin.rs`, `density.rs`, `reorder.rs`, `groups.rs`, `click.rs`, `hide.rs`, `find.rs` (`FindState`: the term, the current match, the find out), `popover.rs` (`PopoverState`: the popover open, its button and cue, which cells (`PopoverCells`), the cues decided by the rule in effect; its keys, its closers, and its drawing), `header_icon.rs` (`IconState`: the icon hovered and the one pressed; the setter, the drawing, and the press); `replace.rs` (in the base: `replace_rows` and the reset it shares with `sort`, whose hooks are `select`'s `reset_selection` and `follow`'s `reset_following`); `hit.rs` holds the presses' hit tests, built with `select`, `reorder`, or `clicks` (the base has only the hover's `cell_at`). Tests only: `cells_text_tests.rs`, `fixtures.rs` (below), `random.rs` (below).

## Rules

- **A feature's pointer drag is a field of its own, never the scrollbar's `Drag`** (`resize`'s `Resizing`, `select`'s `selecting`, `reorder`'s `Moving`): `drag.is_some()` holds page reads until a scrollbar drag rests (`load_or_settle`). It's followed by the move and release handlers the body's `canvas` registers every frame (`src/CLAUDE.md`'s scrollbar gotcha), with a window-wide wheel where the drag scrolls.
- **A new drag ends in both handlers:** at the left button's release only (another button's leaves every drag on), and at a move with no button down, which ends every drag that's on, not the first found (`docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Features").
- **A listener captures a column's index, never the place its frame drew it at** (`resize`'s band, `sort`'s arrow), and the press finds the place in the order in effect: a host's move can land between the frame and the press. A test of it calls the press inside the update that moved the columns (`docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Fixtures").
- **The root's press listener is one with `clicks`** (`click.rs`'s `press`): it takes every button's press and hands `reorder` and `select` a left press at the table's count; without the feature, their listeners are as before. A new feature's press goes through it in a `clicks` build. A scrollbar, band, or arrow added later stops a right and a long press with `click_stops`; a header's band or arrow with `header_click_stops`, whose long press is its header's under `set_header_tap_is_press`, and whose left-press listener hands a finger's press to `press` under it (`tap_is_headers`). A header's icon (`header-icon`) stops only its left press, in a listener of its own ahead of the root's, and lets through what `clicks` calls its header's (`left_press_is_headers`: a Ctrl press that's a context press, and the tap a lifted long press becomes, which `press` drops).
- **What's kept by row goes in `set_rows` when its row does** (the hover, the jump's mark, the flashes, `find`'s current match, the open popover; a `Rows` or `Cells` range cut, a `Columns` range's rows moved to the last row, its drag left on), as the reset clears it (`reset_rows`: `replace_rows`, a sort), and the tooltip is decided again there (a row may lose its page): state a new feature keeps by row gets its line in both and in the random test's invariants 17 and 19.
- **A setter that changes what a held drag or press points at cancels it** (`reorder`'s `cancel_drag` at `move_block`, `set_order`, `set_columns`, and `hide`'s setters, whose places it would hold stale; `sort`'s pressed and hovered arrow forgotten at `set_columns`, and on a column `hide` hid; `header-icon`'s pressed icon at both, and at a `set_column_icon` that leaves its column none, its hovered one at the last two: at `set_columns` the icon at its index is still under the pointer, and GPUI tells a hover only when it changes). A `resize` drag held at `hide`'s setters ends as its release would, its width reported (`end_resize`), as a second band press ends one: reverting it would leave a host that heard the drag's width (a `set_density` mid-drag tells it) stale.
- **A setter's reads are planned once its update ends** (`live`'s `load_after_update`, `copy_load`, the reset's `reset_load`), once for everything the update changed, and after the events it emitted reach their subscribers: a host that can't write a copy cancels it at `Started` before a read is sent.
- **A copy keeps the cap it started with,** checked a row at a time; a copy that ends cancels its reads still out; `copy` and the keys start nothing on a closed table.
- **A new text size is laid out once, by the new font** (`density`'s `apply_sizes` leaves the glyphs stale, and render reads the font then lays out): laying out first by the old font's advances would move `pin`'s run and move it back.

## Tests

- **`fixtures.rs`** (tests only): the fixtures' native tests (`fixtures/README.md`), a child of `state` so the paging scenarios drive the state's own scroll, drag, and paging paths: a `TallTableState` in GPUI's test app (gpui-kit's `test-support`), over a delegate that answers a read only when the scenario says, on GPUI's test clock (only `advance_clock` moves it, so the settle times are the table's own timers). Cell text goes through `shown_text`, what `render_body` draws. The test app's text system gives every character 0.6 em, 8.4 px at the cells' 14 px, which is IBM Plex Sans's digit advance too. Its children, a feature each: `src/state/fixtures/CLAUDE.md`.
- **`random.rs`** (tests only): the random test of the paging and of what the features keep, under every feature built (`docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Fixtures"): proptest driven by hand from a plain `#[test]` (gpui-kit's `property_test` doesn't compile at the pin), each case a scheduler seed, a drawn `Paging` and table, and up to 300 steps with the element in a window, over a source that stamps each row's version and era, answers in any order, and logs every read and cancel. The invariants (numbered as the research's) are checked after every step: the paging's ten against the source's books, each read judged as it went out (`Run::check`); 11 to 18 against the table's state and the last of each event a host heard (`Run::kept`), at a frame against what it drew (`Run::framed`; with `hide`, 21: nothing of a hidden column drawn or hit; with `find`, 24: every drawn cell tinted exactly when it matches by the rule, written apart from the table's, or is the current match; with `popover`, 25: an open popover's cell in view and its text the page drawn's, also checked at each popover step that leaves one open), `find`'s 22 and 23 from its events (a landed match the source's answer, of the era in effect; one step out, one `Found` a `FindStarted`; its finds' ids past every read's, answered after a delay on the test clock; invariant 4's budget skips a step a find landed in, as it skips following's pin: the view moved between two landings), `popover`'s 26 (none open across a sort or `set_columns`) and its lines in 17, 18, and 19 (none open across `replace_rows`), its 29 at a `PopoverRule` step (a popover Enter opened is on the active cell, and under `CutText` on a cut text, worked out apart from the table's rule by the test font's advances), `header-icon`'s 27 (a left press on a drawn icon is held by its column, starts no drag, and changes no selection) and 28 (an icon pressed, held or reported, is a column's that draws one: none held across `set_columns`, a hide, or its removal), and at a pointer step (`pointer_down`, `pointer_move`, `pointer_up`). Its rules:
  - **A new feature's steps never change an existing case:** they're placed among a case's steps from a stream of their own (`with_pins`, `with_densities`, `with_moves`, `with_gestures`, then `with_replaces`, then `hide`'s `with_hides`, then `find`'s `with_finds` (three streams of up to eight), then `popover`'s `with_popovers` (three), then `header-icon`'s `with_icons` (two of `set_column_icon`s, and one of presses on a drawn icon, each with its release: `Zone::Icon` is never among `zones()`, which the gestures draw from), then `popover`'s cells' `with_popover_rules` (`set_popover_cells` toggled, and Enter on a visible cell made active), the last placed, so the others' places stay; `group-headers`' paths a case's own, `with_paths`), never drawn, so every case's draw, and the controls' cases, are the ones without the feature.
  - **A press, a move, a wheel, and a release are steps of their own** (`Down` in a `Zone`, `Drag`, `PointerWheel`, `Up`), a gesture's placed in order with the case's other steps between them: a new pointer behavior gets a zone or a check at one of them, not a step that presses and releases in one go.
  - **A setter with an event gets its line in `Heard`** (the subscription in `Run::adopt`, the check in `kept`): the last event must be the state after every step. A setter that changes what an event reports with no event of its own, by design (`set_columns`' widths, `set_density`'s clamp), is where the step tells `Heard` so.
  - **A bug it finds** is an `#[ignore]`d scenario test there (`found`) and, until it's fixed, an exclusion that keeps the test from it: a step not generated, a step not run, or a check left out, each behind a variant of an `Exclusion` enum named for its finding and listed in an `EXCLUDED` constant, which `found` runs without. The fix removes the variant, and the enum with its last (`docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Fixtures").
  - **Its controls** (the cache's and `copy`'s `Control`s, `reorder`'s `ReadByPlace`, `BlockOverNeighbor`, `SilentReset`, `CountKept`, and `Recount`, `group-headers`' `GroupsByPlace` and `GapOutsideParent`, `resize`'s `KeepOnRelease` and `Unclamped`, `select`'s `KeepPastRows`, `pin`'s `HitIgnoresRun` and `SilentRepin`, the state's `keep_mark`, `sort`'s `Untold`, `hide`'s `DrawnHidden` (a `pin` control: a column at no width drawn, invariant 21) and `FlagByPlace` (18), `find`'s `KeepFindOnSort` (22) and the match rule's `CaseSensitive` (24, set for the thread: `Control::FindRule`), `popover`'s `KeepPopoverOnSort` (26), `StalePopoverText` (25, with `live`), and `EnterOnAny` (29, with `select`), `header-icon`'s `Unstopped` (27: the icon's press goes on to its header); built with each control's feature, run only in the all-on run) must each fail the cases at its invariant.
  - A failure prints the case as run, its placed steps among them, cut to the steps that matter (`shrunk`: steps left out while the same invariant fails; proptest's own shrink works on the draw, and the placed steps move with every step it drops), with the scheduler seed; `SEED` picks the cases (0 unset), `PROPTEST_CASES` how many (16, 48 in the all-on run).

## Commands

```
SEED=<n> cargo test -p seiza-tall-table --features custom-cells,follow,resize,copy,sort,pin,density,format,reorder,clicks,group-headers,hide,find,popover,header-icon random_paging   # another set of the random test's cases (a failure prints its SEED)
just tall-table soak [seeds] [cases] # the random test soaked: `cases` (500) for each `SEED` of `seeds` (1 2 3 4), every feature with steps or controls in the random test on (not `format`, which has none), release with debug assertions and overflow checks (its time: `docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Fixtures")
```
