# The state's feature children (`src/state/`)

Each feature's half of `TallTableState` (a child module of `src/state.rs`), and the state's tests. Claude Code loads this file when a file under `src/state/` is read. The table's rules and the map of its folders are in `components/tall-table/CLAUDE.md`, the repo's in the root `CLAUDE.md`; both apply here. `src/CLAUDE.md` (the state entity and the pure modules) loads with it. Paths are relative to `components/tall-table/`.

## Architecture

- **`src/state/follow.rs`** (`follow`): following, a child of `state`: the flag (`follow`, `let_go` (the host's: off with the view where it is, nothing read, moved, or re-planned), `is_following`; `reader_moved` sets or clears it by each vertical move's `Reach`, before the move, from the top it lands on: down keeps it or reaches the end, a landing (End, a jump, a drag's release) is at the end or not, up and Home let go; a vertical drag holds it, and a wheel more sideways than vertical is sideways; following starting forgets the tail drawn), the tail pin (`pin_to_tail`, after `set_rows`, a page landing (`pin_and_want`, which re-plans), a resize that keeps the bottom, and a drag's release: the view moved down to `PageCache::loaded_through` from its top once every row in view is loaded, never up, not a scroll), the visible pages bottom first (`tail_first`, in `wanted`), and `TailDrawn` (`note_tail_drawn`, in render, from a filled frame's end). Its `Control` (tests only) is the paging fixture's `follow` negative controls (`docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Features").
- **`src/state/resize.rs`** (`resize`): resizing, a child of `state`: the bands (`render_bands`, over the header's cells: each visible resizable column's right edge, 4 px on each side of a 1 px hairline, `table_row_border`, `border` hovered or dragged, the column-resize cursor), the drag (`Resizing`, the state's `resizing`, never a `Drag`: `press_band`, `resize_to` (width at the press + the pointer's move, clamped; back at the press's x, the set width it had; a kind's width outside the min or max held until the pointer brings it inside), `end_resize`; followed by the body's `canvas` handlers, the cursor held window-wide there), the sideways scroll held during it (`held_content`, read by the state's `content_width`: the content counts as reaching at least the view's right edge, so every clamp holds the scroll and the thumb stays at the view; `scroll_by` drops sideways input; a move re-lays out without bumping the layout generation; a release that changed the width runs `lay_out`, which clamps and reports the layout once, and one that didn't redoes nothing), the double-click fit (`fit_column`: the header's name shaped, a numeric column's exact text by character count at the number advance rounded up to a whole pixel, any other's shown text (and the name) cut to `max_width / 2` characters before it's copied, one-lined, and shaped with the state's `text_width`, which the tooltip's cut test shares, over the visible loaded rows), `column_width`, and `ColumnWidthsChanged` at a release or a fit that changed a set width (`docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Features").
- **`src/state/select.rs`** (`select`): selecting, a child of `state`, its state one field (`SelectState`: the selection, the kinds on, a drag, whether the last jump selected, focus at the last render): every change but a drag's own through `replace_selection`, which ends a drag selecting; `select` clamps a host's cells past the end and does nothing for a kind that's off (a debug assertion); the presses (`press_select`, from the state's root on a left press: `target_at` says a cell (the hover's `cell_at`), a row's number, a header, or the corner; Ctrl and a double-click's second press select nothing), the drag (`selecting`, never a `Drag`: it moves only the head's coordinates the press picked and the range spans, `cell_clamped` keeping the pointer to the visible cells; followed by the body's `canvas` handlers, with a window-wide wheel that scrolls the body through `on_scroll_wheel` and moves the head), the keys (`init`, its actions under `seiza_tall_table`, bound in `TallTableSelect` after the base's; `context` and `on_actions`, which the element calls; `key_step`: a plain move collapses from the anchor, Shift moves the head along the range's direction, the ends stop, `scroll_into_view` the least distance through `scroll_by`, an extended head only along the range's direction), `jump_select` and `drawn_mark` (a jump that selected draws no mark), `cut_selection` (`set_rows`), the drawing (`selected_lines` for the gutter's and the header's tint, `render_selection`: the tint and the active cell's edge as quads, one `canvas` a region (the pinned one's only with `pin`, the tint split at the run's edge into a piece a region, each edged), each region's child after `custom-cells`' and before the text), and `Selected`. Its `Control` (tests only) is the selection fixture's negative controls (`docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Features").
- **`src/state/copy.rs`** (`copy`, which turns on `select`): copying, a child of `state`, its state one field (`CopyState`: the host's `CopyOptions`, the last id, the copy running). Cmd+C and Ctrl+C (`CopySelection`, bound by `copy::init` in `TallTableSelect`, after the selection's; with nothing selected the key propagates; past `key_max_cells` cells, `Declined` and nothing read) and `copy` start it: `Started` emitted in the task that asked, then refused at once (`Failed(TooLarge)`) if its separators alone pass `max_bytes` (`least_bytes`). The turns (`copy_turn`): up to `TURN_CELLS` cells from the turn's page (one read for the copy and landed, or the cache's, `PageCache::holding`, as drawn), one `Text` a turn, `Copied` at the last row, `Failed(TooLarge)` when the text passes the cap or the first page's bytes a row project past twice it; the first turn runs in `copy`, the rest spawned (`copy_wake`, also called by every plan, so a page the cache loads for the reader wakes a copy waiting on it). The reads (`copy_wants`: the pages from the turn's, `background_limit` of them, it hasn't in hand, isn't reading, and the cache neither holds nor is reading for a wanted page (`PageCache::loading`: the copy waits for that read, even one short of rows that grew since, then takes the page or reads it); `start_copy_reads` sends what `plan` granted (`take_copy_grant`), the state's `load_wanted` splitting the plan's fetch list at `copy_at` and sending the cache's reads through `fetch_page`, so the copy's go before prefetch's; `copy_read_done`: every answer frees its place in the cache, whatever became of the copy), never cached; planned as a landing page's are, once the update ends (`copy_load`, a `defer`: after the events it emitted reach their subscribers, so a host that can't write the copy cancels it at `Started` before a read is sent; with `live`, not while a scrollbar drag moves, once it rests; every turn and every end plans, so a place a copy frees is used), and a page landing during a drag wakes a waiting copy too. The cap is checked a row at a time, its early projection from a page's worth of rows; a copy keeps the cap it started with. A page the turn passed (taken from the cache while the copy's own read was out) is let go: its read cancelled, its landed page dropped (`drop_passed`); a copy that ends cancels its reads still out. `copy` and the keys start nothing once the table is closed (`copy_ranges`). The cancels (`end_copy`, `drop_copy`: its reads cancelled through the delegate, draining as the cache's do): a second copy, `set_columns`, `set_rows` shrinking under its last row (`cut_copy`), Escape (`escape_copy`, before `select`'s, the selection kept), `cancel_copy`, a read that fails (`Failed(Read)`, and `ReadFailed` with `read_failure`), and `close` (no event). Its tests (`src/state/copy/tests.rs`) drive the element in a window over a source whose reads answer at once or when a test says (`docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Features"). Its `Control` (tests only) is the random test's `copy` controls and `copy.json`'s, the latter through `Writer` (how a turn writes a value, counts bytes, ends the text, and the key declines, and the header's bytes in the check before a read; outside the tests a stand-in at the file's end, each method the rule itself, inlined, so the build's code is as without it).
- **`src/state/sort.rs`** (`sort`): sorting, a child of `state`, its state one field (`SortState`: the sort, the host's icons if all three loaded, the arrow pressed and hovered). `set_sort` refuses on a closed table, and a column past the end or not sortable itself (no event, the source not told; a debug assertion outside the tests), then asks the source (`TallTableDelegate::sort`), and on `Ok` `apply_sort`: a copy cancelled, `PageCache::forget_all` (every page forgotten, its reads cancelled through the delegate, draining as any cancelled read; skipped on a closed table), flashes cleared, hover and the jump's mark cleared, `sort_selection` (`select`: a `Rows` or `Cells` selection cleared, a `Columns` one kept with its rows at 0), the view at the top (direction still, the scroll settle dropped; the sideways scroll kept), `sort_lets_go` (`follow`: following let go unless every row fits, the tail drawn forgotten), `Sorted`, and the reads planned once the update ends (`sort_load`, a `defer` through `load_or_settle`). The arrows (`render_arrows`, a layer over the header's cells under `resize`'s bands): each sortable column wide enough for its padding, the gap, and the box (`arrow_room`; the header cell's right padding takes the room, so its name ends at the gap), the glyph (`ARROWS`) or the host's SVG in one fixed box (`box_width`: the widest glyph's advance, read with the font in `Glyphs`, at least the icon size, which with `density` scales with the text, as the icon drawn does), dim unless sorted or hovered (hover kept in the state, so a frame knows it); its target from 4 px left of the box to `resize`'s band, a press there stopping `select`'s and a release on it running the cycle (`click_sort`, `sort::next`; a refusal emits `SortRefused`), a release off it forgetting the press. `set_sort_icons` loads each path once through the asset source (in the tests, `SortState::assets`), the glyphs for all three if any fails. `set_columns` keeps the sort by the sorted column's name (`sorted_name`, `keep_sort`: cleared whatever the source answers, untold on a closed table) and forgets the arrow pressed and hovered, which `render_arrows` also forgets once their arrow isn't drawn. `room` is what `layout_columns` and `resize`'s fit add to a sortable name; `arrow_box` is each arrow's box, where `render_arrows` draws it and what the layout probe reports (`ColumnProbe::arrow`). Its tests-only `drawn` (each arrow, its color, its target) and `Control` (the cycle fixture's `descending-first`) are `src/state/fixtures/sort.rs`'s (`docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Features").
- **`src/state/pin.rs`** (`pin`): pinning, a child of `state`, its state one field (`PinState`: the host's count, the run in effect). `set_pinned` keeps the count (kept across `set_columns`); `repin` computes the run in effect (`pin::in_effect`) wherever the columns or the view's width change (`set_pinned`, `set_body`, every `lay_out`, which calls it, and `resize`'s release, which may change no width), held through a `resize` drag, and on a change clears the hover, bumps the layout generation, and emits `Pinned`; `place` is the run as drawn (`Pin`, its edge from the columns' widths, a drag's too, clamped to the cells' width). The drawing: `split_layer` and `regions` (a layer over the columns split at the run's edge into two clipped regions, the pinned one only while a column is pinned, each child placed by `Pin::layer_left`): the header's cells, `sort`'s arrows (a scrolled column's not drawn while its target is under the run), `resize`'s scrolled bands (a pinned column's band is clipped to the cells only, over them, so the last one's half past the edge draws and takes the press), and the body's cells, each region with its own `custom-cells` canvas (`pinned_quads`) and `select` canvas, over the one layer of row backgrounds; a failed row's message across both, in the cells layer; `edge_line`, the 1 px `border` line at the edge through the header (over the bands) and the body. Every site goes through the mappings: `Place` in `state.rs` is a stand-in with `Pin`'s methods without the feature, the unpinned math, so each site is written once and the base compiles as before; the base's paths keep their shape where a stand-in cost bytes (`visible_columns`, `cell`, the thumb). With `resize`, a pinned column's drag is clamped at the press's half (`Resizing::pin_limit`), and `in_effect` allows a run 0.001 px past the half (`SLACK`), so the widths summed again at the release can't unpin it by an ulp. The tooltip is placed beside a scrolled cell's part right of the run (`tooltip_cell`). Its `Control` (tests only) is `pin.json`'s negative controls and the window test's unclipped paint; `drawn` and `line` (tests only) are what the body drew (`docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Features").
- **`src/state/reorder.rs`** (`reorder`): the order, a child of `state`, one field (`order`: `order[place]`, the index drawn there; reset to the list's own by `set_columns`). `move_columns` and `move_columns_pinned` (`move_block`: `Move::moves` decides, then the order, `select`'s range through `Move::selection_after` and `replace_selection`, `pin`'s count, `lay_out` (no read; the scroll kept and clamped; `repin`'s `Pinned`), and `ColumnsMoved`); `set_order` (refused, a debug assertion outside the tests, unless `is_order`; nothing if unchanged; a `Columns` or `Cells` selection cleared, `select`'s `order_selection`; the count kept as places). `Glyphs::lay_out` lays the columns out in index order (the format's suffix measured there), then `column::in_order` puts each at its place and sums the lefts again. The drag (`Moving`, the state's `moving`, never a GPUI `Drag`): `press_move`, from the state's root before `select`'s press (a left press on a movable header, `header_place_at`; the bands and arrows stop theirs first; Shift, Cmd, Ctrl, and a double-click's second press never move; with `select`'s `Columns` kind on, only a press inside the selected `Columns` range, every column in it movable, and then `select`'s press doesn't run, the column selected alone at a release that never dragged); `move_drag_to` (past 2 px, `THRESHOLD`, it starts: the tooltip hidden, no hover meanwhile, `is_moving`; then `drag_x` finds the gap again, `gap_at` over `crate::reorder::gap`, a redraw only when the pointer's x or the gap moves); followed by the body's `canvas` handlers (a move with the button up is a release unseen, `release_unseen`: a drag cancelled, a press that never dragged the click it was), with a window-wide wheel (`move_wheel`: the base's wheel, then the gap again); `refresh_gap`, in render before the header, the gap found again at the pointer's x, so a host's `set_pinned` or the keys' sideways scroll moves the line without a pointer move; `end_move` (the left button's release only: `move_block` to the gap the last frame drew, with its side; nothing without one); `cancel_drag` (Escape, a new press, and `move_block`, `set_order`, and `set_columns`, whose places it would hold stale); Escape is `CancelMove`, bound in `init` (`crate::init` calls it last) in the base's context, so it goes first and propagates with no drag to `select`'s or the host's, a press that hasn't dragged dropped first so its release doesn't select again what Escape cleared. The drawing: `lifted` (the block's header text in `muted_foreground`), `render_preview` (the block's header names at their widths over the header, at the pointer less the press's offset, `table_head` at 0.9 opacity, a 1 px `border`), `render_gap_line` (2 px of `drag_border` at the gap's `line` through the header and the body, over the edge line), and the closed hand set in the body's `canvas` while `is_moving`; `sort`'s arrows track their hover meanwhile but draw none and ask no frame for it. Its `Control` (tests only, `reorder_control`) is `reorder.json`'s controls and the random test's `ReadByPlace`, which render's value read takes (the drawn text keeps its index); `move_drawn` (tests only, `Drawn`) is the preview and lines the last frame drew (`docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Features").
- **`src/state/density.rs`** (`density`): the state's density, one field (`DensityState`: what the host set, what's in effect, the window's rem size at the last render). `set_density` resolves at that rem and puts the sizes in effect (`apply_sizes`): a new text size leaves the glyphs stale (`font_size` 0), so render reads the font and lays the columns out once by it, never first by the old font's advances (with `pin`, the run would move and move back); any other change lays out at once; a new row height fits the viewport's rows at once (`fit_viewport`, `set_body`'s step, which keeps the top row, or while following the bottom), since the body's bounds move only once the header has drawn at the new row. `density_at` runs first in render: a new rem size resolves again through `apply_sizes`, so the frame is drawn and fitted by it. A loading cell's bar is 8 px within the row's insets (`Sizes::skeleton`). The state's root sets the text in effect (`text_size`); the tooltip keeps the theme's (gpui-kit's `Tooltip` sets `text_sm`). `density()` is what was set; the layout probe's `row_height` is what's drawn.
- **`src/state/fixtures.rs`** (tests only): the fixtures' native tests (`fixtures/README.md`). A child of `state`, so the paging scenarios drive the state's own scroll, drag, and paging paths: a `TallTableState` in GPUI's test app (gpui-kit's `test-support`, a dev-dependency), over a delegate that answers a read only when the scenario says, on GPUI's test clock (only `advance_clock` moves it, so the settle times are the table's own timers). Cell text goes through `shown_text`, what `render_body` draws. Its children's tests are in `src/state/fixtures/CLAUDE.md`. The test app's text system gives every character 0.6 em, 8.4 px at the cells' 14 px, which is IBM Plex Sans's digit advance too.

- **`src/state/random.rs`** (tests only): the random test of the paging under every feature built (`docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Fixtures"). Proptest driven by hand from a plain `#[test]` (gpui-kit's `property_test` doesn't compile at the pin): each case a scheduler seed (`run_test_once`), a drawn `Paging` and table, and up to 300 steps on a fresh `TestAppContext`, the element in a window (`Shell`). Its source (`Source` over `Books`) stamps each row with a version (`invalidate_rows`) and an era (rows cut by `set_rows`), snapshots them when a read is sent, answers in any order (with the page, failed, refused when sent, or at once), never reuses an id, and logs every read and cancel. With `sort`, a case draws which columns are sortable and which the source refuses (a bit a column), and an accepted sort bumps every row's era, so invariants 5 and 7 catch a row of the old order. With `pin`, up to eight `Pin` steps are placed among a case's steps from its scheduler seed (`with_pins`), never drawn, so every case's draw, and the controls' cases, are the ones without the feature; with `density`, up to eight `Density` steps (one of `DENSITIES`, the default among them) the same way, from a stream of their own (`with_densities`), so `pin`'s places are unchanged; with `reorder`, up to eight `MoveColumns` and `SetOrder` steps the same way, from a stream of their own (`with_moves`, after `let_go`'s), each a `u64` expanded when it runs (`expand_move`: a block, a `to`, a side; `shuffled`: a seeded order). The steps (`Step`, cfg'd by feature) are the reader's moves, the rows growing and shrinking, each feature's calls, answers, drains, frames, and `close` (a few steps on the closed table, then a drain and a new table over the source, as a host replacing one). The invariants (`Run::check`, numbered as the research's) are checked after every step against the source's books; 6 and 8 whenever no read is open and no drag moves. A failure shrinks and prints its steps with the scheduler seed; `SEED` picks the cases (0 unset), `PROPTEST_CASES` how many (16, 48 in the all-on run). A bug it found is an `#[ignore]`d scenario test there and an `Exclusion` (a step generated, a step run, or a pair checked) until it's fixed. Its `controls` (built with each control's feature, run only in the all-on run): each control (the cache's and `copy`'s `Control`s) must fail the cases at its invariant. Each read is judged as it went out (the reads open then, recorded by the source), so one answered when sent is checked too. With `reorder`, invariant 5 also reads back each cell the last frame drew (`drawn_text`: its text names its row and column, which must be its own row and column's index), and invariant 7 a copy's columns as the indices drawn at its places when it started; its control `ReadByPlace` fails at 5.

## Commands

```
SEED=<n> cargo test -p seiza-tall-table --features custom-cells,follow,resize,copy,sort,pin,density,format,reorder random_paging   # another set of the random test's cases (a failure prints its SEED)
just tall-table soak [seeds] [cases] # the random test soaked: `cases` (500) for each `SEED` of `seeds` (1 2 3 4), every feature on, release with debug assertions and overflow checks (its time: `docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Fixtures")
```
