# The table crate (`src/`)

The crate `seiza-tall-table`: the state entity, the element, the delegate, and each feature's pure half. Claude Code loads this file when a file under `src/` is read. The table's rules and the map of its folders are in `components/tall-table/CLAUDE.md`, the repo's in the root `CLAUDE.md`; both apply here. Paths are relative to `components/tall-table/`.

## Architecture

- **`src/state.rs`**: `TallTableState<D>`, the entity: the scroll model and page cache, hover and tooltip, the glyph measurements, and the drawing of the header and body. Every row height, padding, text size, and horizontal estimate is read through `sizes()` (the `Sizes` in effect; `padded` puts the cells' padding on a cell, the base's `px_2` without `density`), and the viewport's rows are fitted by `fit_viewport` (from `set_body`, and with `density` from `set_density`). Its public setters (`jump_to`, `set_rows`, `set_columns`, `retry_failed`, `close`, with `live` `invalidate_rows`, with `follow` `follow` and `let_go`, with `select` `select` and `set_selectable`, with `copy` `copy`, `copying`, `cancel_copy`, and `set_copy_options`, with `sort` `set_sort` and `set_sort_icons`, and with `reorder` `move_columns`, `move_columns_pinned` (with `pin`), `set_order`, and `order`; with `live`, its and `set_rows`' reads are planned once at the end of the update that made them, `load_after_update`; `close` runs once, and a closed table's `set_rows` leaves the cache as it was) and readers; with `live`, a re-read whose load is ready when sent lands in that turn (`answered`), not on a spawned task; `TallTableEvent`, `TallTableStats`, `Paging` (1024-row pages, prefetch 2, 64 MiB, 3 in flight, a 250 ms scroll settle, an 80 ms drag settle), `ActivityOptions`; the keys (`init`, `actions!` under `seiza_tall_table`).
- **Places and indices** (`reorder`): a column's place is where it's drawn, its index its place in `set_columns`' list. `column_info` is in index order, `columns` (laid out) in drawn order, each `LaidOutColumn` holding its index (`LaidOutColumn::index(place)`: the place itself without the feature, inlined, so each site is written once and the base compiles as before). Everything about the screen goes by place (`select`'s ranges and keys, `pin`'s count, the hit tests, the hover, `CellProbe::column`); every site that crosses to the data or a column's settings goes through its index (a page's `value`, `paint`, and `decimals` in the cells, the tooltip, and `resize`'s fit; `live`'s flashes; `column_info`'s settings: `resizable`, `sortable`, the paint's ramps; `sort`'s column; `copy`'s columns, taken at its start; `ColumnWidthsChanged` and `column_width`; `resize`'s drag and `sort`'s pressed and hovered arrow, held by index so a move meanwhile leaves them on their column) (`docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Features").
- **`src/reorder.rs`** (`reorder`): the move's rules, pure (`fixtures/reorder.json`'s subject): `Move` (a block of places and `to`): `fits`, `place_after`, `order_after`, `count_after` (`pin`'s count: *r*, the run's columns outside the block; below it pinned, above scrolled, at it the side given or the first column's; a change by what crossed, never a recount; `None` where the side given isn't `to`'s), `moves` (the decision, with `pin`), `selection_after` (`select`: `Rows` kept, a range mapped column by column and kept while contiguous, its direction kept); `is_order`; and the drag's `gap` over a `View` (the laid-out columns, the cells' width, the sideways scroll, the run in effect and its edge) and the host's count: the pointer's region (the run, left of its edge, or the scrolled columns), the gap after its last visible column whose center is left of the pointer, none inside or beside the block but the run's end across the edge from a block wholly on one side, the side passed on (the pointer's where `to` is the run's end by the count, else `to`'s, so a narrow view's capped run never refuses a gap drawn), and a gap only where `moves` moves something; `Gap::line`, the line's left clamped inside the pointer's region. `count_after` and `moves` also build in the tests without `pin`, so the fixture runs in every `reorder` build.
- **`src/cells_text.rs`**: the loaded cells' text, drawn by one element a region (`cells_text`, a `canvas`: without `pin` the cells layer's, with it the pinned region's and the scrolled one's, each region's last child, over `custom-cells`' canvas, `select`'s, and a loading row's bars), every glyph painted inside one `paint_layer`: no `div` a cell, so no layout for them and one layer a region (`docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Features"). `render_body` collects a `CellText` per loaded cell in the row loop (its left in its region, top, width, alignment, cut, `shown_text`, color); the paint reads the font and size from the inherited text style (as a `div`'s text did), snaps each cell's left, top, and width, the row, and the padding to device pixels on their own (midpoints toward zero, as taffy took the `div`s' lengths), cuts the text only when its shaped width passes the content box (`LineWrapper::truncate_line` with "…": at the end for text and floats, in the middle for integers and decimals), aligns it (numbers right) at the padding, centers it in the row as `paint_line` does, clips it to its cell (a content mask), and walks the glyphs as `paint_line` does (`paint_emoji` for an emoji), so its pixels are the `div`s'. Its tests-only `DrawnText` (each cell's text after the cut, place, color, box, on the state's `drawn_text`) is `src/state/cells_text_tests.rs`'s.
- **`src/density.rs`** (`density`): `Density` (the host's row height and text size, the base's by default) and `Sizes`, the lengths in effect, which every site reads: `resolve` (the clamp, where the rem size is known: the text at least 6 px, the row at least the text; the scale, the text in effect over the default's in its own unit, a `px` text over 14 px and a `rems` text over 0.875 rem), `row`, `text`, `scaled` (a length the base fixes for 14 px text, rounded to whole px; at a scale of 1 the base's own, unrounded, since its name and gutter estimates have half pixels), `pad` and `padding` (8 px a side, scaled), `estimate` (a header name's or the gutter's, rounded once on the total), `least` (a set width's, 40 px scaled), `set` (a set width, never scaled), `inset` (`custom-cells`' bar, a fifth of the row). Without the feature, `column.rs`'s `Sizes` is a stand-in with the same methods returning the base's constants (as `Place` is `pin`'s), so each site is written once and the base compiles as before. Its `Control` (tests only) is `density.json`'s negative controls.
- **`src/pin.rs`** (`pin`): the geometry, pure (tested natively, with a property test of the mappings): `in_effect` (the count clamped and cut at half the cells' width), and `Pin`, the run in effect: `left` (a column's screen left), `layer_left` (in its region), `content_x` (a screen x's content x), `visible`, `into_view` (`select`'s keys, right of the edge; the least move itself is `scroll::into_view`, which the stand-in shares), `track` (the horizontal scrollbar's, from the edge).
- **`src/sort.rs`** (`sort`): `Sort`, `SortDirection` (`first` by kind: a text column ascending, every other descending), `SortRefused`, `SortIcons`, and the cycle (`next`).
- **`src/copy.rs`** (`copy`): `CopyId`, `CopyOptions`, `TallTableCopy`, `CopyFailure`, `CopyProgress`; the text: `push_value` (a value's exact text, quoted when it holds a tab, a line break, or a `"`; NULL empty), `header_line`, `least_bytes`, `TURN_CELLS`.
- **`src/selection.rs`** (`select`): `TallTableSelection` (`Rows`, `Columns`, `Cells`, each an anchor and a head cell; `rows`, `columns`, `contains`), `Cell`, `Selectable` (the kinds on, `smallest`), and `Kind`.
- **`src/tall_table.rs`**: `TallTable<D>`, the `RenderOnce` element a host renders: focus, key context and actions, the press that focuses it, and the border (the focus ring when focused), around the state's view. With `select`, its key context is `TallTable TallTableSelect` while a kind is on, and the selection's actions (`select::on_actions`, with `copy` its `CopySelection` too). The same split as gpui-kit's `DataTable` and `TableState`.
- **`src/delegate.rs`**: `TallTableDelegate` (`load_page`, `cancel`, with `sort` `sort`, refusing every sort by default and accepting none), `TallTablePage` (`value`, `heap_bytes`, with `custom-cells` `paint`, and with `format` `decimals`, a cell's own count, `None` by default), `CellValue`, `PageError`, `PageLoad`.
- **`src/paint.rs`** (`custom-cells`): its bar's padding and inset from the `Sizes` in effect (`draw`); `CellPaint`, `Ramp`, `Bar` (`#[non_exhaustive]`: built through `Bar::new` and its setters), `Stop`, `Domain` (a column's range → 0..1); a column's ramps resolved from the theme once a frame (`ColumnPaint`), mixed in Oklab, premultiplied, as CSS mixes (`Oklab`); what every cell reads beyond its column's, once a frame (`FramePaint`: the two text colors a filled cell picks from by Oklab lightness, and per kind of row a `RowPaint`, what's drawn under a fill and the jumped-to row's tint over it); a cell's fill and bar as quads and its text color (`draw`). Its `Control` (tests only) is the paint fixture's negative controls. `render_body` collects the quads in the row loop and paints them from one `canvas`, the cells layer's first child (`docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Features").
- **`src/flash.rs`** (`live` with `custom-cells`): what a change flashes at (`change`: a rise, a fall, any other change; two NaNs equal), its fade (`opacity`), and `Flashes`, the live flashes by (row, column) on GPUI's clock (`BackgroundExecutor::now`, read through a closure: its `Instant` isn't public). The state compares a landing re-read with the page it replaces (`note_flashes`, before the cache swaps them, over the rows `PageCache::changes` names), draws each flash as a quad over the fill in `custom-cells`' pass, asks for the next frame while one is on screen (`Window::request_animation_frame`, in render), and under `App::reduce_motion` holds it and clears it from a timer (`after_flashes`). Its `Control` (tests only) is the flash fixture's negative controls.
- **`src/column.rs`**: `TallTableColumn` (with `custom-cells`, its `fill`, `bar`, and `text_ramp`; with `live` too, its `flash`; with `resize` or `density`, its `width`, its set width kept on it; with `resize`, its `min_width` (`None` for the table's least, `Sizes::least`), `max_width`, and `resizable`; with `sort`, its `sortable`, off unless set; with `format`, its `format`, kept on the laid-out column for a numeric kind, `Glyphs::lay_out`; with `reorder`, its `movable`, on unless set) and `ColumnKind`, and the layout (`layout_columns`, at a `Sizes`): widths by kind, at least the header's width (with `sort`, a sortable header's arrow and gap added to its name's estimate), an integer column fitting its widest value at the font's widest number advance (`Glyphs`, read once per font and size, `digits_width`); with `resize` or `density`, a set width in place of all that, clamped (a kind's width never is; with `density` alone, to the table's least and 1200 px). A float's budget follows its width. With `reorder`, `in_order` puts the laid-out columns in an order, each its index set. With `density`, the kinds' widths, the padding, and the name's estimate follow the `Sizes` in effect; without it, `Sizes` is the base's stand-in.
- **`src/pages.rs`**: `PageCache`, pure bookkeeping (tested natively): which pages are loaded, in flight, draining (cancelled but still answering, counted against `max_in_flight`), failed; what to fetch (visible pages first: no prefetch starts while a visible page is loading), cancel, and evict (LRU past the budget); a shrink forgets every page wholly past the new end (`set_rows` returns their reads to cancel). With `live`, a loaded page's re-read state (`Live`: stale, its read in flight with the generation it started at, a failed re-read, a landed one not yet drawn, the end of the rows the drawn page and the read in flight hold), a short last page that `set_rows` grows kept stale (`row` answers `Pending` past the rows it holds), each invalidation's generation, and `take_refreshed` for render; with `custom-cells` too, each page's rows invalidated since its drawn read started (`RowSet`s: before the re-read in flight, and during it), while a column flashes (`track_changes`, `changes`); the order re-reads go in and their `max_in_flight − 1` limit are `plan`'s (`docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Features"). With `copy`, a copy's reads in flight by page and request (never cached), counted against `max_in_flight` and, with `live`'s re-reads, against `background_limit` (`(max_in_flight − 1).max(1)`); `plan` grants the pages the copy asked for (`set_copy_wants`) after the visible pages and their re-reads, none while a visible page has nothing to show, before prefetch (`Plan::copy`, `copy_at`), and prefetch (a first read or a re-read) skips a page the copy reads or was just granted; a cancelled one drains as the cache's do (`copy_cancelled`), and any answer frees its place (`copy_answered`); `holding` is a loaded page with the rows a copy wants (with `live`, as drawn), `loading` a read of it the cache has out (short of the copy's rows or not: the copy waits for it). With `live`, a visible grown page whose re-read failed doesn't hold prefetch or a copy back, as a failed page doesn't. With `follow`, `loaded_through`: where the run of rows drawn with their values from a row on ends. With `sort`, `forget_all`: every page forgotten as a shrink forgets the pages past its end, its reads returned in page order to cancel. Its `Control` (tests only, in every build) is the paging fixture's `cache`, `live`, and `sort` negative controls (`keep-past-end`, `drop-grown`, `keep-pages` among them) and seven of the random test's (with `sort`, `SortKeepsPages` and `SortKeepsReads`); its test-only readers (`loaded_slots`, `reread_failed`, `budget_bytes`, and `sent_as`, every read started and what for) are the random test's.
- **`src/scroll.rs`**: `RowScroll`, the f64 row position, and the scrollbar thumb math (tested natively).
- **`src/format.rs`**: `fit_float`, rounding a float to its column like a spreadsheet's automatic format (tested natively); with `format`, its `number` module: `NumberFormat` (decimals, `Sign`, grouping, a suffix, its line breaks shown as `¶`), `Advances` (each of `FORMAT_CHARS`' advance, read once per font and size in `Glyphs`, beside `number_px`; a text's width is their sum), and `ColumnFormat`, a laid-out column's format, whether its column is a float's, and its suffix shaped once after a digit, less the digit, so its kerning with the number counts (`Glyphs::read`; `set_columns` leaves the glyphs to be read again when it brings a suffix they lack, `lacks_suffix`): `measure` (the text and its width: in a float column a float at `{:.N}` of its absolute value or its exact text, grouped while plain, the sign by the raw value, none on an exact zero; in an integer or decimal column its text grouped and signed, its digits as written; `None` for NULL, NaN, an infinity, a value of another kind than its column's, or a text that isn't a plain number), `fit` (the text if its width is at most the content box less a pixel; a float at fixed decimals whose whole digits alone can't fit isn't written out), and `rounded` (the tooltip's alone: a fitted text's number reads back, in the value's own width, as another value). `push_grouped` (every build) is the grouping loop `format_count` shares. `Glyphs::fit_format` is a cell's fit, its page's decimals asked of a float column only (`page_decimals`); `render_body` draws a fitted text whole (`CellText::whole`: the element skips its cut) and any other cell as `shown_text`; the tooltip opens for a fitted text only where `rounded`, and for a fallback as the base's; `resize`'s fit measures the formatted text even where it falls back. Its `Control` (tests only) is `cells.json`'s `format` controls; its test `plex_sans_shapes_formats_at_their_advances` shapes IBM Plex Sans with cosmic-text (the web's shaper, a dev-dependency at gpui-pre-wgpu's locked version): the pairs a format writes at their advances' sum, but `e-`, 0.01 em wider, inside the rule's pixel (`docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Features").
- **`src/activity.rs`**: the frames drawn over the host's window, for rows/s and filled frames.
- **`src/probe.rs`**: `TallTableProbe` (with `select`, its `selection`; with `sort`, its `sort`), `TallTableLayout` (with `pin`, its `pinned`, the run in effect), `ColumnProbe` (with `sort`, its `arrow`: the box, from the column's left; with `reorder`, its `index`, the layout's columns in drawn order), `CellProbe` (with `custom-cells`, its `PaintProbe`), and with `format` the layout's `advances` (each character a format writes, its advance at the text in effect: `Glyphs`' own, which the fit sums): what scripts read.

**Cells:** a loaded cell's text is drawn by `cells_text` (above), never an element a cell; a change to how it's drawn keeps the pixels, checked against the commit before it (`just tall-table smoke --screens`). Text ends in "…", its line breaks shown as `¶` (`cell_text`; `shape_line` debug-asserts there's no `\n`). One tooltip, for the hovered cell (`hover_at`, mapped from the scroll model, not per-cell hitboxes), shows a rounded, cut, or one-lined value's exact text once the pointer has rested 500 ms and its page is loaded (`decide_tooltip`, in render), at once within 300 ms of the last one going, drawn with gpui-kit's `Tooltip` and gpui-base's `TooltipPositioner` in a `deferred` (Root's tooltip overlay is crate-private).

## GPUI Kit gotchas (check before debugging)

- **`use gpui_kit::*` brings gpui-kit's `test` attribute**, which shadows `#[test]` ("recursion limit reached while expanding `#[test]`"). A test module imports what it needs by name, not `super::*`.
- **A `fn` pointer compared with `==` warns** (`unpredictable_function_pointer_comparisons`), in a derived `PartialEq` too: `Bar` compares its `Stop` with `std::ptr::fn_addr_eq`. A `Vec` of them (`Ramp`) doesn't warn.
- **A key context only counts on the focused element's path.** Key dispatch walks from the focused element up, so a context on a child of the element that tracks focus never matches: `select`'s context is the element's own (`TallTable TallTableSelect`). Bindings matching at the same depth go latest-bound first, so `select::init` runs after the base's bindings and its keys win while a kind is on.
- **An action that calls `cx.propagate()` hands its key to the next binding that matches,** in GPUI's dispatch order (deeper first, then latest-bound first at a depth): `reorder`'s `CancelMove` is bound to Escape last, so it sees Escape first, and with no drag it propagates to `select`'s `ClearSelection` and then the host's. A binding that only sometimes wants a key doesn't need a key context switched on and off.
- **A scrollbar release can arrive before the next paint.** The drag's mouse-move and mouse-up handlers are registered every frame in the body's `canvas`, not only once a drag starts: a quick click on the track missed its release in WebKit, and the next pointer move scrolled the table.
