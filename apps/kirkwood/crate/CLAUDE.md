# The crate (`crate/`)

The `kirkwood` crate: the engine (the rows, the clock, the flight, the ride, the integration, a gap, a find, a group, the table, the live columns) and the interface (`host.rs`), with what passes to and from the scene (`scene.rs`, `web.rs`). Claude Code loads this file when a file under `crate/` is read. Kirkwood's rules and the map of its folders are in `apps/kirkwood/CLAUDE.md`, the repo's in the root `CLAUDE.md`; both apply here. Paths are relative to `apps/kirkwood/`.

## Architecture

- **The engine:** Rust owns the orbital elements and their bytes (the scene's attributes are GL buffers of its own, filled from views over each block of rows as it arrives), the clock and how far it goes, Jupiter's orbit (it isn't a row), the color ramp (`crate/src/ramp.rs`: the shader's table and the legend's, one), the sort flight's progress and the axes it lands the dots on (`flight.rs`, `layout.rs`), how far the frame has turned to Jupiter's, the three points in step with it, and the two groups that hold there (`ride.rs`), those two groups' integration under the Sun and Jupiter (`integrate.rs`: the scene is handed their places when the clock moves), a picked gap's marker (`gap.rs`: its orbit, its place, its path in the turning frame, the card's numbers), the row found (`find.rs`: the table's selected row, its name, whether it has a dot), and a drawn group (`group.rs`: the lasso's mode, the shape, and the set of rows under it, picked through the scene's camera a frame's worth at a time; where its dots were in the window, for the camera's flight in; its biggest bodies, named; and a group of one row, for a dot clicked). The scene asks Rust each frame (the clock, the flight, the ride, a picked gap, the panel's width, where the page's text lies, Jupiter's place); Rust calls the scene to hand it a wheel, a press, a drag (its start, its moves, its end), a double click, an arrow or a zoom key, and the way home, each for the camera of a frame (the fixed one, or the ride's own), and a shape's points as they're drawn; and it asks the scene for its camera at a shape's release. A found row it never calls for: the scene asks which row and where it is each frame, and flies its own camera; nor a group: the scene asks each frame whether it changed, and then for its bytes, its box, and its named bodies. A click is the other way round: the crate asks the scene which row's dot is drawn nearest a window position.
- **The rows:** held in Rust, as the bytes of the two files, each written into the wasm's memory as its response arrives and read where it lies (`crate/src/bytes.rs`: 32-bit words, so no `unsafe` and no copy); the table's delegate answers every page from them, through an index of file rows when the table is a drawn group's or sorted (`table.rs`, `View`). Two of the table's 20 columns aren't the files': the live ones, worked out when a cell is drawn (`live.rs`). No DuckDB, and no `engines/duckdb/`. Why: `docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Data" and "Table".

## Gotchas

- **The clock is moved by whoever reads it** (the scene every frame, the date four times a second), and the scene draws when the years it's given are new, so it draws nothing while time is paused, held, or stopped at its last day.
- **The sort flight is moved the same way** (`flight.rs`: the scene every frame, the interface while the dots fly), and it is one number: the shader places each dot by it, so nothing is uploaded and no buffer rewritten. Landed, the scene draws nothing, whatever the clock does: time moves no dot on the axes.
- **The ride moves the same way as the flight** (`ride.rs`, both over `tween.rs`): the scene every frame, the interface while the camera flies. A reading more than half a second after the last is a stall, and moves it a tenth of a second.
- **One action is pressed at a time** (`host.rs`, `toggle_sort`, `toggle_ride`): the press on one lets the other go in the same call, and both move at once. A new action joins the same rule. A picked gap isn't one: it's part of the ride (`pick_gap`), and "Ride with Jupiter" reads pressed with it.
- **A gap is picked only in the frame that turns** (`gap.rs`, `advance`): any reading with the ride at rest in the fixed frame lets it go, so the ride's press back and the sort's press need say nothing to it, and the marker leaves with the ride's own light (`gap::lit`). While riding, two presses let it go (`let_go`, 0.4 s): the card's picked chip (`pick_gap` with `in_card`; never the landed layout's chip, which is still under the pointer as it fades), and "Ride with Jupiter" pressed on again before the camera is back (`toggle_ride`).
- **The marker's orbit turns Jupiter's own mean motion times the ratio, never 1 / a^1.5** (`gap.rs`, `orbit_with`): a part in 2,000 off and its path never closes. The card's periods are from the same number; only the orbit's size is from Jupiter's a.
- **With a gap picked the ride's own additions leave** (the two groups' names, the triangle, their light: `scene.ts`'s `groups`, the shader's `uMarked`), and the title's sentence changes with them (`host.rs`, `GAP_LINE`): change one with the others. One gives its place to the other, never both at once: the ride's additions are gone by half of `gap::shown` (`groups_gone`), and the marker, its dim, and its card come in over the other half (`lit`), as the title's two sentences do (`host.rs`, `shows`).
- **The card starts under the title's lines, as the e axis does** (`host.rs`, `render_card`, `CARD_UNDER_TITLE`): text added to the title moves both. `see.ts` holds the card to 20 px under.
- **The sky leaves by a number of its own** (`scene_sky`: the flight's `space` times the ride's `sky`), and the title's sentence about the stars with it; riding, that sentence's place says what the turned frame brightens (`host.rs`, `RIDE_LINE`). Change the groups' light (`scene.ts`, `RIDE_LIGHT`) and it together.
- **A row's integrated place is the same however the clock came to it** (steps on a grid from the epoch, only ever taken away from it: a clock that moves toward the epoch starts the rows over from there; between two steps, the two-body orbit from the last). Keep it so: nothing may step by a frame's own time, and no step is undone to save a few (a close pass of Jupiter doesn't undo to the same float). `place_of` integrates one row alone from the epoch, and `see.ts` holds the scene's rows to it; it answers by the row's group, whatever the scene is drawing (`ellipse_of` is where a row is drawn with `?integrate=0`, or while the rows catch up).
- **After a jump of the clock the two groups are on their ellipses for some frames** (`web.rs`, `INTEGRATE_MS` of steps a frame; `stats.catchingUp`). A check on a date far from the epoch waits for it (`see.ts`, `withBelt`). Landed on the axes the rows are stepped and no place is worked out.
- **The two long studies in `integrate.rs` run only with `KIRKWOOD_STUDY` set** (a step's length weighed, which Hildas the resonance holds): `KIRKWOOD_STUDY=1 cargo test -p kirkwood --release <name> -- --include-ignored --nocapture`.
- **The camera is a hand's only in space, at rest in one of two frames,** and the interface decides which from the crate's own state, never the scene from its last frame (`host.rs`, `hands_frame`): the fixed frame's camera, or, once the frame turns with Jupiter, the ride's own (`scene::Frame`, handed over with every wheel, drag, and double click). On the way between the two, and with the dots out of space, an input moves nothing. The crate's state can be a frame ahead of the scene's picture (its own reading moves the ride on), so the scene takes an input only if the camera it last drew is at rest in that frame (`scene.ts`, `handOf`). Any button's release ends a drag.
- **A double click is two plain presses the surface itself took, in one frame, and its second starts no drag** (`host.rs`; `scene.rs`, `surface_press`): the click count alone also counts a first click on a chip that then leaves from under the pointer, or one made while the camera flew.
- **The compass is a view of its own** (`host.rs`, `Compass`), as the date is: a turn redraws it alone, and the interface is drawn again only for its first showing and the hint's fold.
- **The corner is drawn only in space, once the scene is up** (`host.rs`, `corner_shown`): landed, its place is the count of rows in view's, and the keys do nothing. It fades with `flight::space`, takes no input from the sort's press on (`in_space`), and that press shuts an open list. Something new at the bottom right of the landed layout shares that place.
- **The hint folds for good on the camera's first move by a hand, or when the controls' list first opens** (`host.rs`, `fold_hint`; the page keeps it in `localStorage`, key `kirkwood:hint`). The flight home and a flight of the scene's own don't fold it. Where what the panel leaves is narrower than `HINT_ROOM` it is drawn as its button without being folded: a longer row of actions needs a wider `HINT_ROOM` (1215 with four actions).
- **The controls' list keeps clear of the table's handle** (`host.rs`, `handle_left`, measured when the handle is laid out): it is as tall as the handle is high up. A row added to it makes it taller; it must still fit under the title in a window 900 px high.
- **The keys are the interface's root's, by its focus handle** (`host.rs`, `key`): it takes the focus whenever nothing has it and the panel is shut, and hands it back when the panel shuts. With the panel open every key is left alone, so the table's reach the table. A key with Ctrl, Alt, or the platform's key held is the browser's.
- **A found row is the table's selected row, and nothing else's** (`host.rs`, `selected`, `find`, `lose`): the interface finds the selected row at each `Selected` of one row (a range of more finds nothing new), and lets go by clearing the table's selection. Anything new that finds a row selects it in the table (`jump_to`, as "Find Ceres" does); anything that lets one go calls `lose`.
- **A find is the third action, one pressed at a time** (`host.rs`, `find`, `toggle_sort`, `toggle_ride`, `pick_gap`): a find lets the sort and the ride go, and each of them (a gap too) lets the found row go. "Find Ceres" reads pressed while the first row is the row marked, whatever found it.
- **A group is the fourth action** (`host.rs`, `toggle_lasso`, `drop_group`): "Draw a group" reads pressed in the lasso's mode, while a release waits for its rows, and while a drawn group is held, and pressed then it lets all of that go (a dot clicked isn't a group drawn: pressed then, its row is let go and the mode begins). Its press lets the sort, the ride, and a found row go; the sort, the ride, and a gap's chip let the group and the mode go; a find leaves the mode but keeps a group (the row found is one of its rows), and "Find Ceres" lets go of a group Ceres isn't in. Anything new that changes the group calls `refilter`.
- **Time is paused from the mode's press until the group is let go** (`clock.rs`, `pause_for_group`, the sort's own pause): the release gives nothing back. Run pressed in the mode leaves it; pressed with a group held it runs, and the group stays lit (`docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Scene").
- **The pick is the crate's, a few milliseconds a frame, and the scene's tick drives it** (`web.rs`, `scene_pick`, `PICK_MS`; `group.rs`, `step`): every row is placed from the mode's press on, while the shape is drawn, and a closed shape's rows are tested once they're all in. It leans on time being paused: `step` begins again if the clock's years moved. The places are 19 MB, held only in the mode. Don't call `scene_pick` from a call the crate made: a group picked updates the interface.
- **A row is in the group by the pixel its place falls in** (`group.rs`, `Mask`, `Camera::window`): its place held in 32-bit floats, through the scene's own matrix, drawn only in front of the camera and between its planes, into a pixel whose center is inside the shape by the even-odd rule. `see.ts`'s `rowsInside` is that rule written again: change them together. A Hilda or a Trojan is where the integration has it (`scene_pick`'s `apart`).
- **A shape is drawn only through the fixed frame's camera at rest, and the camera holds still under it** (`host.rs`): from its press until its rows are in, a wheel and the keys move nothing. Its points are kept to what the panel leaves (`in_scene`).
- **The way back flies only when the crate says it was the way back** (`scene.rs`, `back`, `backs`: "Show all rows", "The whole belt", "Draw a group" pressed back, all `host.rs`'s `show_all`). A group let go by another action (`drop_group`, `let_group_go`) leaves the camera where it is. Anything new that lets a group go picks one of the two.
- **The named bodies are the interface's to choose and the scene's to draw** (`host.rs`, `describe_group`, with the header's classes, one pass over the group's rows; `group::name`; `scene.ts`, `body`): set inside `scene_pick`'s call, before the scene asks. Their labels sit before the group's chip and the found row's name in `scene.labels()`, which checks read from the end.
- **A dot clicked is a group with no shape** (`group::pick_one`, `group::clicked`, `group::drawn`): the gray and the table's one row are a group's, the mark and the flight a found row's (its row is selected with `jump_to`). `host.rs` asks `drawn()` wherever a shape is meant ("Draw a group" pressed, the header's words), and `held()` wherever any group is.
- **The panel is glass in two places that must agree on when** (`host.rs`, `GLASS`: the panel's fill, and through `web.rs` the table's clear fills; `scene.ts`, `glass`: the page's element that blurs the scene behind it): both are on unless `?glass=1`. Anything new drawn in the panel sits over a blurred scene at a fifth of the fill: give text there the foreground or the muted color, never something dimmer, and a fill of its own if it needs one (the table's head has one).
- **A row of the table isn't a row of the file** once the table is a group's or sorted: the interface asks the view (`table.rs`, `View::file_row`, `View::at`) at every `Selected` and before every `jump_to`. A filter is `set_rows` and then the table's own sort set again, which forgets every page (`host.rs`, `refilter`; the tall table's `live` isn't built here).
- **Only the files' number columns sort** (`table.rs`, `SORTABLE`, `sortable`, the one rule the delegate and the checks' hook ask): a key each with nothing to build, the order a radix sort of the rows that have a number, made in the delegate's `sort`, in the task of the press (a frame or two for every row: `docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Table"). Its keys order as integers the way the floats do; a row with none never gets a key. The text columns and the live ones have no arrow.
- **A column of the table isn't a column of the files** (`table.rs`, `shown`): the live columns stand after the full name, so every column after it is two on from its place in `catalog::column`. Anything handed a column by the table (a sort, a page's cell, the checks' hooks) asks `shown`; `see.ts` names the table's columns (`DIAMETER`, `LIVE`), and its `kirkwood(page, …)` closures can't see them, so they hold the numbers themselves.
- **Every live cell of a frame is of one instant, and only the interface moves it** (`live.rs`, `show`; `host.rs`, `refresh_live`, twice a second while the panel is in sight, and at the panel's opening): a cell is worked out when it's drawn, from that instant, never from the clock as it is now, so a scroll or a hover between two refreshes changes no number. A new live column is a case in `live::Live` and its number in `live::distance`; it needs no page read again. Rule 5 is the rate's and the decimals': `docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Table".
- **A live cell of a Hilda or a Trojan is its integrated place's,** moved from the epoch alone and kept for the instant shown, as every drawn row's place is (`live.rs`, `PLACES`), whatever the scene is drawing: with `?integrate=0`, or while the rows catch up after a jump of the clock, the cell and the dot differ.
- **The live columns have set widths** (`table.rs`, `live_width`; the table's `density` is on for that alone): both must end inside the panel as it opens, which `see.ts` holds. A third live column, or a wider panel's first columns, is weighed against that.
- **Over the glass the table's head keeps a fill** (`web.rs`, `GLASS_HEAD`, set on the theme at the start): the table draws its columns' names dimmer than its cells, and with a clear head they can't be read over dust in full color.
- **A found row with no ellipse has no dot** (`find::marked` is nothing): no mark, no flight, the sort and the ride left as they were, and the panel says why (`host.rs`, `no_dot_line`).
- **A drag is the scene's from its press on the surface to its release, wherever that is** (`scene.rs`, `DRAG`; `host.rs`, `drag_listeners`: the window's own listeners, registered every frame). A new element over the scene needs nothing for it: occluding, it takes its own press, and a drag begun on the scene crosses it.
- **The press pauses time, and the press back gives it back** only if the time control wasn't pressed between (`clock.rs`, `pause_for_sort`, `resume_after_sort`; `docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Scene").
- **The axes are the crate's** (`layout.rs`): the shader is handed their ends and where they are in the window, already in clip space (`Plot::clip`, which the crate's test of the flight takes too), and anything drawn on them (GPUI's furniture) asks the same `Plot`. Never type an axis's end or a gap's place in `scene.ts` or `host.rs`. A row is in view by a comparison in 32-bit floats, the shader's own, so the rows counted are the rows landed.
- **The axes' furniture is GPUI's, on the crate's plot** (`host.rs`, `render_furniture`; `layout.rs`'s ticks, chips, and groups): every place asks the `Plot` the shader lands the dots on, with the panel's width in it. It takes no input and doesn't occlude, so a press or a wheel on it reaches the surface under it; but the chips, which are buttons and occlude. `Plot::chips` keeps them apart and inside the plot, and gives none in a plot too narrow for four in a row: the line over them and their drops go with them. Its opacity is the flight's (`flight::axes`, `flight::landed`): `docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Interface".
- **The e axis starts under the title's lines** where they end below the plot's top (`host.rs` reads the bounds the title measured for the scene's quiet), and the title asks for one more frame when those bounds move while the axes show. Text added to the title moves the axis's start; `see.ts` holds it to 8 px under the title.
- **What's said of the landed dots needs a belt:** the chips, the names, the line over the chips, and the count are drawn only once the scene has drawn rows; the axes alone stand without (`docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Interface").
- **A published target goes beside a bordered box, not in it:** an absolute child fills the box inside its border, a pixel short all round (`host.rs`, a gap's chip, the card).
- **A wheel is the scene's only in space:** the interface hands none over while the dots are headed for the axes, on them, or on their way back (`host.rs`), and the way back from a sort is never turned down, whatever failed since the press.
- **A fading run of text is a highlight's `fade_out`,** not a clear color: a highlight's color is blended over the text's own (`host.rs`, the line about the stars).
- **GPUI's canvas is transparent because of two things:** `web/src/alpha.ts` (the WebGPU context's alpha mode, set before the wasm starts) and the theme's `tokens.background` set transparent after `Theme::change` (`web.rs`). It leans on GPUI clearing to transparent and blending source-over, which it doesn't promise.
- **GPUI's text is set to grayscale at the start** (`web.rs`): subpixel text, which a real GPU draws and headless Chromium can't, leaves the canvas's alpha at 0 over the clear window and is composited away. No check sees it: after a change to how the window is made clear, look at the title and the axes' numbers in a real browser (`docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Table").
- **The transparent `tokens.background` is read by more than the window's fill.** The tall table takes it as the text color over a cell's fill (`components/tall-table/src/paint.rs`, `text_over`), and gpui-kit components that paint with it (inputs, tooltips, outline buttons) would be see-through over the scene. Nothing here uses either yet: before the table gets a cell fill or a ramp, or such a component is added, give it a color of its own.
- **An element over the scene must `occlude()`,** or a wheel on it also zooms the scene (`docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Input").
- **The scene quiets its picture where the title's lines lie** (`host.rs` measures them, `scene_scrim`). Text added over the scene elsewhere has no quiet under it: give it a fill, or widen what's measured.
- **A published target is placed from its parent's corner** (`host.rs`'s `target`: `top_0`, `left_0`): an absolute child left at its static place starts inside the parent's padding, and its bounds come out shifted.
- **The table has the keys from the moment its panel opens** (the host focuses it); a press elsewhere takes them away, as in any GPUI view.
- **The click's wait is in the scene's gotcha** (`web/CLAUDE.md`, "A click's row is read off a pass of the scene's own"): `CLICK_WAITS` and `undo_click` stand against the platform's own double click.

## Commands

From the repo root.

```
just kirkwood test     # native tests: the clock and its reach, the files' rules, Jupiter against data/horizons.json, the integration's steps; and, on the real files, the readers against data/fixture.json, five rows against Horizons, the integration against Horizons and the Trojans in their clouds across the clock's reach, the shader's 32-bit arithmetic against the 64-bit place for every row, and its flight against the place on the axes for every row in view (`#[ignore]`d in a plain `cargo test`; here they run, about two minutes, and fail without data/elements.bin and data/rows.bin)
```
