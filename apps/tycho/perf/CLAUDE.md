# Tycho's checks (`perf/`)

The checks that stay, the one done by hand, the probes the crate publishes, and `results/`. Claude Code loads this file when a file under `perf/` is read. Tycho's rules and the map of its folders are in `apps/tycho/CLAUDE.md`, the repo's in the root `CLAUDE.md`; both apply here. Paths are relative to `apps/tycho/`.

Tycho's measurements are retired (`docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Perf tooling and budgets", 2026-11-17 and 2026-11-23): nothing here times the app, and a check that drove the old page or read a retired sample is gone, to be written again by the goal that rebuilds what it tested. Their fixtures stay (`data/CLAUDE.md`), and so does `results/`: version 1's measurements, history, never edited.

## What stays

- **The wasm's size,** gated by `just tycho check` (`perf/size.ts`): the app wasm, brotli at quality 11, at most 15% over `baseline.json`'s `wasmBrotliKiB` unless a `budget-notes.md` row dated on or after the baseline accepts the new size. The limit is in `budgets.json`; change it there, never in the script. `budgets.json` lists no metrics, so `report` skips Tycho.
- **The page's weight before first paint,** printed by `just tycho build` (`shared-web/scripts/asset-sizes.ts`'s `--first-paint` row: the document, the entry JS, the frame's stylesheet, the app wasm, the UI font; React's chunk loads after the first frame and isn't in it). Reported, not gated. A file renamed out of that set fails the build until the justfile's parts name it again.
- **The home-path check,** in `check`, and its control, `just tycho home-paths` (`.claude/rules/toolchain.md`, "Release profile").
- **The smoke check** (`just tycho smoke`): in Chromium, Firefox, and WebKit, with `?perf`, a first frame appears (its time printed, stored nowhere), the page is cross-origin isolated, no console errors, and after each resize (and a frame's wait: WebKit's screenshot can hold the last size's image) the canvas fills the table's slot and has drawn at its right, where the panel is. On a release build, Chromium also holds Tycho to rule 2: no request outside the first-paint set (React's chunk included) starts before `gpui:first-frame` (`--control` must fail it). Against the dev server by default, where that part is skipped; pass the preview's URL (`just tycho preview`) for the release build. Run it on the release build after a change to what loads when.
- **The engine's lockdown** (`just tycho lockdown [url]`, `perf/lockdown.ts`): in Chromium, Firefox, and WebKit, through the app's own bridge (wrapped by an init script, so every call Rust makes is logged with its time): nothing registered and nothing queried but the lockdown's statements before `tycho:engine-locked`; each refusal in its list refused; the sample (its first-screen row, pressed), a dropped Parquet file, and a dropped CSV open, and `SUMMARIZE`, `parquet_metadata`, a re-read, and a file registered after the lock read; and, with `LOAD parquet` failed on purpose, a file dropped while the engine loads waits unregistered until Retry (the status bar's) locks it. What it tried for later goals (a remote file, `COPY` inside the directory) is printed, not judged. Run it on the release build after a change to the engine's start or to the names Tycho registers.
- **The engine end to end** (`just tycho engine [url] [--only <browsers>]`, `perf/engine.ts`): in Chromium, Firefox, and WebKit, every run with `?selftest`, which runs again after each Retry: the self-test passes after the lock; with the engine's chunk refused (Playwright's route, set before the page loads), the status bar shows "didn't load" and Retry, nothing locks, and the status bar's Retry, the chunk let through, locks it, the self-test passes, and the sample opens; with the sample open, DuckDB's worker throws (`page.workers()`, the `blob:` one) and End makes the app's next call, so the file closes, the status bar shows "stopped" and Retry, no engine starts before it's pressed (`duckdb:engine-start`), and Retry locks a new engine (`tycho:engine-locked` once more), the self-test passes again, and the sample opens and reads to its last row. Run it on the release build after a change to the engine's start, its Retry, or the status bar's controls.
- **The view state** (`just tycho view [url]`, `perf/view.ts`): in Chromium, Firefox, and WebKit, in the sample, a header dragged, an edge dragged wider, the first column pinned (`__tychoPin`), a column dragged into the pinned run, and 20 pinned (more than the table draws), each read back from `__tychoView` and matched against `__tychoTableLayout` (the view's count as the table pins it in effect: the leading columns within half the cells' width); a header is pressed once before it's dragged, since with the table's `select` only a selected column moves, and pressed at its middle, past its type icon (whose press is the icon's alone) and clear of a CSV's gutter widening as its rows grow; and a generated CSV (298 MiB, its last row turning `code` to text) arranged while it loads, its widened column keeping its place, width, and pin; the CSV is deleted when the run ends. The layout's `left` and `top` are the canvas's pixels: the check adds the canvas's offset in the page. Run it on the release build after a change to the controller or to the table's features Tycho turns on.
- **The command bar and the log** (`just tycho command [url]`, `perf/command.ts`): in Chromium, Firefox, and WebKit, driven by keys, a paste, a drag, and the log's buttons, each step read back from `__tychoView`, `__tychoTable`, the bar's DOM, and the log's: Cmd/Ctrl+K from the first screen (focus first on the canvas's input) and from the table; the sample opened and the log opened from the top bar, its one entry the opening, current; `pin` typed, pinned in the view, the log's newest entry typed and current, focus on the table; a pasted action of two steps (the paste event and one `insertFromPaste` input: Playwright's `insertText` composes in Firefox) refused whole, naming step 2, logged as a pasted error; a sentence's answer (waited for by its period: the preview says the words before Enter); `jump 1204` marking its row in view; a header dragged (pressed first, as `view.ts` presses one), logged in words from a click; `undo` giving back the pin's state, and the way back on the opening the file's own; select-all in the log taking its entries alone; Escape there giving the table focus, the log open; a small generated CSV chosen from the bar's list (Playwright's file chooser) starting the log again; no console errors. Run it on the release build after a change to the bar, the log, the grammar, or the controller.
- **The table's menus, the column list, the popover, and the row view** (`just tycho table [url] [--only <browsers>]`, `perf/table.ts`): in Chromium, Firefox, and WebKit, at 1440×900 in the sample, each step read back from `__tychoView`, the table's probes, the log's entries, and the DOM: a header icon's press (`__tychoIconPress`) opens its column's menu, and Hide, Pin to the left, Unpin, and Fit to its values each land in the view and the log from a click, focus on the table after each; a right-click on a cell opens its menu, and Copy writes it (`__tychoCopy` `written`; in Chromium, granted the clipboard, it reads back); `hide object_detail, purpose` typed, `{"show": ["purpose"]}` pasted, and `undo` giving the hide back; the column list's search, Enter on a hidden column's row opening its menu (Show alone), and Show; the `name` column narrowed by its edge so row 1's value is cut, its cue (`__tychoTable.cue`), Enter opening the popover, and Enter again the Row tab with focus at that field; Enter on a number opening the Row tab at once; a cue inside a range of `name`'s cells opening the popover with the range kept, one outside it selecting its cell (`cueOn` hovers rows until a cut one shows its cue; the cue's and the foot's boxes are the canvas's pixels), and the foot pressed taking focus at the field; with the panel closed, a double-click opening it on Row with focus left on the table; Next row moving the selection (whose column is the drawn place, not the index: an unpinned column stays first); a hidden column's field marked; Cmd/Ctrl+C on a range of three cells; the cell menu's "Focus row in panel" with the panel open and then closed, focus at the field each time; a header's press (a column's range) leaving the Row tab "No row selected"; and Next row at the last row (reached by `jump`) keeping focus, `aria-disabled`. Then at 390×760 with touch: a tap on an on-screen header opens its menu as a sheet (a press off the screen lands nowhere), and the panels' sheet holds Columns, Row, and Log, the Columns tab listing every column and the Row tab saying no row is selected (the tap selected a column). No console errors. A menu item's name may go on with its key's hint ("Copy⌘C"). Run it on the release build after a change to the menus, the panels, the popover's settings, or the presses the table sends the page.
- **Sort, filter, and find** (`just tycho filter [url] [--only <browsers>]`, `perf/filter.ts`): in Chromium, Firefox, and WebKit, at 1440×900 in the sample, each step read back from `__tychoView` (`filters`, `sort`), the toolbar's count (`[data-count]`, waited for past "Counting rows…"), the log's entries, the frame's line (`__tychoWorkbench.notice`), and the DOM: `satcat_no`'s sort arrow (the layout's `arrow`, a column on the canvas with the log panel open: `mass_kg` and `launch_site` aren't) pressed, sorting largest first, a click's `sort satcat_no desc`; `sort launch_date desc` typed, the sort's chip named "Sorted by launch_date, newest first", and its remove a click's `clear sort`; the filter editor from Filter (Base UI's Selects, an option by name; its foot `filter inclination_deg >= 90`), Apply giving "18,691 of 71,431 rows"; `filter object_type = Payload` typed and a `launch_date >= 2000-01-01` filter pasted as JSON, each count fewer; `undo` giving the pasted filter back, the way back on the typed sort's entry giving that sort and no filter, and on the pasted filter's entry the three filters and no sort; `sort launch_date` then `clear all`; a cell's menu's keep (`object_type`) and leave out (`name`); `filter name = "no such object"`, "0 of 71,431 rows" and the frame's line; then find: Cmd/Ctrl+F from a cell taken (prevented) and opening find with its tag, a second press in its bar not prevented (each key event kept from `window`'s capture phase and read after dispatch: the page takes the key on `document` and stops it), `cosmos` typed and counted, the table's term, Enter "1 of n" with its cell selected and focus kept, Escape giving the table focus, and find opened from the toolbar's button and closed before a step giving focus back to the button (in WebKit, where a click doesn't focus a button, the table). Then at 390×760 with touch: the toolbar a 40 px row of four icons with no words, a filter's icon showing "1", and find on a second row under it. No console errors. Each command waits for the bar to close before the next opens it. Run it on the release build after a change to the toolbar, the editors, find, the filter or sort verbs, or the view's rows.
- **Memory at a 1 GB drop, by hand,** when a change could make the engine copy a dropped file: build the files (`just tycho drop-files x3.parquet x27.parquet`: ~100 MB and ~1 GB), open the release build (`just tycho preview`) in Chrome with `?perf`, drop each in a fresh tab, press End and Home, and read the panel's Memory row and Chrome's task manager (the tab and DuckDB's worker). The 1 GB file's peak stays within a few MiB of the 100 MB file's; a copy of the file would show as its size. Version 1's numbers: `results/2026-09-27-m5.md`.

## The probes

The crate publishes these; the checks the rebuild writes start from this list, and a change to one in `crate/` changes it. `lockdown.ts` reads `__tychoBridge`, `__tychoWorkbench`, and the `tycho:engine-locked` and `tycho:file-shown` marks, and presses the first screen's sample row (`#first [data-action=sample]`) and the status bar's `[data-action=retry]`; smoke reads `gpui:first-frame` and `#table-slot`; `view.ts` reads `__tychoView`, `__tychoTableLayout`, and `__tychoWorkbench`'s `ingest`, calls `__tychoPin`, and presses the sample row; `engine.ts` reads `__tychoSelftest`, `__tychoWorkbench`, `__tychoTable`, the `tycho:engine-locked` and `duckdb:engine-start` marks, and the status bar's text, and presses the sample row and `footer [data-action=retry]`; `command.ts` reads `__tychoView`, `__tychoTable` (`marked`, `first`, `end`), `__tychoTableLayout`, the bar's text (`#command-bar`), and the log's entries (`#log li[data-entry]`: `data-origin`, `data-reply`, `data-current`, `[data-command]` or `[data-words]`, `[data-back]`), and presses the sample row, `header [data-action=log]`, the way back, and the bar's `[data-action=pick]`; `table.ts` reads `__tychoView`, `__tychoTable` (`selection`, `cue`, `popover`), `__tychoTableLayout`, `__tychoIconPress`, `__tychoCopy`, the log's entries, the open menu (`[data-menu]`, its `[role=menuitem]`s, a sheet's `aria-label`), the panel's tabs and the Row tab (`[data-row-head]`, `[data-row-step=next]`, `#row-view [data-field]`, `data-active`, `[data-row-empty]`), and the column list (`#columns`, its search, `button[data-column]` by name), and presses the sample row, `header [data-action=log]`, `header [data-action=columns]`, and at a phone `#log-sheet`'s tabs; `filter.ts` reads `__tychoView` (`filters`, `sort`), `__tychoTable` (`selection`, `find`'s `term`), `__tychoTableLayout` (each column's `arrow`), `__tychoWorkbench`'s `notice`, the log's entries, the toolbar (`#toolbar`, its `[data-tool]`s, `[data-count]`, the sort chip's label and remove), the filter editor in its `[data-menu]` (`[aria-label=Column]`, `[aria-label=Operator]`, "The value", `[data-line]`, Apply), the cell menu's items, and find's bar (`#find`, its input, `[role=status]`, `[data-find-tag]`), and presses the sample row, `header [data-action=log]`, the way back, and the toolbar's Find; no script reads the rest.

`?bench` exposes the bridge as `globalThis.__tychoBridge` and the app's `WebAssembly.Memory` as `globalThis.__tychoWasmMemory`; `?page=`, `?prefetch=`, and `?budget_mib=` override the table's paging (`crate/src/table.rs`, `paging`). With `?perf` or `?bench`: the table publishes what it shows to `globalThis.__tychoTable` (`marked` is the row a jump landed on, 0-based; `top` and `scrollX` the scroll position; `tooltip` the shown tooltip's text), where the cells are to `globalThis.__tychoTableLayout`, the open file's view state (`crate/src/view.rs`'s `ViewState`, frozen all the way down; null with none open) to `globalThis.__tychoView`, and what the workbench shows (state, file, rows, message, notice, a CSV's `ingest`, `readBytes`, `chunks`, `focused`, `engine`, and `status`, the frame's line) to `globalThis.__tychoWorkbench`. With `?cells` too, every drawn cell's exact and shown text goes to `globalThis.__tychoCells` each frame. `?bench` also adds `globalThis.__tychoPin(n)`, which pins the shown table's first `n` columns through the table (no gesture pins from none; `docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Perf tooling and budgets"), and `globalThis.__tychoProfileAsk(columns)`, which asks for those columns' profiles as the column list's press does: an ask holds until the file shown is closed or replaced (one made while a file opens is kept for it), so a check asks again after each open. While measuring, `globalThis.__tychoProfile` holds the asked columns' profiles (`profiles`: each `column`, its `profile` with its `stage`, `rows`, `nulls`, `nonfinite`, `lo`, `hi`, `mean`, `median`, `distinct`, `shape` and `shapeStage`, then `reading`, `waiting`, and `failed`) and the footer's null counts of an unfiltered Parquet view (`footerNulls`, each `[nulls, rows]`), frozen. While measuring, the table's features publish through the bridge: the selection to `globalThis.__tychoSelection`, the last press to `__tychoClick`, the hidden flags to `__tychoHidden`, the popover's button to `__tychoPopoverButton`, a header icon's press to `__tychoIconPress`, and a copy's outcome to `__tychoCopy`. `?selftest` runs the engine self-test once DuckDB's lockdown settles, again after each Retry, into the panel and `globalThis.__tychoSelftest` (replaced whole each run).

Marks: `tycho:engine-locked` when the lockdown is on (the engine takes files from then); `tycho:first-rows` always; with `?perf` or `?bench`, `tycho:viewport-filled` after each frame that fills the viewport, `tycho:csv-rows` on each frame showing a CSV's count grow, and `tycho:csv-done`. The frame's controls are DOM, pressed as a visitor does: `#first [data-action=pick]` and `#first [data-action=sample]` (the first screen's rows: Base UI names their ids, which its input's `aria-activedescendant` points at), the command bar's input (`[role=combobox]` in `#first` or `#command-bar`, the top bar's opened by Cmd/Ctrl+K), the log's button (`header [data-action=log]`) and panel (`#log` at a wide page, `#log-sheet` at a phone, the panels' sheet, its Columns tab first), the left panel's button (`header [data-action=columns]`) and panel (`#columns`: its rows `button[data-column]`, named by the column, ", hidden" after a hidden one's; its head's `[aria-label="Show and hide columns"]` opens `#columns-dropdown`, whose rows' buttons are named "Hide x" and "Show x"), an open menu (`[data-menu]`, its items `[role=menuitem]`), the panel's tabs (`[role=tab]`, "Log" and "Row"; at a phone "Columns", "Row", "Log" in `#log-sheet`), the Row tab (its head's `[data-row-head]` "Row n", `[data-row-step=previous]` and `[data-row-step=next]` (`aria-disabled` at the ends, never `disabled`), `[aria-label="Copy the row"]`; its fields `#row-view [data-field]`, by the column's index, the active one `data-active`, a hidden one's text "hidden in the table", each with `[aria-label="Copy this value"]`, its menu's button `[aria-haspopup=menu]`, and a long value's `[data-show-all]`; with nothing selected `[data-row-empty]`), `[data-action=retry]`, `[data-action=theme]`, the toolbar (`#toolbar`: its controls `[data-tool=filter]`, `[data-tool=sort]`, and `[data-tool=columns]`, named for what they show ("2 filters", "Sorted by launch_date, oldest first", "3 hidden columns", or "Filter", "Sort", "Columns"), the sort's `[aria-label="Remove the sort"]`, the count `[data-count]`; the filters' list's rows' "Remove the filter: …" and its "Add a filter" and "Clear all"; an editor's `[aria-label=Column]` and `[aria-label=Operator]` (Base UI's Select, its items `[role=option]` by name), its fields "The value", "The values", "From", and "To", its command `[data-line]`, Apply, Cancel, the sort's `[role=radio]` directions and "Remove the sort"; at a wide page in `[data-menu]` popovers, at a phone in sheets; the toolbar's Find, `[data-tool=find]`, and find's bar, `#find` (`role=search`): its input "Find in the table", its count or place in a `[role=status]` ("26 matches", "1 of 26"), the tag `[data-find-tag]` and its live region `[data-find-said]`, and "Previous match", "Next match", and "Close find"); a header's sort arrow is the layout's `arrow` on its column (`[x, width]` from the column's left, none for a file that can't be sorted), and the table's find its probe's `find` (`term`); the frame's state shows on the root as `data-file`, `data-panel`, `data-log`, and `data-columns`.

## Commands

```
just tycho smoke [url]         # a first frame, cross-origin isolated, no console errors, the canvas following its slot, in three browsers; on a release build, no request before first paint but its own (--control)
just tycho lockdown [url]      # the engine's lockdown in three browsers: refused, still read, held before the lock
just tycho engine [url]        # the engine in three browsers: its self-test, its chunk refused, its worker stopped, and Retry after each (--only chromium,firefox,webkit)
just tycho view [url]          # the view state in three browsers: a column dragged, widened, and pinned, read back; a CSV's widened column keeping its place, width, and pin
just tycho command [url]       # the command bar and the log in three browsers: keys, a paste, a drag, undo and the way back, another file
just tycho table [url]         # the table's menus, the column list, the popover, and the row view in three browsers, wide and at a phone (--only chromium,firefox,webkit)
just tycho filter [url]        # sort, filter, and find in three browsers: an arrow, the editor, typed and pasted filters, undo and the way back, a cell's keep and leave out, find's keys and focus; at a phone the toolbar's icons and find's row (--only chromium,firefox,webkit)
just tycho home-paths          # the home-path check's control: a build without `trim-paths` must fail it
```

`check` runs the size gate and the home-path check after its own release build; `node perf/size.ts` alone reads whatever `web/dist` holds.
