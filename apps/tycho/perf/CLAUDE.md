# Tycho's checks and measurements (`perf/`)

Every check and measurement script, the probes they read, the perf gotchas. Claude Code loads this file when a file under `perf/` is read. Tycho's rules and the map of its folders are in `apps/tycho/CLAUDE.md`, the repo's in the root `CLAUDE.md`; both apply here. Paths are relative to `apps/tycho/`.

## Perf script gotchas

The harness's helpers and headless Chromium's traps (the DPR shim, wheel deltas halved at DPR 2) are in `.claude/rules/perf-harness.md`, which loads with this file; these add to it. Debugging a slow or failing table, CSV, or paging check starts with the engine's DuckDB-Wasm gotchas (`crate/CLAUDE.md` points to them).

- **WebKit can deliver a scripted press before hover updates,** and GPUI fires a press only on a hovered element. Wait two frames after moving to a control (`hoverSettles` in `table.ts`).
- **Canvas controls have no DOM:** Tycho publishes their bounds from `crate/src/targets.rs`; click them with `shared-web/perf/`'s `clickTarget` and `targetRect` (`.claude/rules/perf-harness.md`).
- **The open observation panel swallows clicks under it** (the window's lower right: at 1440×900, about the right 400 px from y ≈ 340 down). Its bounds are `globalThis.__seizaPerfOverlayRect` (`[x, y, w, h]`, gone while hidden). Click only targets clear of it (check, as `perf/jump.ts` does), or hide it first (`shared-web/perf/`'s `setPanel`), and have every check confirm the result, not the click: a click on the panel fails silently. It keeps clear of the table's scrollbars (at 1440×900: x 1015–1399, the vertical track at x 1407), but not of the cells, nor of anything the empty state lays out wide (Retry once sat under it after a long status line).
- **Jump to row from a script:** Cmd/Ctrl+G focuses the input with its text selected (or click `jump-input`), type, Enter. `__tychoTable.marked` is the row it landed on (0-based); `__tychoWorkbench` has `jumpText`, `jumpRefusal` (the inline line, bounds `jump-refusal`), and `focused` (`jump`, `table`, `workbench`, or null).
- **Dropping a file from a script:** set it by path on a scratch `<input type=file>` (Chromium reads it from disk, no copy), then dispatch `dragenter`/`dragover`/`drop` with a `DataTransfer` holding it (`perf/harness.ts`, `dropFile()`). What the workbench shows (state, file, rows, notice, and a CSV's `ingest` state, `readBytes`, `chunks`) is in `globalThis.__tychoWorkbench` with `?perf` or `?bench`; each frame showing a CSV's count grow marks `tycho:csv-rows`, and the one showing it done `tycho:csv-done`.
- **The perf panel publishes at its refresh (~4 Hz), not every frame:** `globalThis.__seizaPerfOverlay` (rows) and `__seizaPerfOverlayAt` (when they were taken; the live rows cover the 2 s before it). Compare a live row against your own measurement over that window (`perf/panel.ts`). Load steps publish as rows under their own names ("Engine ready", "Sample → schema", …).
- **Per-frame work time, measured outside the app:** `shared-web/perf/`'s `workRecorder` (an init script) times every rAF callback that made a GPU call. It matched the panel within 5% at p50 and p95. The panel times from the shell's render to a microtask after the present. Both are the rAF callback only: the observatory's per-frame work (each frame's `BeginMainFrame` in Chromium's trace, with the input and the rendering update around the callback) runs ~0.4 ms more on the same fling, so Tycho's numbers don't compare with the observatory's (`apps/observatory/docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Measurement").
- **A/B against HEAD in a worktree, but not on a shared `target/`.** The justfile reads the wasm from the repo's `target/`, so a worktree build pointed there overwrote the main tree's fingerprints, and the next `just tycho build` called the main tree fresh and shipped HEAD's wasm (same hash). Give the worktree its own `target/`, or `cargo clean -p tycho -p seiza --release --target wasm32-unknown-unknown` after it, and check the built wasm's hash differs from HEAD's. Run each A/B side's `perf.ts` asynchronously when the same process hosts the preview servers: `execFileSync` blocks the servers and every navigation times out.
- **The placeholder hides the canvas until the first frame and fades out over 120 ms after it.** A script that screenshots the app or reads canvas pixels must wait for `#seiza-placeholder` to be gone, not just for `gpui:first-frame` (`shared-web/perf/placeholder-shots.ts`, `perf/placeholder.ts`). Its checks run with `reducedMotion: 'reduce'`, which stops the skeleton pulse and the fade.
- **Headless DPR 2 hides what a DPR 1 display shows.** GPUI and the browser round half-pixels in opposite directions (`.claude/rules/gpui-web.md`, "GPUI lays out in whole device pixels"); at DPR 2 that's half a CSS pixel, at DPR 1 a whole one. The placeholder's jump was invisible in every DPR 2 check and plain on a DPR 1 screen. `shared-web/perf/placeholder-shots.ts`'s `shoot` takes `{ dpr }`.
- **Budgets come from `perf/budgets.json`** (`limits('table', [...])` in `perf/harness.ts`); change a limit there, never in a script, and `just tycho report` shows it in the docs. **Label a before-run `<…>-before…`, an A/B `<…>-ab` or `<…>-ab-<detail>`, and a negative control `<…>-control…`:** the report skips those (`budgets.json`'s `exclude`), and otherwise takes the newest file holding a metric's path, so a control's numbers would become the docs' "Now".
- **Check the machine before calling a slowdown a regression.** `just tycho paging` (raw queries through the bridge, no table code) is a quick control: if it's slower by the same proportion, it's load.

## The probes

`?bench` in the URL exposes the bridge as `globalThis.__tychoBridge` for the perf scripts; `?page=`, `?prefetch=`, and `?budget_mib=` override the table's paging (`crate/src/table.rs`, `paging`). With `?perf` or `?bench`, the table publishes what it shows to `globalThis.__tychoTable` and marks `tycho:viewport-filled` after each frame that fills the viewport; `tycho:first-rows` (the overlay's "first rows") is always marked.

With `?cells` too, every drawn cell's exact and shown text goes to `globalThis.__tychoCells` each frame (never in perf runs); with `?perf` or `?bench`, where the cells are is `globalThis.__tychoTableLayout` (republished only when the layout changes; the scroll position is `__tychoTable.top` and `.scrollX`) and the shown tooltip's text is `__tychoTable.tooltip`.

`?selftest` in the URL runs the engine self-test once DuckDB is ready; results show in the panel and in `globalThis.__tychoSelftest`. With `?bench`, the app's `WebAssembly.Memory` is `globalThis.__tychoWasmMemory`.

## Commands

```
just tycho burst [flags] # the most requests one visitor sends in any 10 s (fling visit, random jumps on Gaia, held PageDown, reloads), for the WAF rate limit → perf/results/<date>-<label>.json (--label, --only)
just tycho sample [flags] # Sample checks on the release build: click → schema time, bytes transferred, early click (Chromium/Firefox/WebKit), failure modes and retry
just tycho table [flags] # Table checks on the release build: first rows, jump to 90%, last row vs count(*), fling and steady-scroll frame times, reference + throttled (--runs, --reference-only, --label, --sample asteroids|gaia; fling and steady scroll also record work per frame; --memory [--passes N]: full passes with a 128 MiB cap; --browsers: Chromium/Firefox/WebKit table check; --sweep: page size × prefetch; --memory-only, --browsers-only)
just tycho panel [flags] # Panel checks on the release build: every observation-panel value vs an independent measurement (page timings, rAF recorder, work-time wrapper, /data/ bytes at the server), and the fling with the panel open and closed, reference + throttled (--runs, --label, --reference-only)
just tycho cells [flags] # Cell-overflow checks on the release build: every fully visible cell's padding checked for spilled ink in screenshots (top, middle, end, three seeded rows, every column), rounded doubles within half a unit, tooltips on rounded, cut-text, and cut-DECIMAL cells, on both samples and drop/overflow.parquet (--files asteroids,gaia,overflow, --label)
just tycho jump [flags] # Jump-to-row checks on the release build, asteroids + Gaia: first, middle, last, out of range (the input and its refusal clear of the open panel), keys stay in the input, IME full-width digits, paste (Ctrl/Cmd+V, context menu with and without clipboard-read), Cmd/Ctrl editing keys, focus after a failed open; reference + throttled (--runs, --label, --reference-only, --samples, --browsers: Firefox/WebKit)
just tycho errors [flags] # Error-state checks on the release build: engine didn't load and engine stopped (Retry), sample and page reads offline (rows recover by themselves), reopen while reads drain, files over the limits refused, the panel clear of the scrollbars (--only 1,2,…, --label)
just tycho hardening [flags] # Hardening experiments: --only footers|ceiling|wheel-end (--files, --opens, --label); needs the drop files
just tycho drop [flags] # Drop checks on the release build: drop ~1 GB → schema and first rows (reference + throttled), 100 MB vs 1 GB peak memory (+ a held-copy negative control), unsupported files, a drop superseding a load, drop + file dialog in Chromium/Firefox/WebKit (--runs, --layout-runs, --label, --reference-only, --only drop|memory|unsupported|supersede|browsers)
just tycho csv [flags]  # CSV checks on the release build: drop ~1 GB CSV → schema and first rows, row-count updates ≤ 500 ms apart, fling while it loads, final count (reference + throttled); fixture count and tricky rows; supersede + table cleanup; Firefox/WebKit (--runs, --throttled-runs, --label, --reference-only, --only drop|fixture|supersede|browsers)
just tycho ingest [flags] # CSV ingest experiment: raw-file queries vs whole-file vs chunked ingest, by chunk size, compression, one table vs one per chunk (flags in perf/ingest.ts)
just tycho paging [flags] # Paging experiment: LIMIT/OFFSET vs file_row_number vs ingest, by row-group size (--files, --strategies, --setup, --dataset asteroids|gaia; needs bench/ files in local R2: the asteroids' built as in perf/results/2026-09-25-m4.md, Gaia's by `just tycho data gaia --bench …`)
just tycho smoke [url]  # Playwright Chromium/Firefox/WebKit: paints, crossOriginIsolated, no console errors, resize
just tycho home-paths   # the home-path check with its control: Tycho built without `trim-paths` (a cold build in target/untrimmed/) must fail it
just tycho perf [flags] # the full suite (~1 h 45 min): cold load, panel, table on both samples, jump to row, the ~1 GB CSV, reference + throttled, median of 10 → one perf/results/<date>-<label>.json (each part's own output in results/raw/; --runs, --label, --only cold,panel,table,jump,csv, --reference-only; --url <origin>: every part but panel against that origin, cold interleaved with the local build; budgets apply to the local build only, so a --url run records its load numbers without enforcing them); needs `just tycho drop-files csv`
just tycho cold [flags] # cold load only, reference + throttled, median of 10 → perf/results/<date>-<label>.json (--label, --runs, --write-baseline, --trace, --url; --vs-local with --url: also the local build, interleaved, and the app part of TTFP, wasm ready → first frame, against it; recorded, not budgeted); also checks no engine request starts before first paint
just tycho placeholder [flags] # Placeholder-shell checks on the release build: the placeholder shell vs GPUI's first frame in screenshots (light and dark, DPR 2 and 1), its steadiness while up, its font's characters and bytes, and the theme toggle (button, Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+L, system changes, reload); --controls: negative controls; --ab <dist>: interleaved TTFP and placeholder-paint A/B against another build, failing when either side's TTFP spread is 6% or more (--runs, --reference-only, --label)
just tycho engine [flags] # engine self-test from Rust (`?selftest`: SELECT 42, every shown type, cancel) in Chromium ×10 + Firefox + WebKit, plus blocked-engine negative controls (--label, --runs)
```

`table`, `jump`, and `csv` take `--url` like `cold`; `panel` doesn't (its "Read" check counts bytes at the local server).

`just tycho perf`, `cold`, and `check` serve `web/dist` through `vite preview`, brotli-compressed like production. `check` builds first; before `perf` or `cold`, run `just tycho build` yourself.
