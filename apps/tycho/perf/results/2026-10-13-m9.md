## What changed

- **Part A:** the perf harness's app-agnostic half moved to `shared-web/perf/`; canvas targets became `__seizaTargets`.
- **Part B:** Tycho's DuckDB layer became `engines/duckdb/` (`seiza-duckdb`, `@seiza/duckdb`), reporting through `EngineEvent`s.
- **Part C:** Tycho's table became `components/table/` (`seiza-table`) behind a `TableDelegate`; the Parquet source became `seiza-duckdb`'s `table` feature; the marks are Tycho's, from the table's events.
- App wasm (brotli): **2840.2 KiB** against 2838.0 before M9: +2.2 KiB, +0.08%.
