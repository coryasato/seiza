# M10: `trim-paths`, no home paths in the shipped wasm (2026-11-01)

Measured on Apple M1, Darwin 25.6.0 arm64, machine `local`. Sizes are exact bytes of one build each; TTFP is `perf.ts --check`'s reference run, Chromium headless=new, 1440×900 at DPR 2, median of 10 (root CLAUDE.md, "Measurement protocol").

Raw: `2026-11-01-m10-wasm.json` (the app wasm before and after, with its home paths counted).

## What changed

- The root release profile remaps build paths in the compiled output (`trim-paths = "object"`; root `docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Toolchain and build"), built in the same session as the observatory's M7 (its note: `apps/observatory/perf/results/2026-11-01-m7.md`).
- `check` fails if the shipped wasm holds the builder's `$HOME`, `/Users/`, or `/home/` (`shared-web/perf/home-paths.ts`); `just tycho home-paths` runs it with its control.

| The app wasm | Before | After | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Raw (cargo's output) | 13,827.5 KiB | 13,789.1 KiB | −38.4 KiB |
| After wasm-bindgen and wasm-opt (shipped) | 10,238.0 KiB | 10,195.1 KiB | −42.9 KiB |
| Brotli 11 | 2,855.2 KiB | 2,854.8 KiB | −0.4 KiB |
| Home-directory paths | 1,015 | 0 | |

## Against the budgets

| Check | Budget | Reference | Pass |
|---|---|---|---|
| TTFP (`check`) | 214.8 ms (baseline 195.3 + 10%) | 204.8 ms | yes |
| Wasm, brotli | 3,247.1 KiB (old baseline + 15%) | 2,854.8 KiB | yes |

`perf/baseline.json`'s `wasmBrotliKiB` rewritten to 2,854.8 (was 2,823.6, from 2026-10-10; the app has grown since without a rewrite). Its TTFP was kept: the check passed, and a change of 0.4 KiB brotli can't move first paint (204.8 ms is 4.9% over a baseline from three weeks of changes ago, not this one's). The file's `date` stays the TTFP's.

## Experiments and controls

- **Control:** Tycho built without the setting (`CARGO_PROFILE_RELEASE_TRIM_PATHS=none`, in `target/untrimmed/`, then wasm-bindgen and wasm-opt as shipped) holds 1,023 home paths, and the check fails it (`just tycho home-paths`).

## Reproducing it

```sh
just tycho build
just tycho home-paths
just tycho check
```

## Production

`just tycho deploy` (Worker version `8435b48e`): its check PASS (assets, /data range handling). The app wasm fetched from tycho.seiza.io as br and decompressed is byte-identical to the build and holds no home path.
