# M9 part B: the engine to `engines/duckdb/` (2026-10-13)

Measured on Apple M1, Darwin 25.6.0 arm64, machine `local`, Chromium headless=new, 1440×900 at DPR 2 (root CLAUDE.md, "Measurement protocol"). Part-end runs per `PLAN.md`: the suite parts the engine touches at 3 runs, reference only; `check`, `drop`, `sample`, `engine`, and the A/B at 10. Load average 1.97 at the start of `check` → 2.16 after the A/B. Not quiet (nothing heavy was running; ~1.6–2 was this machine's floor with the terminal and Claude Code up), so the A/B is a part-end check, not the milestone's quiet-session one.

Raw: `2026-10-13-m9-b-ab.json` (interleaved TTFP and placeholder A/B against the pre-M9 build, `raw/pre-m9-e0faf21/dist`, plus the placeholder checks), `-suite.json` (`--only table-asteroids,csv --runs 3 --reference-only`), `-drop.json`, `-sample.json`, `-engine.json`, `-errors.json`.

## What changed

- Tycho's DuckDB layer moved out, unchanged in behavior: `crate/src/engine/` and `arrow.rs` became `seiza-duckdb` (`engines/duckdb/crate/`), and `web/src/bridge.ts`, `engine.ts`, the extension pins and their script, and the Vite config's extension 404 rule became `@seiza/duckdb` (`engines/duckdb/web/`, its `duckdb()` plugin).
- The engine reports through `EngineEvent`s; Tycho's `crate/src/engine.rs` turns them into the panel's "Engine ready" and "Parquet ready" steps and the `tycho:parquet-ready` mark. Its JS marks are now `duckdb:engine-start` and `duckdb:engine-ready`. The pause message comes from `createBridge({ refusal })`.
- App wasm (brotli): **2840.2 KiB** against 2838.0 before M9 (part A's suite): +2.2 KiB, +0.08%. The first build of the move was 2838.7 KiB; the code review's fixes (the Parquet events' ordering, one `update_later` helper) added 1.5 KiB.

## Experiments and controls

- **Code review:** a `ParquetReady` sent from inside the load could arrive before `Ready` when an early sample click started the load, and `Ready` then reset the step to running for good (and `?selftest` never ran). Now the prefetch's result is always reported by the engine's start after `Ready`, and a load reports itself only once the prefetch has settled. `sample`'s "Parquet blocked, then retried" and `engine` passed after the fix.
