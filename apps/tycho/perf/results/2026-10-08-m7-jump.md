## Size and first paint

The field is **gpui-base's unstyled single-line `Input`** (`gpui_kit::base::input::Input`), styled in `workbench.rs`, with the right-click menu built there too. The first build used **gpui-kit's `Input`** and was rejected for its size.

| | HEAD (5545fdf) | gpui-kit `Input` (rejected) | gpui-base `Input` + menu (shipped) |
|---|---|---|---|
| App wasm, brotli | 2713.3 KiB | 2907.4 KiB (+194.1, +7.2%) | **2801.3 KiB (+88.0, +3.2%)** |
| TTFP reference, Δ vs HEAD (median of 20, interleaved) | — | +9.5 ms (196.9 vs 187.4) | **+3.4 ms** (196.0 vs 192.6) |
| TTFP throttled, Δ vs HEAD (median of 10, interleaved) | — | +199.7 ms (3802.1 vs 3602.4) | **+101.0 ms** (3691.6 vs 3590.6) |

The two A/Bs ran an hour apart (load 3.4–4.9, then 2.6–4.7), so compare each to its own HEAD. The context menu costs 2.1 KiB of the 88.0 (measured with and without it). The code-review fixes after the A/B (Mac bindings split and gated, the root's press handler, the parser) changed the wasm to 2799.3 KiB (+86.0). Raw: `2026-10-08-m7-jump-ab.json` (the rejected build's medians under `rejected`).

Why gpui-kit's `Input` costs more (`twiggy diff` of unstripped builds): the element renders any of three input states (single-line, textarea, code editor), so one single-line field links all three engines. That means three `TextElement` and `InputBaseState` monomorphizations, three `SearchPanel`s, LSP sync, completion and code-action lists, and a highlighter factory. gpui-base's element renders only the single-line state. Its 88 KiB is the single-line engine itself: ropey, undo, selection, and the IME and clipboard plumbing.

**`just tycho check` fails by 1.5 ms: 196.5 ms against the 195.0 ms limit** (baseline 177.3 + 10%; the run before it read 208.8 ms, spread 193–266, at load 3–4). HEAD's dist measured right after it, the same way, read **194.9 ms**. HEAD alone sits at the limit on this machine today, and the change's own cost is the A/B's +3.4 ms. The wasm is within budget (2801 of 3012 KiB). **Accepted** (2026-10-08): 195.0 stays the target, the baseline gets re-recorded in a quiet session, and the throttled +101 ms is to be won back by part E's placeholder shell.
