# The observatory's data (`data/`)

The six data files, how they're built, and their pins. Claude Code loads this file when a file under `data/` is read. The observatory's rules and the map of its folders are in `apps/observatory/CLAUDE.md`, the repo's in the root `CLAUDE.md`; both apply here. Paths are relative to `apps/observatory/`.

## Data

**Data:** six files (gitignored), built by `data/prep.ts` with native DuckDB. `asteroids.parquet` (the base level's), by `prep.sql` from the full asteroid sample, `data/raw/asteroids/sbdb.parquet` (gitignored; 37.7 MB, never served): 11 of its 18 columns, every row, its writer settings. The sample is built by `prep-sbdb.sql` from the JPL Small-Body Database's pages, fetched once by `fetch_asteroids.ts` into `data/raw/asteroids/`, and checked against the SHA-256 `MANIFEST.json` pins (the asteroids' `sources`); the catalog grows, so a new fetch differs and only `--only asteroids --repin` takes one (a new file: regenerate the fixtures and `cells.json`); a bare `--repin` leaves the asteroids as pinned, since the pinned pages can't be fetched again. A sample on disk is used as it is, its fetch date read from its own metadata. Tycho's `data/drop_files.ts` builds its test files from the same sample, checked against the same pin, so a repin changes them too. `ghcn-2025-tmax.parquet` (the wide sheet's), by `prep-ghcn.sql` from NOAA's GHCN-Daily: its three sources kept in `data/raw/ghcn/` (gitignored) with the version file, each checked against the SHA-256 `MANIFEST.json` pins (`sources`); NOAA appends late reports to a year's file, so a new download differs and only `--repin` takes one (a new file: regenerate `cells.json`). The pinned bytes exist only in a `data/raw/ghcn/` that has them; a machine without them downloads today's file, which fails the pin. `data/MANIFEST.json` (committed) holds each file's rows, bytes, SHA-256, row groups, its source's credit, terms, and license, and the GHCN file's citation, version, sources, and `range` (its least and greatest high; the heat's domain is the scenario's, `scenarios.ts`); `market-instruments.parquet` (market watch's), by `prep-market.sql` from `contenders/market/feed.ts`'s instruments: the table's 12 columns at the open (each price its reference, no change, sizes and volume 0), a row per instrument in the feed's order, after checking that no symbol is listed on a US exchange (NASDAQ Trader's `nasdaqlisted.txt` and `otherlisted.txt`, fetched at each build; its control, two symbols they must hold); `wall-instruments.parquet` (the monitoring wall's), by `prep-wall.sql` from `contenders/wall/instruments.ts`: `Desk`, `Symbol`, `Last` (the reference), `Change %` and `Volume` (0), a row per instrument in the feed's order, each desk's 50 in a run, after the same listing check (the directories fetched once a run) and checking that none repeats market watch's symbols or another of its own; `nasa-http-jul95.parquet` (live tail's), by `prep-nasa.sql` from NASA-HTTP's July 1995 log, kept in `data/raw/nasa/` (gitignored, downloaded when missing) and checked against its pin (a 1995 file: another download fails): time, request, status, and bytes, the host dropped and the requests cut by the rules in `docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Scenarios"; prep fails unless its rows and span are the replay's (`contenders/tail/data.ts`), and runs `sql.json`'s `live-tail` pages against it in native DuckDB (each row the file's row *i* mod *n*, its time moved on by its laps; the control, no shift, must differ); `countries.parquet` (the admin table's), by `prep-countries.sql` from the rows `data/countries.ts` derives (its rules: the regions, each name in its likely language and in English, the flag from the code, the capitals, the population as text), its sources kept in `data/raw/countries/` (gitignored, downloaded when missing): CLDR's two npm tarballs, pinned by version in `prep.ts` (another download fails), and the Wikidata query's result (`WIKIDATA_QUERY`), pinned in `MANIFEST.json`'s `sources` (a live query: only `--repin` takes a new one, a new file); prep fails unless it gives 250 rows, and `MANIFEST.json` carries CLDR's license notice verbatim (`notice`), which the README's credits show. The page shows the credit (`cold.ts`). Each must stay under 25 MiB (a static asset); `prep.ts` fails otherwise. Why these files and columns: `docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Data" and "Scenarios".

## Files

```
  data/             # prep.ts + fetch_asteroids.ts + prep-sbdb.sql + prep.sql + prep-ghcn.sql + prep-market.sql + prep-wall.sql + prep-nasa.sql + countries.ts and prep-countries.sql (native DuckDB, its own lockfile), MANIFEST.json (committed); the .parquet files and raw/ (the asteroid pages and sample, GHCN's, NASA-HTTP's, and the countries' pinned sources) gitignored
```

## Commands

```
just observatory data [flags]  # data/asteroids.parquet (from the pinned asteroid sample in data/raw/asteroids/, its pages fetched when missing) + data/ghcn-2025-tmax.parquet (from GHCN-Daily's pinned sources in data/raw/ghcn/, a missing one downloaded) + data/market-instruments.parquet (from the feed's instruments; fails on a symbol listed on a US exchange) + data/wall-instruments.parquet (from the wall's instruments; the same check, and none repeating market watch's) + data/nasa-http-jul95.parquet (from NASA-HTTP's pinned log in data/raw/nasa/, downloaded when missing; the replay's pages checked against it) + data/countries.parquet (from CLDR's pinned tarballs and the Wikidata query's pinned result in data/raw/countries/, downloaded when missing) + data/MANIFEST.json (--only asteroids|ghcn|market|wall|nasa|countries, --repin)
```
