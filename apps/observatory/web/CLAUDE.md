# The observatory's page (`web/`)

The page's architecture. Claude Code loads this file when a file under `web/` is read. The observatory's rules and the map of its folders are in `apps/observatory/CLAUDE.md`, the repo's in the root `CLAUDE.md`; both apply here. Paths are relative to `apps/observatory/`.

## The page

**The page** (`web/`, Vite on `shared-web/` and `@seiza/site-ui`, `base: '/observatory/'`, React without JSX): one HTML file per route (`index.html` the Overview, `tall-table/index.html` the TallTable's page, `tall-table/<scenario>/index.html` each scenario's comparison, the only routes that take a parameter; `src/components.ts`'s `ROUTES`, from `contenders/scenarios.ts`), each prerendered by `shared-web`'s `prerender` (`src/prerender.ts`, Node only) and hydrated by `src/main.ts` as the route its HTML names (`data-route`, not the URL), in the site's shell (`SiteShell`, no footer). Its own UI, the dashboard (`docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Page"): `src/page.ts` (the sidebar from `site/ui`'s Nova parts: the groups, one item filled and the rest muted, a component's row linking its page with a chevron opening its scenarios' list (a disclosure of its own, open on its own routes), the source link at its bottom; the inset card, its header holding the sidebar's toggle and the crumb (on a scenario's route "TallTable / <scenario>", the name a menu of every scenario's link); a scenario's link carries the route's `?side=`, never `?stop=`; on a scenario's route the contender's window filling the first screen, then the description, rendered after first paint), `src/gallery.ts` (the Overview's and the component's page's bodies (the admin table drawn as its own: names in their scripts and the find bar on "Ísland"; the monitoring wall as eight small tables, four across in two rows), a chunk of their own: the prerender renders them directly and the client hydrates them through `lazy` (`page.ts`'s `Galleries`), so their HTML is whole and their code isn't first-paint JS; the Overview's thesis, its sides, a card per component (its scenarios' frames drawn small, its decision rule) and "More components coming"; the component's page's cards per scenario (a drawing, its use, its verdict), how far each side keeps up (each row opening its scenario's Compare by `#compare`, which the slot opens after first paint and clears), and why each side wins (`src/mechanisms.ts`, each scenario's in its copy, `components.ts`'s `leans`); every comparing word from `virtual:gallery`), `src/components.ts` (the components, their contenders (name, label, a URL per level; never a contender's code), their scenarios and what the page says of each (the lead, the dial's label), and the routes), `src/slot.ts` (the contender's window: one contender at a time in a same-origin frame at the scenario's level, its URL `?scenario=`, `?stop=`, and `?theme=` (and on the market, tail, and wall levels `?readout`: the frame keeps this tab's Shown in, `docs/contender-contract.md`, "The readout's ring"), created after the page's first paint (`src/paint.ts`), between a title bar and a status bar; the title bar the GPUI | TanStack toggle and, for a scenario with stops, the dial (a segmented control, each stop's parameters in its title and the dial's explainer with this window's count; a phone's menu button), both disabled until the shown frame's first `contender:filled` or its `contender:failed` arrives by `postMessage`; on live tail's route, beside the dial, the follow toggle (`numbers.ts`'s `FollowToggle`, after first paint, disabled until the frame fills: "Pause" while the frame says it follows, else "Follow · <n> new rows", the rows since following was let go, counted from the frame's reports; a press asks the frame, `docs/contender-contract.md`, "The follow toggle"; a phone's its icon and the count); on the admin table's, where a dial would be, the fonts toggle (`numbers.ts`'s `FontsToggle`, after first paint: on GPUI it reloads the side with `?fonts` or without, disabled while a load or the faces are coming; on TanStack disabled, its explainer why; a flip comes back without the faces; the window keeps its state from the frame's `contender:fonts` and `contender:fonts-failed`, and says it in the side's state, "loading fonts for 13 scripts…", then "Ready · warm · Fonts loaded after first paint: N KiB…" from the frame's Resource Timing, and in the load's explainer; a frame that fails leaves it usable), the wide sheet's Copy button in the same place (`numbers.ts`'s `CopyButton`, after first paint: the frame made with `?readout` tells the page its selection and copy, kept in `slot.ts`'s `toolsStore` outside React's state so only the button and the status bar's left re-render; disabled until something's selected, "Copy anyway" after a declined key, "Cancel" while copying; it calls the frame's `__contenderTools` inside its click; `docs/contender-contract.md`, "The copy line"), the side's state (the page's `aria-live` text for a load, changed only by a load: the app, the engine, the data, ready cold or warm, failed; a phone's cut to "Ready" or "Loading…" beside the dial; the wide sheet's selection and copy are said in the status bar instead: its left holds the copy line in the data label's place while something's selected, truncating in a narrow bar, and a polite region of its own that speaks the line's words when they change, never a copy's share, a selection's count once it rests; a phone's Copy sits there) with its explainer (what the load fetched and what came from the cache: the frame's Resource Timing; the engine's worker's requests aren't in it), and the lead; a flip or a stop destroys the frame and creates the new one; `?side=` and `?stop=` pick the first frame, and a switch writes them, the defaults omitted; the page's theme into the frame as `?theme=` once, then by message, and again at the frame's `load`; the status bar's box, `.observatory-status` in `page.css`, in the prerendered HTML with `contain: strict`, its contents rendered after first paint; the meter over the frame (not while the panel is open); the panel over the frame while open, its close and Esc focusing the frame), `src/meter.ts` (the meter, in the numbers' chunk: the frame-time and memory graphs, one canvas each, drawn on `live.ts`'s meter refresh, their text by `textContent`; open, a pill, or hidden, in one of four corners, both in `seiza-observatory-meter`, a phone starting with the pill; it takes the pointer only on its buttons and its header, so a click or a wheel under it reaches the contender; the header drags it with pointer capture and it snaps to the nearest corner; the picker and the arrow keys move it), `src/explainer.ts` (every explainer and the phone's overflow menu: hover opens on a fine pointer, a press pins, Enter or Space opens, Esc closes keeping focus, one open at a time; the popover rendered into the body, fixed from its trigger, so no box clips it), `src/metrics.ts` (the metrics module, loaded after first paint), `src/numbers.ts` (a chunk of its own, rendered after first paint, never prerendered: the status bar's contents (each scenario's cards: Ready in, Rows in, Smooth, Memory; market watch's, live tail's, and the monitoring wall's Ready in, Shown in, Smooth, Memory (the wall's Memory less every canvas buffer, as the admin table's); what the scenario's data is (its copy's `dataLabel`) and its rows on the left, in a database icon's explainer in a bar under 1,100 px (neither on a phone); Meter; Run benchmark disabled until it's built; Compare; the Memory explainer's line that Chrome leaves out GPUI's WebAssembly memory (`readouts.ts`'s `CHROME_MEMORY`); on a phone the scenario's own two (`readouts.ts`'s `phone`: Smooth and Memory, market watch's, live tail's, and the monitoring wall's Shown in and Smooth) and an overflow), their readings written by `textContent` from `live.ts`'s refresh, never a React render; the panel, per scenario (Big file's: the cards with both devices, their verdicts, "best", this tab's row once a side has loaded, Download by part, the input-to-response chart on one 0–50 ms scale; a scenario with stops': "Where each side keeps up" (what it reads and its line, the scenario's `keeps`: a share on 0–100 %, or live tail's Row shown in p95 on a 10 ms–1 s log scale), its verdict and a chart per device, the stops under them picking the cards' stop without touching the frame, the cards at that stop (three in a row, live tail's four in a 2 × 2; the collection card the pauses' time, the longest and those in a late frame beside it), a line under the stop's heading where its numbers need one (`stopNote`: live tail's 100k on a slow device), and the card for what no stop changes (live tail's with the soak's memory, `onceCards`); the admin table's, a scenario of capabilities (`components.ts`'s `capabilities`), "What each side can do": its verdict, the six capabilities (`capabilities`, the scripts row by the window's fonts state), each script with fonts loaded (`src/scripts.ts`'s `SCRIPT_ROWS`, pure: every face's script, then Han's and Hangul's; `cold.ts` fails the build on a face without a verdict), and four cards with both devices (Ready in, Download, Memory with the second canvas buffer's line, and the fonts' own, GPUI's alone, `ScenarioCold.gpui`)); the description below the window, per scenario (`STORIES`; a story's `shortfalls`, where the canvas falls short beyond every scenario's: the wall's phone column, the wide sheet's whole cells and pointer-only resize), its "where each side wins" generated, the data's credit and citation, the versions measured with, and the other scenarios), `src/readouts.ts` (pure: each scenario's cards (`cardsOf`, `WIDE_STOP_CARDS`, `WIDE_DRAG_CARDS` and `WIDE_COPY_CARDS` (the panel's "While dragging" at the cards' stop and "Copying" at the default stop, under the stop's cards; a card's `measures`, `noteOf`, `explainOf`, and `format`; a scenario's `onceReference`, metrics read on the fast device alone), `MARKET_CARDS`, `MARKET_STOP_CARDS`, `TAIL_CARDS`, `TAIL_STOP_CARDS`, `SOAK_MEMORY`, `WALL_CARDS`, `WALL_STOP_CARDS`; a card with `apart` wins only where the two sides' runs don't overlap, its `-min` and `-max` metrics), its metrics and their keys (`metricsOf`, `coldKey`, a stop's first where measured per stop; `derive`, "keeps up"'s share), the chart's rows, the Download card's parts and their `perf/results/` sources per scenario, and the words: `verdict`, `keepsVerdict`, `wins` and `winsOf`, `measuredWith`, the number formats; `cold.ts` and `perf/page.ts` read it too), `cold.ts` (the cold numbers as `virtual:cold-numbers`, each scenario's with them (`components.ts`'s `hasCold`), resolved at build time from `perf/budgets.json` and `perf/results/` by `shared-web/scripts/results.ts`, as `report` resolves them: never type a number into the page; the galleries' words as `virtual:gallery` (`readouts.ts`'s `galleryOf`: the rule, each scenario's verdict, each side's reach), the only numbers in the first-paint JS; the data file's rows from `data/MANIFEST.json` as `virtual:data-rows`, the one value the prerendered title bar needs, so the prerender gets the plugin too; `resolveCold`, the same numbers for `perf/page.ts`), `src/live.ts` (this tab's live readouts: the slot hands it each frame and its relayed marks; it records raw readings, epoch ms, from listeners on the frame's window, a rAF loop only while a scroll is under way (on the wide sheet a drag's too: a pointer moved with a button held, which TanStack's frames take only there) or, under a feed, from the first rows painted on (kept to about the meter's window; the page having been hidden starts a new run), the feed's Shown in samples copied from the frame's ring at each refresh, and the frame's memory by `measureUserAgentSpecificMemory` (never during a scroll; the last minute's kept), its `JavaScript` type read, as the cold memory at equal work; the metrics module's `liveReadings` turns them into numbers at 4 Hz, and `meterReadings` into the meter's graphs while it's shown, nothing while the page is hidden; a side loaded second or from the cache is warm). Why these cards and readings: `docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Page". The description says GPUI's touch limits, as the README's "Canvas tradeoffs". The dev and preview servers serve what the frames load, as the deploy does: both contenders' release builds at their bases (each feature level's under its contender's), the engine (the GPUI build's `duckdb/`), the font, the admin table's script faces, and the data. `scripts/grays.ts` (in `lint`) holds the site's surface grays within ΔE 2.5 of the contenders' `theme.css`. Budgets: first paint (paint timing) and first-paint JS, `perf/page.ts`. Anything below the fold renders after first paint: prerendered, it delays it.

## Commands

The page's own check, `just observatory page`, is in `perf/CLAUDE.md`.

```
just observatory dev           # the page's Vite dev server, with COOP/COEP, its frames on the contenders' release builds (build them first)
just observatory build         # the page's release build (one prerendered HTML file per route), asset sizes
just observatory preview       # serve that build (brotli) with the contenders' builds, the engine, the font, and the data, as the deploy does
```
