## The TanStack contender

**The TanStack contender** (`contenders/tall-table/tanstack/web/`, Vite, base `/observatory/contenders/tall-table/tanstack/`, a level's under it): React and TanStack Virtual over the DOM, no Wasm of its own. Built once per feature level, as the GPUI contender is (`--mode wide`: `web/dist-wide/` at `/observatory/contenders/tall-table/tanstack/wide/`; `--mode market`: `web/dist-market/` at `…/tanstack/market/`; `--mode tail`: `web/dist-tail/` at `…/tanstack/tail/`; `--mode wall`: `web/dist-wall/` at `…/tanstack/wall/`): `__LEVEL__` is a build constant (`contenders/level.ts`'s `levelConstants`, in the prerender's server too), so every branch of another level's is dropped from a level's bundle, and `./level.css` resolves to `wide.css`, `market.css`, `tail.css`, `wall.css`, or the empty `base.css`. Keeping the other levels' bundles byte-identical takes three habits (`docs/DECISIONS.md`, "TanStack contender"): a top-level call in a level's module (`memo(…)`) marked `/* @__PURE__ */`, a level's imports in `main.ts` after the view's (imported first, they reorder the other levels' modules), and anything Vite transforms whatever the branch (a worker's `new Worker(new URL(…))`) only in a module the level alone imports; a level's value rides on the view's one `scene` prop (`LevelScene`) rather than a prop of its own. `main.ts` resolves `?scenario=` and `?stop=` against the build's level (`pickScenario`; an unknown id shows the failure, marks `contender:failed`, opens nothing, and the replay rejects at once) and hands the view the wide level's scene. Its first screen, the table's empty frame with one status line (as the GPUI contender's), is prerendered: `vite.config.ts` gives `shared-web`'s `prerender` the view's `App` (`FIRST_SCREEN`), rendered into `index.html` in dev and build, so `view.ts` and what it imports must not touch browser globals at import. It follows the page's theme live (`followPageTheme`: the root's `data-theme`, which `theme.css` follows). `src/main.ts` hydrates it (React without JSX, `createElement`; `hydrateRoot`), sets `contender:first-pixels` at the line's Element Timing paint and `usable` in the hydration commit, and once both the paint timing entry and that commit have happened, imports the data layer and Arrow, starts the engine, marks `open`, opens the file, and the table replaces the line. "After first paint" is the paint timing entry, not a frame callback (Chromium's `first-paint` is the frame's presentation, after the task that drew it); a page that can't paint starts at once: hidden now or before its paint (a background tab), or with an empty viewport (a `display: none` frame, which reports itself visible and never paints). ## Commands

Its checks (`tanstack-view`, `tanstack-parity`, `tanstack-contract`, `tanstack-sizes`, and each level's) are in `perf/CLAUDE.md`.

```
just observatory tanstack-dev [level]     # the TanStack contender's Vite dev server at /observatory/contenders/tall-table/tanstack/ (a level's under it: `wide`, `market`, `tail`, `wall`), with the engine, the font, and the data files by range
just observatory tanstack-build [level]   # its release build at a level (`base`, `wide`, `market`, `tail`, `wall`) into web/dist[-<level>]/ (Arrow or DuckDB's JS in a first-paint chunk fails it), asset sizes
just observatory tanstack-preview [level] # serve that build (brotli), with the data files
just observatory theme-css [--check] # the TanStack contender's theme.css from gpui-kit's default theme (native; --check compares)
```
