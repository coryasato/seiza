# The GPUI contender (`contenders/tall-table/gpui/`)

The bare GPUI host of the tall table. Claude Code loads this file when a file under `contenders/tall-table/gpui/` is read. The observatory's rules and the map of its folders are in `apps/observatory/CLAUDE.md`, the repo's in the root `CLAUDE.md`; both apply here. Paths are relative to `apps/observatory/`.

## The GPUI contender

**The GPUI contender** (`contenders/tall-table/gpui/`): `crate/` (package `tall-table-gpui`; its native example `theme_css` writes the TanStack contender's theme) is the bare host, `seiza-tall-table` over `seiza-duckdb`'s `tall-table` source on `seiza` without `panel` (the workspace's `seiza` entry turns defaults off; Tycho names `features = ["panel"]`), built alone (`-p`), once per feature level (`contenders/scenarios.ts`): the base, and with the crate's feature `wide` (the table's `custom-cells`, `pin`, `density`, `resize`, `select`, and `copy`, and the bridge's) the wide sheet's build, its wasm in `web/pkg-wide/` and its build in `web/dist-wide/`, at `/observatory/contenders/tall-table/gpui/wide/` (`web/vite.config.ts` takes the level as Vite's mode, `--mode wide`, which the recipes and the perf harness's `preview` pass; the base carries none of the level's code). `scene.rs` is what the page asked for: the file's SQL name and, on the wide level, the stop's row height, text size, and days' width, the key columns pinned, and heat on every day (`HeatRows`, the engine's pages each painted through the scenario's domain, which the JS host passes in the scene's numbers, and its diverging ramp, whose stops are constants here that `wide` checks against `scenarios.ts`'s `heat`); `web/src/main.ts` resolves `?scenario=` and `?stop=` against the build's level (`pickScenario`) and hands `start` the numbers, or the error an unknown id makes, which the host shows and marks `contender:failed`. `host.rs` opens the scenario's file once the engine is ready (`open_parquet` under the file's own name, no metadata keys) and shows the table; until then the table's empty frame holds one status line ("Engine loading…" in the first frame). `contract.rs` publishes the contract (`docs/contender-contract.md`) from the table's events, the mark, targets, and probes' JS values through `tall-table-bridge` (`components/tall-table/bridge/`, every host's): `contender:open` and `contender:filled` (with `mark_after_current_task`, always), `contender:failed` (the open failing, or the engine `Failed` or `Stopped`), `__contender.state`, the targets (`seiza::targets`), and the probe key with `?measure` (`contender:input`: the first key the window gets after `gpui:first-frame`, through `intercept_keystrokes`, notifies the host, whose next render marks it), the drawn cells and the columns' budgets with `?cells` (the table's cell probe on, for the cell fixture; `docs/contender-contract.md`, "The cell probe"), and the replay's wasm exports (`replayOpen`, `replayJump`, `replayGeometry`, `replayView`: the count of changed frames and whether the last was filled, since a view that lands already filled sets no `contender:filled`). `state.cacheBytes` is read at most every 100 ms, outside the frames. The table probes every frame (`probe_frames(true)`), measured or not: the replay's fling reads the rows and row height from them. With `?cells` on the wide level, the layout probe too (`__contender.layout`: the row height and the pin in effect, for `perf/wide.ts`). On the wide level, `src/wide.rs` (a file of its own, reached through `contract.rs`'s `watch_level`, so no other level's code moves) holds the tools at the table's defaults (every column resizable as `pin` allows, every kind of selection, `key_max_cells` 100,000, `max_bytes` 64 MiB, no header line): each `TallTableEvent::Copy` handed to the bridge's `clipboard::Clipboard`, a copy it can't write cancelled, the writer told when the host closes the table (`contract.rs`'s `opening_level`, right after the close: a replay's reopen); a second Cmd+C (Ctrl+C) on the selection the key declined the table's `copy` past the key's limit, in the key's task (a selection change in between declines again; Cmd+Shift+C is the browsers' Inspect Element, so no chord); with `?measure` the copy's marks (`contender:copy-started`, `copied`, `copy-written`, `copy-declined`) and `__contender.copy`, `.selection`, `.widths`, and `.layout` (at each layout change, never a frame); with `?readout` the page's copy line (`docs/contender-contract.md`, "The copy line"). A touch drag on a band resizes nothing; a tap selects. The crate's feature `market` (the table's and the bridge's `custom-cells`, `live`, and `format`) is market watch's level, built into `web/pkg-market/` and `web/dist-market/` at `/observatory/contenders/tall-table/gpui/market/` (`--mode market`): `src/market.rs`, its store (`Quotes`, folding a batch's every tick into plain buffers, a batch that isn't one refused whole; `ticked`, the instruments a batch ticked), its feed fixture and controls run natively; `src/scene/market.rs`, the scene (`Market`: the flash's ms, mix, and columns, the rise and fall per theme, each instrument's decimals, all handed in by the JS host, held for the table's `fn` stops: no copies of `scenarios.ts`), the file checked at the open (its 12 columns, `COLUMNS`, and a row per instrument), the columns as the canvas writes them (prices at the page's per-instrument decimals, Change signed, Change % signed at 2 with `%`, the sizes and Volume grouped; Change and Change % through a text ramp, the fall, the foreground, the rise, over ±1e-9; the flash on the columns named), and the source (`MarketRows` over the engine's: the open's page read by the engine, the store seeded from its Last, every read after answered from the store in hand, the Symbol and Name the open's page's; its tests natively, `cargo test -p tall-table-gpui --features market market::`); `src/readout.rs` (wasm, the market and tail levels), the readout's ring with `?readout`: a sample waiting under a key until a frame draws it (market watch's visible batch for the `Refreshed` of its generation, live tail's count for the `TailDrawn` that reaches it, `contract.rs`'s `on_event`; a change of following drops live tail's), on `__contender.shownIn`; `src/feed.rs` (wasm), the feed: `market()` takes how to make the worker, its start message, and the flashing columns before `start`; the wasm makes and starts the worker when the table first fills (`watch`), and folds each batch in its message's task (`contract::with_host_now`, `Host::feed`: the fold, each run of ticked rows invalidated, a refused batch failing the host), recording it with `?measure` on `__contender.batches`; a replay `open()` ends the feed. `contract.rs` adds, with `?measure` on that level, `contender:refreshed` and `__contenderRefreshed` (the bridge's refreshed mark and its raised generation); the worker failing to load or throwing fails the host too. The crate's feature `tail` (the table's and the bridge's `custom-cells`, `follow` with its `live`, `format`, and `density`, the last for the columns' widths alone; and its own optional `web-sys` and `wasm-bindgen-futures`, so no other level's build changes: `docs/DECISIONS.md`, "GPUI contender") is live tail's level, built into `web/pkg-tail/` and `web/dist-tail/` at `/observatory/contenders/tall-table/gpui/tail/` (`--mode tail`): `src/tail.rs`, the replay's page SQL over the looping file (`TailQuery`, every page from the file fetched whole, `WHOLE_FILE`) and the time's text (`time_text`), their fixtures run natively; `src/scene/tail.rs`, the scene (`Tail`: the error color per theme and the columns' widths, the canvas's, handed in by the JS host), the file checked at the open (its four columns, `COLUMNS`), and the source (`TailRows` over the engine's pages: the time the log's text, never the engine's, in the muted foreground; a 4xx or 5xx status in the error color; the bytes grouped); `src/clock.rs` (wasm), the replay's path: `tail()` takes how to fetch the file whole, how to make the clock's worker, and its start message before `start`; the open reads the file by range under its own name and shows the table at 0 rows, following (`scene::set_level`); then `watch` fetches the file whole and registers it (once a page), and only then makes and starts the worker; each count is set in its message's task (`Host::rows_due`), recorded with `?measure` on `__contender.batches`; the fetch, the registration, or the worker failing fails the host (`Host::fail_feed`); a replay `open()` ends the clock and the new table's `watch` starts another, at 0 rows; `clockPause`, the scroll back's control; with `?readout`, the page's follow toggle: `followTail` (the table's `follow` or `let_go`) and the reports (`tail()`'s `report`, `follow.ts`'s `followToggle`, called at the table's `Following` and at each count while let go). `contract.rs` adds, with `?measure` on that level, the bridge's tail mark and globals (`contender:tail`, `__contenderTail`, `__contenderFollowing`, the last set at `watch` too, since the table follows before the subscription hears it). The crate's feature `wall` (market watch's table and bridge features and `density`) is the monitoring wall's level, built into `web/pkg-wall/` and `web/dist-wall/` at `/observatory/contenders/tall-table/gpui/wall/` (`--mode wall`): market watch's store and feed (`market.rs`, `feed.rs`, `readout.rs`) under `cfg(any(feature = "market", feature = "wall"))`, and a host of its own in the bare host's place (`lib.rs`: `use wall as host`, so the contract and the feed reach it as `crate::host::Host`): `src/wall.rs`, the stop's N tables over one store, the open reading the file's summary (its five columns and 400 rows, `scene/wall.rs`'s `check`), then the shown desks' rows in one read through the page SQL, which seeds the store (`WallStore`), then a table a desk (`DeskRows`, every read in hand), each at the grid's rows, text, and widths, laid out in two rows of N / 2 with each desk's title (the names from `wallDesks`, before `start`), and under the grid's `oneColumnBelow` one column of tables, each its title, its header, and the grid's `phoneRows` whole above the table's scrollbar room, at most the grid's `columnWidth` and centered, the column scrolled only from a title, a gap, or its sides (`docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Page"); the marks across the tables (`filled` from the frame that leaves no table unfilled, which also starts the feed; with `?measure` one `refreshed` a frame that draws any table's re-read and `__contenderRefreshed` an entry a table; `feed::record_wall`, each batch's generations and visible mask; with `?readout` `readout::drawn_wall`, the ring pooled across the tables; with `?cells` `__contender.tables`, every table's cells, columns, layout (three numbers from `TallTableLayout`), and box (the table's bounds at prepaint, `probed`)); the first table's events publish the rest of the contract (`contract::watch`), `state.tables` beside them. `src/scene/wall.rs` holds the level's numbers (`Wall`: the tables, the file's rows, the grid, then market watch's `Market` for the colors, the flash, and the shown instruments' decimals), the four columns as the canvas writes them, the store, and the source, its tests run natively (`cargo test -p tall-table-gpui --features wall scene::wall::`). The edits a level makes to `contract.rs`, `host.rs`, `market.rs`, and `scene/market.rs`, the files whose panic locations market watch's wasm carries, stay on their lines, and the wall's own code is at their ends or in its own files, so market watch's wasm is byte-identical (`docs/DECISIONS.md`, "GPUI contender"). The base level's fonts toggle, the admin table's (`docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Scenarios"), is the crate's feature `fonts` (`seiza`'s `canvas-font-fallback`), which only the base build turns on (`contenders/level.ts`'s `gpuiCargoFeatures`, the recipes; `sizes` checks no other level carries it): `src/fonts.rs` exports `scriptFonts(mark)` (the toggle on before `start`: `EmojiAndCjk` through `Bootstrap::canvas_font_fallback`, and the mark's name) and `addFonts` (the faces added and the window refreshed, the mark set from the host's next render, right after the frame drawn with them). The JS host's base-level host, `web/src/base.ts`, calls `scriptFonts(MARKS.fonts)` for the admin table with `?fonts` (`FONTS_PARAM`) and, at the first `contender:filled`, loads `web/src/fonts.ts`, a chunk of its own, which fetches the 13 faces from `/observatory/fonts/` at once, checks each against its pin (gpui-pre-wgpu's `add_fonts` skips bytes it can't parse without an error), marks `contender:fonts-in`, and calls `addFonts` (a fetch or a pin failing marks `contender:fonts-failed`). `web/src/level.ts` (on the wide level, the page's copy line: with `?readout`, `contenders/tools.ts`'s reporter handed to the wasm's `tools`, and `__contenderTools` over its `toolsCopy` and `toolsCancel`) is the level's host, which `vite.config.ts` replaces with `web/src/base.ts` on the base level, `web/src/market.ts` on the market level, `web/src/tail.ts` on the tail level, and `web/src/wall.ts` on the wall level (the stop's tables, the grid, market watch's numbers over the stop's desks' decimals, the desks' names, and the feed's worker with the stop's prices; its replay `open()` alone, resolving at the next `filled`) (the error color and the widths, the whole file's fetch, the clock's worker, with `?measure` `__contender.pause`, and with `?readout` the follow toggle's messages): its numbers (`scenarios.ts`'s flash and signs, `feed.ts`'s `decimalsOf` per instrument), `?ticks=<n>` (the feed's limit; anything but a count a failure), and the worker's `new Worker(new URL(…))`, so no other build carries it. `web/` (Vite, base `/observatory/contenders/tall-table/gpui/`, a level's under it): `src/main.ts` creates the bridge (`extensionDir: 'duckdb'`, `extensionBase: '/observatory/'`), sets `first-pixels` and `usable` at the placeholder's paint and the first frame's, passes the page's theme to `start` and each later one to `setTheme` (`contract.rs`: `seiza::stop_following_system_appearance`, then gpui-kit's `Theme::change`; without one the window follows the system), and defines the replay hooks; `vite.config.ts` has the placeholder (the frame and its line: `.sz-frame`) and the preloads (the wasm, and the font at `/fonts/`).

## Commands

Its checks (`cold`, `placeholder`, `sizes`, `gpui-parts`, `cell-fixture`, and each level's) are in `perf/CLAUDE.md`.

```
just observatory gpui-dev [level]     # the GPUI contender: debug wasm + Vite dev server at /observatory/contenders/tall-table/gpui/ (a level's under it: `wide`, `market`, `tail`, `wall`), with the engine, the font, and the data files by range
just observatory gpui-build [level]   # its release build at a level (`base`, `wide`, `market`, `tail`, `wall`): wasm alone (-p, the level's feature) + wasm-opt into web/pkg[-<level>]/, Vite build into web/dist[-<level>]/, asset sizes (the base's wasm with the crate's `fonts`)
just observatory gpui-preview [level] # serve that build (brotli), with the data files
```
