# M14 part C2: the TanStack side's copy (2026-11-12)

Checked on Apple M1, Darwin 25.6.0 arm64, machine `local`, Playwright's Chromium, Firefox, and WebKit, 1440×900 at DPR 2 (the copy line's page and touch checks at DPR 1). Nothing timed but the contract's marks: sizes, fixtures, parity, and the copied text.

Raw: `2026-11-12-m14-c2-tanstack-sizes-wide.json` (`tanstack-sizes --level wide --write-baseline`, then `--check`), `2026-11-12-m14-c2-wide-tools.json` (`wide --only tools`, both sides), `2026-11-12-m14-c2-tanstack-contract.json` (`tanstack-contract`, every level, its `wide` section new), `2026-11-12-m14-c2-tanstack-parity.json` (`tanstack-parity`, every level, its wide tools with Cmd+C), `2026-11-12-m14-c2-tanstack-parity-chromium.json` (its Chromium rerun).

## What changed

- The TanStack contender's wide level gains the copy: `data/copy.ts` (the table's copy, its reads beside the cache, ported as functions behind `__LEVEL__ === 'wide'`), and `copy.ts` (the bridge's clipboard writer and the GPUI host's `wide.rs` on the DOM: the keys, the confirming key, Escape, the marks, `__contender.copy`, the copy line). `pages.ts`'s `plan` grants the copy's reads; `paging.ts` plans them (`loadWantedCopy`), a release plans for a running copy, and `close` drops it.

## Experiments and controls

- **The copy fixture** (`node --test`, `fixtures.test.ts`, at the wide level): `copy.json`'s 30 cases pass against the data layer's copy (the first page read for the view, every read answered at once and counted), and its options are `COPY_OPTIONS`. Each control fails every case it names: `null-as-text`, `no-quoting`, `key-limit-off-by-one`, `whole-floats-bare`, `trailing-break`, `utf16-bytes`. A FLOAT column's copy is its float32 text (`0.1`), as GPUI's engine gives it; as a double it would be `0.10000000149011612` (the control). The contender's tests: 140 pass, 1 skipped (`sort.json`).
- **The same text on both sides** (`wide --only tools`, Chromium, at `rows-20`), each pasted text native DuckDB's to the byte, on both sides:
  - The week (Jan 1–7, a header press and Shift+press): 74,424 cells, 346,361 characters.
  - A block across the pinned edge: 7 rows × 4 columns.
  - The sheet: Cmd+A, Cmd+C declined (its mark alone), declined again after a selection change (the control), then Cmd+C again written: 18.0 MiB, `__contender.copy`'s bytes DuckDB's.
  - Each text's SHA-256 the same on both sides: week, block, and sheet.
  - The marks in order (started → copied → written), Ctrl+C on a cell, Cmd+Shift+C copying nothing, `layout` held through a scroll, none of the globals without `?measure`.
- **The copy line** (`wide --only tools`, a parent page in Chromium, Firefox, and WebKit): 6 messages for a 12-cell block and the button's copy (0, 1, 12 cells; copying, writing, written), as GPUI's; the paste DuckDB's text. Without `?readout`: no message, no `__contenderTools`.
- **The contract** (`tanstack-contract`, Chromium): a block's Cmd+C marks copy-started +0.5 ms, copied +0.8 ms, copy-written +25.8 ms from the key's event, started before the next frame (in the key's task); the sheet's key copy-declined alone, then Cmd+C again started it, and Escape cancelled it (`failed`, `cancelled`, no written mark, the selection kept). Without `?measure`: a block's copy written (the clipboard holds its 4 × 3 cells) and the sheet's key declined, with no copy mark and no `__contender.copy`.
- **The parity** (`perf/parity-tools.ts`, GPUI in Chromium): Cmd+C on a block pasted GPUI's 44 characters in Chromium, Firefox, and WebKit; with nothing selected, Cmd+C set no copy mark on either side (the control).

- **The review's fixes** (all eight of `/code-review high`'s findings) came before the final runs. The sizes, `wide --only tools`, `tanstack-contract`, and `parity-tools` above are of the fixed build; the four other levels are still byte-identical to HEAD's. The fixes: the copy's columns are the file's, a FLOAT copied as float32; the events after a throwing handler still told in order; one `MessageChannel` a page; the copy line's reporter made once; one range helper; the unmeasured contract copying a real block; the shared `copyMarks`; the port in the list.
- **The rest of `tanstack-parity`** (run before the review's fixes) passed in all three browsers but for two failures in the base level's Chromium tooltip timing. There the script itself took 475 ms to move on, past the 300 ms grace it tests, so the failure was the machine's timing, not the tooltip's. The base build is byte-identical to HEAD's. The Chromium rerun passed everything (`m14-c2-tanstack-parity-chromium`).

- **`check`** (on the fixed build): every step passed but the last, `page --check`. Its checks all passed, and its first paint read over the 72.5 ms budget: a median of 94.2 ms (69.8–153.5 across the 10 runs), then 82.5 ms (57.2–110.4) run alone. The load average was 10 on the 8-core machine: the CPU was busy. Nothing under `web/`, `site/`, or `shared-web/` changed this part.
