Raw: `2026-11-12-m14-c1-tanstack-sizes-wide.json` (`tanstack-sizes --level wide --check`), `2026-11-12-m14-c1-tanstack-parity.json` (`tanstack-parity`, every level, its `wideTools` section the new checks), `2026-11-12-m14-c1-wide-tanstack.json` (`wide --side tanstack`: every stop's layout and heat, both themes, with the new CSS).

## Experiments and controls

- **The fixtures** (`node --test`, `fixtures.test.ts`): `selection.json`'s 13 scenarios pass against `Selector`, and each of its five controls (`loop`, `center`, `plain-moves-head`, `extend-across`, `keep-past-rows`) fails it. `cells.json`'s `resize` passes against the layout, the drag's clamp equal to the layout's at each width. A budget a character off either way fails it, and so do widths without the clamps. 109 tests pass, 2 skipped (`copy.json`, `sort.json`).
- **The tools against GPUI's** (`perf/parity-tools.ts`, the GPUI side in Chromium, at `rows-20`), the same in Chromium, Firefox, and WebKit:
  - Fits: Jan 1 46 px and Station 195 px (GPUI's 46, 195).
  - Jan 1 dragged 400 px left: clamped at 31 px (GPUI's 31). A drag out 50 px and back to the press: still 31 on both sides.
  - Twenty selection steps (a cell, Shift+press, a drag, a header, Shift+header, the gutter, Shift+press in rows, arrows, Shift+arrows, Page Down, Shift+↑, End, Cmd+↑, Escape twice, the corner, Cmd+A): GPUI's selection and top row after each.
  - A page key moves by each side's own page. TanStack's view holds 42.3 rows, GPUI's 41.5, because headless overlay scrollbars take no height and GPUI draws its own. Page Down moves 41 rows against 40, as the base parity allows.
  - The tint's box, from the pixels a selection changed, for a block across the pinned edge and one in the days: GPUI's to the pixel ([228, 97, 615, 237] CSS px for the first). Control: a block a row off differs.
- **The bands** (TanStack, three browsers):
  - Jan 1's and Place's hairline sits at its column's right edge less a pixel, the cursor is `col-resize`, and a press there finds the band. Control: 40 px left is another band.
  - Mid-drag, Jan 1's header cell is 50 px wider before the release, with no widths published until the release.
  - At the right end, the last day narrowed 40 px with a trackpad's wheel meanwhile (3 px sideways, 120 down): the rows moved, the sideways scroll held mid-drag, and it was clamped below that at the release.
  - Place narrowed 150 px: the scrolled names reach past the view's right (1,577 px against 1,423 in Chromium), with no strip left undrawn.
  - Two ↓ in one task from the last whole row: the active row two on, flush at the bottom (the top 1.7 rows, as computed).
- **The review's fixes** (all nine of `/code-review high`'s findings) came before the final runs: the sizes above, the tools, and the whole `tanstack-parity` and `wide --side tanstack` are of the fixed build. Sharing the layout's clamp first moved the base's bundle (Rollup kept the helper as a function), so `layOut` keeps its inline copy and the test pins the two equal; the four other levels are byte-identical to HEAD's again.
- **The rest of `tanstack-parity`** passed with the new CSS, including the wide level's run held to the pixel under a sideways and a vertical scroll.
