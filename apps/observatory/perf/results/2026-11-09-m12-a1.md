# M12 part A1: the countries file, its scripts' fonts, and the capabilities' checks (2026-11-09)

Measured on Apple M1, Darwin 25.6.0 arm64, machine `local`: native DuckDB (`@duckdb/node-api` 1.4.5-r.1, `data/`'s lockfile) for the file; fontTools for the fonts' coverage; Playwright 1.63.0's Chromium, headless, 1440×900 at DPR 2, for the capabilities' probe. Not a protocol run: nothing here is timed.

Raw: none. The derivation is `data/countries.ts` and `data/prep-countries.sql` (drafts, which part B wires into `prep.ts`); the fonts' sizes and the capabilities' probe were one-off scripts, their results below.

## What changed

Nothing built in the contenders. `data/countries.ts` (the rows: regions, names, flags, capitals, the population's text) and `data/prep-countries.sql` (the file) drafted.

## The file

By `data/countries.ts`'s rules (decided with the user: [`docs/DECISIONS.md`](../../docs/DECISIONS.md), "Scenarios", 2026-11-09):
- **The regions:** CLDR's two-letter regions, minus the macro-regions (EU, EZ, UN, QO), the unknown region (ZZ), the pseudo-locales' (XA, XB), and ISO 3166's exceptional reservations (AC, CP, CQ, DG, EA, IC, TA). Kosovo (XK, user-assigned) stays. **250 rows.**
- **The name:** the region's name in its likely language, by the likely-subtags algorithm (`und-<region>`, else `und`'s `en-Latn`), from the CLDR locale `<lang>-<Script>` or the bare language when that's its own likely script. Ten languages have no region names in CLDR (Samoan, Papiamento, Haitian Creole, Dhivehi, Tok Pisin, Palauan, Guaraní, Tokelauan, Tuvaluan, Bislama; Cocos Malay's `ms-Arab`): those regions take their most-spoken official language that has them, else English. Only the Maldives falls to English, so Thaana isn't in the file.
- **The flag:** the code's letters as regional indicators.
- **The capital:** every best-rank capital of every item with the code, alphabetical, joined by `, ` (eight regions have several: Bolivia "La Paz, Sucre", Sri Lanka "Colombo, Sri Jayawardenepura Kotte", South Africa "Bloemfontein, Cape Town, Pretoria", and Eswatini, Mayotte, Montserrat, Palestinian Territories, Yemen); empty text for the ten with none (Antarctica, Bouvet Island, Caribbean Netherlands, Heard & McDonald Islands, Hong Kong, Macao, Svalbard & Jan Mayen, Tokelau, U.S. Outlying Islands, Western Sahara). AQ and CY each match two items (Antarctica and the Antarctic Treaty area; two Cyprus items), and their capitals agree.
- **The population:** CLDR's figure as text in the canvas's form: two decimals in B (`1.42 B`), whole M from 100 M, one decimal below it (`3.0 M`), two under a million (`0.36 M`), and `< 0.01 M` under 5,000, each band starting where the one below would round into it (17 boundary cases checked). 16 regions read `< 0.01 M`, among them Bouvet Island and Heard & McDonald Islands, uninhabited, which CLDR gives a population of 1. A first draft in three significant figures with K, M, or B (`2.98 M`, `364 K`) was dropped for the canvas's form.
- **The columns:** `flag`, `name`, `in English`, `capital`, `population`, all `VARCHAR`; sorted by the English name, accents and case aside (`COLLATE NOACCENT.NOCASE`), the code breaking ties.
- **Size:** 8,226 bytes, one row group, byte-identical across two builds (SHA-256 `8e46b48d…5b2e`). The longest name and English name are 40 characters, the longest capital 34.

## The capabilities' probe

Both contenders' base builds on the asteroids file (the countries file isn't served yet), Chromium, each check with a control: a `<p>` with a word of its own, added to the same page.

| Check | TanStack | GPUI | Control, both sides |
|---|---|---|---|
| `window.find("Ceres")` (Ctrl+F's proxy) | found | not found | its word found; a word not on the page not found |
| A drag across the first row | `1 20000001 1 Ceres (A801 A…` selected | nothing selected | its word selected |
| CDP's accessibility tree, a node named "Ceres" | `StaticText`, `InlineTextBox` | none | `StaticText` |
| `document.body.innerText` holds "Ceres" (what Translate reads) | yes | no | — |

GPUI's tree is a `Canvas` and gpui-pre-web's IME mirror (`textbox`). **The TanStack contender has no table semantics:** its tree holds `generic` and `StaticText` alone (no `table`, `row`, `cell`, `columnheader`), so a screen reader reads its text, but not as a table with headers. The canvas says "A table, read cell by cell", which the contender doesn't do today: it gains ARIA table semantics at every level (decided with the user, [`docs/DECISIONS.md`](../../docs/DECISIONS.md), "Scenarios", 2026-11-09).

**Ctrl+F past the rendered rows.** On the same TanStack page, `window.find("Dynamene")` (asteroid 200, off screen) is not found, and `document.body.innerText` (3,033 characters) doesn't hold it: 34 rows are in the DOM. So a virtualized DOM table's search, Translate, and screen reader reach only the rows near the view. The contender renders every row of a table that fits in one page (1,024 rows: decided with the user, [`docs/DECISIONS.md`](../../docs/DECISIONS.md), "Scenarios", 2026-11-09), so the admin table's 250 are all in its DOM.
