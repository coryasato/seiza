# M13 part B2: the GPUI contender at the wall level (2026-11-10)

Measured on Apple M1, Darwin 25.6.0 arm64, machine `local`, Chromium headless=new, 1440×900 at DPR 2, median of 10, reference and throttled (root CLAUDE.md, "Measurement protocol"). The sizes are brotli at quality 11, deterministic.

Raw: `2026-11-10-gpui-cold-wall.json` (the level's cold runs, its baseline), `2026-11-10-m13-b2-wall.json` (`perf/wall.ts`, the GPUI half).

## What changed

- **The crate's feature `wall`:** the table's and the bridge's `custom-cells`, `live`, `format`, and `density` (`sizes` checks them against `LEVEL_FEATURES.wall`). Market watch's store, feed, and readout ring are shared with it. The host is the wall's own, `src/wall.rs` in the bare host's place, with `src/scene/wall.rs` its numbers, columns, store, and source.
- **The JS host:** `web/src/wall.ts` (the stop's numbers, the desks' names, the worker with the stop's prices), Vite's `--mode wall`, and `main.ts`'s replay (`open()` alone on the wall). `FeedStart` gains `prices`, the wall's stop's instruments, market watch's 50 when left out.
- **`scenarios.ts`:** `BUILT.gpui` gains `wall`, and the grid gains `oneColumnBelow` (768) and `phoneRows` (6).

## `perf/wall.ts`, GPUI

- **The fold:** the wall's fixture's 2,000 ticks at 8 tables. All 8 tables drew their 1,600 cells as the fixture has them, light and dark: exact and shown text, Chg % by sign, no flash left. The control (1,999 ticks) differs in 7 cells in each theme.
- **The flash:** 96 cells flashing in 6 of 6 tables at the default stop, Last alone, all at the mix.
- **The marks**, with 3 s under the feed at each stop. One smoke run at 1440×900 and DPR 1, not the measurement protocol: the times say each batch was paired with a near frame, and Shown in is part D's to measure:

  | Stop | Filled | Refreshed (sampled, right after a presented frame) | Batches (gaps) | Visible paired across their tables | send → mark p50, max |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | 4 | 1, presented | 154 (22 of 22) | 156 (0) | 156 of 156 | 12.5, 29.0 ms |
  | 6 | 1, presented | 154 (22 of 22) | 155 (0) | 154 of 155 | 14.0, 22.7 ms |
  | 8 | 1, presented | 154 (22 of 22) | 157 (0) | 156 of 157 | 16.4, 29.4 ms |

  Every batch carries a generation per table and every refreshed log an entry per table. `state.tables` is each stop's. A batch folded in the window's last frame may have no mark yet: the script allows two.
- **Unmeasured:** filled, no refreshed marks.
- **The phone** (390×844, 4 tables): a wheel over the first table scrolls it (top 120) and holds the column (the first title's pixels unchanged). A wheel over the top gap moves the column.
- **Failures:** the worker's 404 fails the view after filled. An unknown stop (`5`), an unknown scenario, `?ticks=x`, and the wall on the base build each fail the load.

## Experiments and controls

- **After the code review:** the probe's filled test is one macro (`contract.rs`'s `probe_filled!`, its expansion the bare host's expression), the ring's write one `Ring::push`, and the wall's frames and batches clone no entities or names. Market watch's, the base's, and the tail's wasm stay byte-identical to HEAD. Giving `seiza` a hook for the wall's refreshed mark was tried and dropped: a public non-generic function beside `mark_after_current_task_then` moved Tycho's wasm by 513 bytes at the same size (`ef2e6e40…` → `3dc790b4…`), `#[inline]` on it 61 bytes larger, and the control, an unused private function, left it as it was. So the wall queues its own microtask around seiza's `mark` (`docs/DECISIONS.md`, "GPUI contender").

- **Market watch's wasm, kept byte-identical:** its panic locations come from four of the crate's files (`contract.rs`, `host.rs`, `market.rs`, `scene/market.rs`: the paths in its strings). The wall's edits in those stay on their lines. The first build differed by 10 bytes, and reverting `contract.rs` alone restored the hash. Of its edits, `filled_mark`'s `cfg!` didn't change it. Wrapping `publish_state`'s `state` in a by-value `fn with_tables(state: Object) -> Object` did, and the by-reference `&Object → &Object` didn't.
- **The phone's tables at 196 px** showed 5⅓ rows: the table keeps 16 px for its horizontal scrollbar. At 212 px they show 6 (`docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Page").
