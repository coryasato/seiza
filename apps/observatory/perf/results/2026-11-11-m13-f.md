Measured on Apple M1, Darwin 25.6.0 arm64, machine `local`, Chromium 153 headless=new, 1440×900 at DPR 2. `burst` is one run per use, unthrottled (a peak, not a timing: `perf/burst.ts`). No new measurement of either contender's per-use metrics: the README shows part D2's numbers (`2026-11-11-m13-d2.md`) through `report`.

Raw: `2026-11-11-m13-f-burst.json` (the requests one visitor sends in the busiest 10 s, thirteen uses, counted at the server).

## What changed

- **The README** has the monitoring wall's section: what the scenario is (4 to 8 desks of 50 fictional instruments in one frame, one seeded feed at 2k updates a second a table, one canvas against a tree of elements a table), how keeps up and Update shown in read across the tables (a batch counted once every table it ticked has drawn it), where each side wins (GPUI: keeping up on a slow device, through 6 tables against TanStack's 4, and 80% against 43% at 8; lower work per frame; an update shown sooner on a slow device; 3–4 collections in 10 s against 22–33. TanStack: ready sooner, a fraction of the memory and the bytes), and where they're even (smoothness and Update shown in on a fast device, time to data, first pixels on a fast device). Three generated tables, as market watch's: each stop, the runs' range of Update shown in, and once at 6 tables (memory once the ticks are drawn less every canvas buffer, and the runs with a second buffer, as the admin table's). It says the wall was measured on gpui-kit 0.9.1 and the other scenarios on 0.9.0, with the A/B that found them no different. The intro names the sixth scenario and the status bar's Shown in on the wall; "Canvas tradeoffs" (root rule 4) gains the phone's column, which on GPUI scrolls only from a title, a gap, or its sides, while TanStack's nested scrollers chain; "Data credits" gains the wall's 400 instruments (CC0 1.0, synthetic).
- **`perf/budgets.json`:** 54 of the wall's recorded metrics show in the README's three `readme:monitoring-wall*` sections. The rest (the collection pauses' time, longest, and those in a late frame; one canvas buffer; the runs measured) feed the page's cards only.
- **`burst` covers the wall:** `wall` (its route; each side at every stop under the feed, 2 s a stop) and `wall-reloads` (five reloads of its route).

No build changed in this part: `burst.ts`, the README, and `budgets.json` run in none. Every level was rebuilt from HEAD (`just observatory builds`) before the burst and the deploy. The budgets are part E's full `check` (E8), on the same code.

## The deploy

`just observatory deploy` from this session (2026-11-11): the builds staged with a quality-11 `.br` beside each build file (DuckDB's two oversize wasm left out, served as `.br` and `.gz`), `wrangler deploy` with `DATA_FILES` now naming `wall-instruments.parquet`, then `worker-check --wait`. **PASS:** 101 build files, 27.1 MiB on the wire as br, each exactly the staged `.br`, among them the wall level's builds on both sides and the wall's route; the two oversize wasm as `.gz` without br; a 304, the redirects, a 404; each of the six data files a ranged HEAD and `bytes=0-0` → 206, its closed ranges byte-exact, no Content-Encoding, its SHA-256 the `MANIFEST.json` pin (`wall-instruments.parquet`: 3,716 bytes, `5cbf2407…`, 7 closed ranges).
