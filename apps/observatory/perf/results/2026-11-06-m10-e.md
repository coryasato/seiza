# M10 part E: market watch on the page (2026-11-06)

Measured on Apple M1, Darwin 25.6.0 arm64, machine `local`, Chromium 153.0.8010.12 headless=new. These are checks and one small A/B, not a protocol run: the cold numbers the page shows are part D's (`2026-11-06-m10-d2.md`), resolved at build time. No JSON files: `page --check` ran without `--label`, and the A/B was a scratch script, not kept.

## What changed

- The page shows market watch: its route, title bar, status bar, panel, description, and the galleries' card and rule.
- The readout's ring (`docs/contender-contract.md`, "The readout's ring"), which a frame keeps only with `?readout`, so the bare cold runs are unchanged. Sizes, brotli, release builds of the market level, HEAD against this change:
  - GPUI's wasm: 2,790.6 → 2,792.6 KiB (+2.0).
  - TanStack's entry: 75.5 → 75.6 KiB; its `quotes` chunk (the market level's table, after first paint) 4.5 → 4.7 KiB.
  - Neither side's base or wide build changes but for the rate stops' label in the scenario list every build carries (identical to HEAD with it reverted).
- The page's first-paint JS: about +1.5 KB raw (main chunk 303.3 → 304.8 kB), ~0.4 KiB brotli.

## Experiments and controls

- The control: neither bare page (no `?readout`) keeps a ring once filled (`page --check`).
- Dropping every rAF of the top page from its start hangs it (it creates its frame after its first paint, through rAF), so the "off" variant drops them only once the ring is filling.
