## The deploy

`just observatory deploy` from this session (2026-11-06): the builds above staged (55 files, 20.7 MiB on the wire as br; DuckDB's two oversize wasm as `.gz`), `wrangler deploy`, then `worker-check --wait`. The Worker served everything right; the check failed 14 times on `market-instruments.parquet` alone, and the fault was the check's: its closed ranges were sized for multi-MiB files (a 64 KiB tail, a 1 MiB readahead, 4 KiB spots), so on a 4,078-byte file it asked for a range starting before byte 0, one past the end (the Worker's 416 is right), and spots running past the end (the Worker's clamped 206s are right), and expected their full lengths. `worker/scripts/check.ts` now keeps each range inside the file and drops one starting past its end. Re-run: **PASS**, every build file exactly the staged `.br`, a 304, the redirects, a 404, and each of the three data files a ranged HEAD and `bytes=0-0` → 206, its closed ranges byte-exact (8, 8, and 7 for the market file), no Content-Encoding, its SHA-256 the `MANIFEST.json` pin.
