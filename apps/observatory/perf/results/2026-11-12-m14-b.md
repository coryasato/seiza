Measured on Apple M1, Darwin 25.6.0 arm64, machine `local`, Chromium headless=new, 1440×900 at DPR 2, median of 10, reference and throttled (root CLAUDE.md, "Measurement protocol"). Load average not recorded before the cold run; 2.7 after the part's runs.

Raw: `2026-11-12-gpui-cold-wide.json` (the wide build's cold load, reference and throttled, written as `levels.wide`'s new baseline), `2026-11-12-m14-b-sizes.json` (`sizes --check`: each level's Cargo features and shipped wasm), `2026-11-12-m14-b-wide-tools.json` (`wide --side gpui --only tools`: the tools' checks, the copy line from a parent page in three browsers, touch).

## Experiments and controls

- **The tools** (`perf/wide-tools.ts`, at `rows-20`): Jan 1's band dragged 60 px (68 → 128 px, `widths` and the layout agreeing), double-clicked (fitted to 46 px); Place's band dragged 2,000 px right, held at 511 px, the pinned run 668 px of the cells' 1,336 (half); a selection drag landing on the cells pressed; the week (74,424 cells, 346,361 characters) and a block across the pinned edge (Place to Jan 2, 7 rows) pasted as native DuckDB's values to the byte; the sheet's Cmd+A then Cmd+C declined (`copy-declined` alone), declined again after a selection change in between (the control), then Cmd+C again written, its 18.0 MiB DuckDB's to the byte; Ctrl+C; Cmd+Shift+C setting no mark; marks started → copied → written for every copy. The control: without `?measure` none of `layout`, `widths`, `selection`, `copy` is published.
- **`layout` never in a frame:** the same object through a scroll of 1,200 px down and sideways (20 wheels). The table emits `Layout` only when its layout changes, so a measured run's frames carry no publication of it; the A/B the part allowed for was moot.
- **The copy line** from a parent page on the frame's origin, in Chromium, Firefox, and WebKit: 6 messages for a 12-cell block and a copy (0, 1, and 12 cells, then copying, writing, written), the button's copy pasted as DuckDB's in each; without `?readout`, no message and no `__contenderTools` (the control). Chromium first refused the button's write (the frame's document unfocused after the press); the frame now focuses itself before the copy ([`DECISIONS.md`](../../docs/DECISIONS.md), "Page").
- **Touch** (Chromium, `hasTouch`): a tap selected; a touch drag on Jan 1's band resized nothing, as the TanStack side's bands take a mouse or a pen alone ([`DECISIONS.md`](../../docs/DECISIONS.md), "Scenarios").
- `perf/wide.ts --side gpui` whole: every stop's layout and heat in both themes, the failures, then the tools, passed; `placeholder --level wide` matched in both themes at DPR 2 and 1.
- `just observatory check` on this tree: every step passed, three on a rerun after a timing reading on code this part doesn't build (live tail's clock, 3.95 rows off the rate between two stamps; the wall's TanStack side, 3 visible batches without a frame of their own within 50 ms; the page's first paint 73.6 ms against 72.5, then 69.7, its runs 50–83 ms at load 4.2). The wide wasm moved 0.4 KiB raw after the first `check` (the writer kept for a copy the next table started, if the old one is released late), so the tools, `sizes`, and the cold baseline were run again on that build. The review's fixes (the confirm a second Cmd+C, the writer told at the close, the copy line's stale fields) moved it again, to 2,798.6 KiB; the other four levels stayed byte-identical to HEAD's, and `wide --side gpui` and `sizes --check` were run on the final build (`m14-b-wide-tools`, `m14-b-sizes`).
