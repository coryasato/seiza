# M10 part B3: the GPUI contender at the market level (2026-11-05)

Measured on Apple M1, Darwin 25.6.0 arm64, machine `local`, Chromium headless=new. The cold runs at 1440×900 and DPR 2, median of 10, reference and throttled (root CLAUDE.md, "Measurement protocol"); `market` at DPR 1 (2560×1700 for the fold, so every row and column draws). Load average about 2.2 through the session.

Raw: `2026-11-05-gpui-cold-market.json` (the level's cold load, written as its baseline, after the code review's fixes; the three files were rewritten under the same labels then, replacing the review's input), `2026-11-05-m10-b3-sizes.json` (each level's shipped wasm and Cargo features, the panel's variants, the home-path control), `2026-11-05-m10-b3-market.json` (the level's check: the fold, the flash, Shown in's marks).

## What changed

- The GPUI contender builds the market level (`--features market`: the table's and the bridge's `custom-cells`, `live`, and `format`): the instruments opened through the engine, then every re-read answered from the feed's store, drawn as the canvas writes it. Its wasm is **2,790.6 KiB brotli, +20.4 KiB** over the base's 2,770.2 (the wide level's 2,776.7). Of that, the table's features alone in its bare host: `custom-cells` +4.2, `live` +4.4, the two together +12.8, `format` +3.8 KiB brotli (`components/tall-table/perf/results/2026-11-05-m12-c-size.json`); the rest is the host's (the store, the source, the scene, the feed's glue).
- The base's and the wide level's wasm: the same size as before (10,142,558 and 10,160,705 B after wasm-opt), differing in 11 and 13 bytes, each a panic location's line number moved by a `cfg(feature = "market")` block above it (184 → 188, 334 → 352, …). Brotli 2,770.3 → 2,770.2 and 2,776.6 → 2,776.7 KiB.
- The lists by side (`ALL_SCENARIOS`, `BUILT`): market watch's entry in the one scenario list every contender bundle carries, at brotli 11: the GPUI base's entry chunk 23,565 → 23,684 B (+119), the wide's 23,570 → 23,699 (+129), TanStack's base 75,072 → 75,195 (+123), its wide 79,034 → 79,215 (+181). The market build's entry chunk is 24,882 B (its host module and the feed's instruments), and its worker a chunk of its own, loaded after the table fills.

## Experiments and controls

- **The fold on screen** (`market`): with `?ticks=2000`, all 600 cells (50 instruments × 12 columns) match the feed fixture's fold, exact text and formatted text, the sign colors within one 8-bit step, no flash left, light and dark. The control, `?ticks=1999`, differs in 6 cells (TDLW's Bid, Bid size, Ask size, exact and shown), the fixture's last tick.
- **The flash under the feed** (5k): 150 cells flashing, all in Last, Bid, and Ask, each the rise's or the fall's color at the 30% mix.
- **Shown in's marks**, 3 s at each stop with `?measure`: every sampled `contender:refreshed` (22–23 a stop) right after a presented frame; each visible batch paired with its own frame (the first mark whose generation reaches its own, after its send and within 50 ms): 125 of 125 (100), 156 of 157 (500), 155 of 155 (1k), 153 of 154 (5k), the one short folded in the window's last frame; send → mark p50 10.8–12.7 ms, at most 22.4. The feed's ticks: 298, 1,499, 2,980, 14,956 in the window, the stops' rates. Unmeasured, no refreshed mark is set.
- **The feed failing:** the worker's chunk answered 404 after the open, the view fills, then marks `contender:failed`.
- **Cargo's features per level** (`sizes`): base none; wide `custom-cells`, `density`, `pin`; market `custom-cells`, `format`, `live`, the table's and the bridge's alike, each `LEVEL_FEATURES`.
- **Failures:** an unknown stop, an unknown scenario, `?ticks=x`, and market watch on the base build each mark `contender:failed`.
- **The placeholder** at the market level matches its first frame, light and dark, DPR 2 and 1.
