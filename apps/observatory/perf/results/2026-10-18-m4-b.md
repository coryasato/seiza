# M4 part B: the runs (2026-10-18)

The comparison's numbers: both bare contender pages, cold, median of 10, reference and throttled, the contenders alternated (GPUI first in odd rounds, TanStack in even ones), every metric from `metrics/metrics.ts`. Measured on Apple M1, Darwin 25.6.0 arm64, machine `local`, Playwright 1.63.0's Chromium headless=new, 1440×900 at DPR 2 (root CLAUDE.md, "Measurement protocol"). The user's browsers were closed. Load average 1.2–2.6 through the runs, except the first `usable` run (below). Every run passed every guard: 0 failures in each file.

Raw (`just observatory metric <name> --runs 10 --label m4-b-<name>`). Scripts date files in UTC, and the runs crossed midnight UTC, so some files carry 2026-10-19:
- `2026-10-19-m4-b-usable.json`: time to first usable, confirmed by the probe key, and first pixels. `2026-10-18-m4-b-usable-loaded.json`: the first run, outside the protocol (below).
- `2026-10-18-m4-b-data.json`: time to data.
- `2026-10-18-m4-b-response.json`: input to visible response, 6 inputs of each kind per run (60 per kind, side, and profile).
- `2026-10-19-m4-b-fling.json`: behavior under load.
- `2026-10-18-m4-b-reach.json`: reach and correctness.
- `2026-10-19-m4-b-cost.json`: main-thread CPU, memory, and bytes over a session.
- `2026-10-18-m4-b-sizes.json`, `2026-10-18-m4-b-tanstack-sizes.json`: the bundles (footnotes).

## Where each side wins

- **TanStack:** first usable, by 3.4× at reference and 7.1× throttled (its prerendered screen hydrates from 72 KiB of JS; GPUI's canvas waits for 2.7 MiB of wasm). Page memory (a twelfth of GPUI's at the end of a throttled session) and page bytes (180 KiB against 2.8 MiB). A wheel's first frame, 16 against 34 ms: the compositor scrolls before the main thread runs. End → last row, by 6 ms and 46 ms. Main-thread CPU under throttling, 2207 against 3319 ms. A key under throttling, by 2.6 ms. Bundle: 106.9 KiB of JS against 2695.7 KiB of wasm.
- **GPUI:** keeping the rows up under load. Throttled, 27.6% of TanStack's main-thread fling frames were late against 0.3% of GPUI's, and its rows trailed by 5 steps (p95) against 2. Both readings are the main thread's (`underLoad` samples the state each animation frame): on the DOM the compositor keeps scrolling the content through a late main-thread frame, so a late frame there is a late update of the rows, not a stalled scroll. On GPUI the main thread's frame is the presented frame. Per-frame work p95 is lower at both profiles. Rows real after a track press (18.8 against 30.8 ms) and a wheel (34.1 against 43.5 ms). Main-thread CPU at reference, 953 against 1560 ms. First pixels throttled, 244 against 391 ms; at reference, 37.8 against 42.0 ms, inside both sides' 15–16% spread.
- **Even:** time to data, within 1%, and reach on every run.

## What the runs showed

- **The two sides spend their frames differently under throttling.** TanStack's median fling frame does 0.28 ms of main-thread work (the compositor scrolls, and nothing re-renders), but its p95 is 24.4 ms: the frames where TanStack Virtual re-renders the rows run past a 4×-slowed refresh, so 27.6% are late (the compositor's scroll goes on through them). GPUI does 12.6 ms every frame, the same each time, and misses almost none. So TanStack is cheaper in total under throttling (less CPU) and worse at keeping its rows up (more late main-thread frames).
- **Under throttling Chromium coalesced more of TanStack's wheel events:** the page got 155–174 of the 180 (reference: 179–180). Their deltas still add up to the whole fling in every run (the script checks it).
- **GPUI's first usable, throttled, is its wasm's download.** It lands within 0.2% of 3.48 s in every run, as the cold TTFP does (`2026-10-16-m2-b-gpui-cold.json`, 3502.5 ms). The page is usable at its first frame.
- **First pixels:** the GPUI side's HTML placeholder paints sooner than TanStack's prerendered screen when throttled. Both are HTML. The TanStack line's Element Timing paint may be waiting on the full IBM Plex Sans (69 KiB brotli), where GPUI's placeholder uses `shared-web`'s subset. This wasn't tested here.
- **A throttled fling reads less data** on both sides (5937 against 21,904 KiB): it skips more pages, the same ones on both sides.

## Experiments and controls

- **`usable`, first run:** it ran right after both builds and the size scripts, at load average 5.9. Its medians (GPUI 139.7, TanStack 41.0 ms reference; 3477.2 and 480.9 throttled) agree with the rerun's within 2%, but it ran outside the protocol, so it's kept as `-loaded` and excluded. The rerun at load 2.2–3.0 is the published one.
- The negative controls for every guard ran in part A and in `just observatory check`. Here each guard ran on every reading: the usable probe confirmed 40/40, GPUI's key `EventLatency` guard 20/20, every input reading 720/720, and the fling's summed-delta check 40/40.

## Reproducing it

```sh
just observatory gpui-build && just observatory tanstack-build
just observatory sizes --label m4-b-sizes && just observatory tanstack-sizes --label m4-b-tanstack-sizes
for m in usable data reach response fling cost; do just observatory metric $m --runs 10 --label m4-b-$m; done
just observatory report
```
