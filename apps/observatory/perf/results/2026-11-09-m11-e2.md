# M11 part E2: the follow toggle (2026-11-09)

Measured on Apple M1, Darwin 25.6.0 arm64, machine `local`, Chromium 153.0.8010.12 headless=new. These are checks, not a protocol run: no cold number changes, and the page's are still part D2's (`2026-11-08-m11-d2.md`). No JSON files: `check`'s scripts print what they read.

## What changed

- Live tail's follow toggle: one Pause / Follow button beside the dial, the page's (`web/src/numbers.ts`'s `FollowToggle`, after first paint). The frame lets go or follows when the page asks (`{ seiza: 'follow', on }`: GPUI's `followTail` → the table's `let_go` or `follow`; TanStack's `follow.ts` `letGo` or `follow`), and tells the page whether it follows and the clock's rows (`{ seiza: 'following', on, rows }`, at a change and at most every 250 ms while let go), with `?readout` only (`contenders/contract.ts`'s `followToggle`; `docs/contender-contract.md`, "The follow toggle").
- The status bar names the scenario's file beside its rows, on every route.
- Every Memory explainer ends with Chrome leaving out GPUI's WebAssembly memory (`readouts.ts`'s `CHROME_MEMORY`).

## After the code review

Eight findings; six fixed, two answered.

- Fixed: the toggle disabled once the replay has stopped (a press then reached no table); a count held back by the 250 ms limit is sent when the time is up, so the last one arrives though the clock stops; `followToggle` in a module of its own (`contenders/follow.ts`), out of the TanStack entry; `page --check` reads the file's rows from the manifest (`cold.ts`'s `dataRows`), not the cold numbers; one ring reader (`ringSamples`, `ringAtLeast`) for the toggle's check and the readouts'; GPUI's `watch` reads following once.
- Answered: a report lost before the page's listener exists can't happen: the toggle mounts in the render that creates the frame, and its listener is added in that commit, before the frame's document loads. A stale table's `Following` can't reach the page: a replaced table is dropped with its subscription, and the page's frames make no replay `open()`. The Rust flag beside the JS reporter's stays: it spares a call into JS at every count while following.
- After: `page --check` PASS (the toggle on both sides, the control, the phone); `tail` PASS on a rerun (its first run failed one clock message 13.6 rows off the rate at a load average of 4.8, the worker's timing, untouched here); `tanstack-contract`, `sizes --check`, every level's `tanstack-sizes --check` PASS; the tail TTFP above.
