# M5 part D2: the window and the panel (2026-10-20)

The contender's card became the window part C designed (https://claude.ai/artifact/6TUq4g7KJ3jrFdKVUGRY67): a title bar (the toggle, the side's state, its explainer, the lead), the frame, and a status bar (Ready in, Rows in, Smooth, Memory; Meter, Run benchmark, Compare; on a phone Smooth, Memory, and an overflow). Compare opens the panel over the frame: five cards with both devices and computed verdicts, and the input-to-response chart. The description below the window generates "where each side wins". Measured on Apple M1, Darwin 25.6.0 arm64, machine `local`, Playwright's Chromium headless=new.

No JSON of its own: `check` writes none. The page's numbers below are `check`'s run.

## What changed

- `web/src/slot.ts` (the window), `explainer.ts` (new), `numbers.ts` (rewritten: the status bar's contents, the panel, the description), `readouts.ts` (the cards, the Download card's sources, `verdict`, `wins`), `cold.ts` (the parts, the rows, `resolveCold`), `page.ts`, `page.css`, `components.ts` (TallTable, the stacks' copy).
- `metrics/metrics.ts`: `liveReadings`' `pageMiB` is now less the canvas (`canvasMiB` apart), as the cold card's.
- `shared-web/src/prerender.ts`: a `plugins` option for its build-time server (the observatory's prerender needs `virtual:data-rows`). The TanStack contender's prerender is unchanged; its sizes held (`tanstack-sizes --check` below).
- `perf/page.ts`: the part B readouts' checks replaced by the window's.
- Meter and Run benchmark are in the bar, disabled ("coming soon"), until part D3 and M7.

## The checks (`perf/page.ts`, in `check`)

- **Nothing shifts:** the frame's box 683 px at first paint (the JS held back) and once the status bar's numbers render. Control, the HTML without the box's class: 713 → 697 px, FAIL as it should.
- **This tab:** GPUI Ready in 132 ms and Rows in 217 ms, equal to its frame's own marks; Smooth "—" until a wheel scroll, then 100 %; Memory 12.8 MiB, the frame's `JavaScript` type, as the cold reading. Idle 2 s: nothing but Memory changed. Scrolling: 2 refreshes in 1.75 s, and the title bar's live text didn't change.
- **Explainers:** a hover opens one and leaving closes it (after 150 ms, time to reach it); hovering another leaves a pinned one open; a press in the frame closes a pinned one; a press pins it and a second press unpins; pinning another closes the first; focus alone opens nothing; Enter and Space open; Esc closes with focus kept on the trigger. A phone: hover opens nothing, a tap does.
- **The panel:** every card's cold values on both devices match `perf/results/`, and so do "best" and the verdicts: Ready in "TanStack 3.4× faster · 7.1× on a slow device", Rows in "Even", Smoothness "GPUI smoother on a slow device", Memory "TanStack 1.6× less · 1.6× on a slow device", Download "TanStack 15.5× smaller" (2.7 MiB against 180 KiB). The chart has all 20 values. The header gets its edge once the body scrolls. Esc with an explainer open closes only the explainer, a second Esc closes the panel, and Esc and the close button both leave focus on the frame.
- **Flip:** TanStack's state reads "Ready · warm", and this tab's row shows GPUI's 132 ms kept and TanStack's 34.4 ms labelled warm.
- **Description:** "GPUI stays smoother on a slow device, moves the table sooner after a key or a scrollbar press, and fills the rows sooner after a scrollbar press or a wheel." "TanStack downloads less, is ready sooner, holds less memory, and moves the table sooner after a wheel." "Even: rows on screen after opening." Each equals `wins()` over the same numbers.
- **Phone:** Smooth and Memory in a 44 px bar, Compare in the overflow, which opens the panel; the touch limit in the description.
