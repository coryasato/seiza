# M10 part C2: the TanStack contender at the market level (2026-11-05)

Measured on Apple M1, Darwin 25.6.0 arm64, machine `local`, Chromium headless=new (Firefox and WebKit for `tanstack-parity`). Sizes are brotli at quality 11 from the release builds. `market` at DPR 1 (2560×1700 for the fold, so every row and column draws; 1440×900 for the marks); the contract and parity at the protocol's 1440×900, DPR 2 in Chromium. Load average about 3.4–4.3 through the session. No cold runs: the level's per-use numbers are part D's.

Raw: `2026-11-05-m10-c2-tanstack-sizes-market.json` (the level's JS, written as its baseline), `2026-11-05-m10-c2-market.json` (both sides' market check: the fold, the flash, Shown in's marks, the same on both), `2026-11-05-m10-c2-tanstack-contract.json` (the contract, the base's and the market level's), `2026-11-05-m10-c2-tanstack-parity.json` (parity in three browsers, the market level's sign colors, flash, and tooltip). Each was rewritten after the code review's fixes, under the same label.

## Experiments and controls

- **The fold on screen, both sides** (`market`): with `?ticks=2000`, all 600 cells on each side match the feed fixture's fold, exact and shown text, Change's and Change %'s colors the rise's or the fall's within one 8-bit step, no flash left, light and dark. The control, `?ticks=1999`, differs in 6 cells on each side (TDLW's Bid and sizes, the fixture's last tick). Between the sides: the 12 columns at the same `x` and `width`, and all 600 cells written the same.
- **The flash under the feed** (5k, both sides): 150 cells flashing, all in Last, Bid, and Ask, each the rise's or the fall's color at no more than the 30% mix.
- **Shown in's marks**, 3 s at each stop with `?measure`. TanStack: 131–134 refreshed marks a stop; each visible batch paired with its own commit (the first mark whose generation reaches the batch's, after its send and within 50 ms), 115 of 115, 131 of 131, 134 of 135, 131 of 131 (the one short folded in the window's last frame); send → mark p50 8.8–10.5 ms, at most 19.7. Every mark is set inside a frame's animation frame callback but one at 100 and one at 5k (in an earlier run, one at every stop): a commit outside one, a commit of the view's own rather than the level's frame commit (TanStack Virtual's re-render or a scroll's both run outside a rAF callback; which one wasn't pinned). That commit draws the landed re-read, and the next frame paints it, so the check allows two a run. GPUI in the same run: 22–23 sampled marks all right after a presented frame, 118–134 visible batches paired (134 of 135 at 1k), send → mark p50 12.0–13.6 ms. These send → mark times aren't Shown in (that's to the frame's presentation, from the trace: part D).
- **Unmeasured:** no `contender:refreshed`, no `__contenderRefreshed`, no `batches`, no state, no cell timing.
- **The feed failing:** the worker's chunk answered 404, the view fills, then marks `contender:failed`. An unknown stop, an unknown scenario, `?ticks=x`, and market watch on the base build each mark `contender:failed`.
- **The contract** (`tanstack-contract`, the market level): the first screen prerendered and hydrated in place; usable ≤ open < filled; the probe key 1.1 ms after its event; 50 rows; 66 batches in 1.5 s, in order from 0; 66 refreshed marks, the drawn generation the last batch's (534); End's lastCell XYLO; the replay's `open()` ends the feed (no batch after it on the old feed's array, none on the new table's) and fills; `fling()` ends at rows 22–50; framed, every mark relayed (19), the refreshed ones too.
- **Parity** (`tanstack-parity`, the market level, Chromium, Firefox, WebKit, light and dark): 336 cells on screen; 26 rises and 22 falls in their colors; 71–73 cells flashing, only in Last, Bid, and Ask, at the rise's or the fall's color and the mix; after a 200-tick feed, the 63–64 lit cells all fade. The tooltip (light): a rounded Change % (`+0.01%`) shows its exact text (`0.01187648456055927`), a dressed Last (`31.70`) none, in all three. The computed colors read the same in all three.
- **The probe's race, found by parity's first labeled run:** Chromium read one Change cell at −0.01 drawn in the foreground. The probe took each value from the paging when read, and a page that had landed in the frame's gap wasn't drawn yet. The cells now carry the exact text of the value they drew (`data-exact`, with `?cells` only), and the probe reads that. Every check above is from the runs after the fix.
- **The engine** (`engine-files --controls`): every level's `duckdb/` on both sides the same files as the GPUI base's; its controls fail as before.
