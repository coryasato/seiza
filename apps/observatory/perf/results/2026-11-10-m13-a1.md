Measured on Apple M1, Darwin 25.6.0 arm64, machine `local`, Chromium 153 headless=new, 1440×900 at DPR 2, cold (a fresh browser per run), median of 3. This is a spike that sizes the feed's path, not a protocol run: the numbers that publish come from part D. Load average ~3.2–3.7 over the runs (the machine in use).

Raw: `2026-11-10-m13-a1-feed-review-desks-4.json`, `-review-desks-6.json`, `-review-desks-8.json` (folds `objects`, `typed`, `wasm` at 4, 6, and 8 desks × 2k ticks a second, reference and throttled), measured after the code review's fixes (below), all from `spike/feed-run.ts --desks <n>` over `spike/feed.html`. It's market watch's feed spike ([M10 A1](2026-11-04-m10-a1.md)) widened:

## The instruments (A1-1)

- **400 fictional instruments,** the canvas's 8 desks of 50 (Energy, Rail and freight, Banks, Chips, Health, Retail, Materials, Utilities). Each is a symbol and a reference price, with no name: the wall draws none.
- **The symbols:** seeded pronounceable words (seed `0x3a11`) cut to the first letter and the next three consonants, as market watch's read as their names' letters (`RKND`, `BRLD`, `STND`).
  - Generating them rejected 110 candidates listed on a US exchange (NASDAQ Trader's two directories, 14,246 symbols, with the AAPL and IBM control), any of market watch's 50, and repeats.
  - They're pinned as literal text, so the file doesn't change when the directories do; prep checks them again at each build, as market watch's.
- **The prices:** log-uniform in a range per desk, to the cent: Energy 18–160, Rail and freight 14–130, Banks 12–95, Chips 25–480, Health 8–320, Retail 6–150, Materials 4–85, Utilities 22–115.
- **The draft file** (native DuckDB 1.4.5, ZSTD): `Desk` VARCHAR, `Symbol` VARCHAR, `Last` DOUBLE, `Change %` DOUBLE, `Volume` BIGINT; 400 rows in the feed's order (a desk is 50 rows in a run), 3,646 B, one row group.

## The feed (A1-2)

Every stop's ticks, uniform over the shown desks' instruments, in market watch's format (the u16 instrument holds 0–399).

| Desks × 2k | Delivered | Batches / s | Post → handler p50 | Generation p50 | Worker busy | Main thread busy, `objects` / `typed` / `wasm` | Digest (all runs) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4 (8k/s) | 100% | 52.0 | 0.18 ms | 0.06 ms | 16 ms/s | 10.5 / 9.5 / 10.5 ms/s | `4e9ccb7d` |
| 6 (12k/s) | 100% | 52.1 | 0.23 | 0.10 | 21 | 14.1 / 9.4 / 10.2 | `7ddb7168` |
| 8 (16k/s) | 100% | 52.0 | 0.22 | 0.12 | 23 | 15.3 / 9.4 / 10.1 | `8c545d85` |

Reference. The worker is idle most of its thread at 16k (market watch's held 50k at 32 ms a second). One transferred buffer a frame costs the same at ~150 ticks as at ~300.

**The fold of a batch, reference:**

| Desks | `objects`: µs a tick, ms a second | `typed` | `wasm` (the batch copied in) | Collections in 10 s, `objects` (longest) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | 0.41 µs, 3.3 ms/s | 0.15, 1.2 | 0.25, 2.0 | 12 (0.65 ms) |
| 6 | 0.42, 5.0 | 0.11, 1.3 | 0.19, 2.2 | 15 (0.77) |
| 8 | 0.37, 5.9 | 0.08, 1.3 | 0.15, 2.4 | 17 (0.94) |

- **The fold is cheap at every stop,** as market watch's was: the dial's cost will be in the drawing (A2).
- **Collections:** the idiomatic store (a new row object per tick, a new array a batch) collects 12–17 times in 10 s. The typed and Rust folds make no garbage. One collection showed in each at 8 desks (0.97 and 0.71 ms), which isn't the fold's.
- **`--agree`, at 8 desks and at market watch's 50:** `typed` and `wasm` hold the same store as `objects` after 20,000 ticks. The control, `objects` dropping tick 10,000, differs.

**The throttled profile reads faster here, as in market watch's A1:** the handler at ~¼–½ of the reference's time, and the worker less busy. On a page that draws nothing, the OS's scheduling of an idle renderer changes under the throttler's pauses ([M10 A1](2026-11-04-m10-a1.md), "The throttled profile"). Its counts hold: 100% delivered at every stop, 55–57 batches a second, and the same digests and collections. Its times aren't read.

**The module (decided for B):** one feed module, market watch's. `Feed` takes its instruments' prices (market watch's 50 its default, so market watch's ticks are unchanged and its fixture regenerates byte-identical, which `write.ts --check` proves in B). The worker's start message names the scenario's instruments and the stop's desks. The wall's 400 have their one home in `contenders/wall/`.

## The rate a desk (A1-3)

- **What changes a drawn cell:** the wall draws Last, Chg %, and Volume, so a quote (40% of ticks) changes no drawn cell. A trade always adds to Volume, and moves Last half the time.
- **What a frame redraws,** counted from the store's values (`changed.ts`), not from the ticks: the feed's own ticks, 3,000 frames at 60 Hz, over the visible rows only.
  - Cells changed counts only columns whose value moved: a trade that leaves Last where it was changes Volume alone.
  - Cells flashing counts those within 400 ms of a change: Last on the wall, Last, Bid, and Ask on market watch.
  - The wall has 8 desks × 16 rows on screen (a bare page's 900 px in two rows of tables); market watch has 29 of its 50 (30 px rows), an estimate A2 replaces with the contenders' own counts.

| | Rows changed a frame | Cells changed a frame | Cells flashing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wall, 1k a desk | 23.3 (18.2%) | 47.3 | 115.8 of 128 |
| Wall, 2k a desk | 42.3 (33.0%) | 86.7 | 126.5 of 128 |
| Wall, 5k a desk | 81.0 (63.3%) | 172.0 | 127.9 of 128 |
| Market watch, 100 | 0.9 (3.3%) | 4.4 | 26.5 of 87 |
| Market watch, 500 | 4.5 (15.4%) | 20.9 | 70.2 of 87 |
| Market watch, 1k | 8.3 (28.5%) | 39.1 | 83.1 of 87 |
| Market watch, 5k | 23.6 (81.4%) | 125.1 | 87.0 of 87 |

- **The share of rows changed doesn't depend on how many show:** the feed spreads a desk's ticks evenly over its 50.
- **At the canvas's 2k a desk:** 87 cells changed a frame across 8 tables, between market watch's 1k and 5k (39 and 125), where its edges fell. Nearly every Last on screen is flashing at any moment, more flashing cells than market watch ever has.
- **Kept for A2's spike,** which also runs 5k a desk if no edge falls between 4 and 8 tables.
- **The first version of this count was wrong** (before the code review). It counted trades rather than changed values, put 3 cells on every changed row, and left the flash out. Its market watch figures (~84 and ~240 cells) were estimates, not counts.

## Reproducing it

```sh
node apps/observatory/spike/wall/gen.ts --write    # replaces the pinned instruments.ts (fetches today's NASDAQ Trader directories: other instruments)
node apps/observatory/spike/wall/prep-draft.ts <out.parquet>
node apps/observatory/spike/wall/changed.ts
node apps/observatory/spike/wall/same-feed.ts
node apps/observatory/spike/feed-run.ts --agree --desks 8
node apps/observatory/spike/feed-run.ts --desks 8 --rates 16000 --folds objects,typed,wasm --runs 3 --label m13-a1-feed-review-desks-8
```
