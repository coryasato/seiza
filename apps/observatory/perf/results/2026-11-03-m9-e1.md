# M9 part E1: the routes, the dial, and a switch as a load (2026-11-03)

Measured on Apple M1, Darwin 25.6.0 arm64, machine `local`, Chromium 153 headless=new, 1440×900 at DPR 2; gpui-kit 0.9.0, React 19.3.1, TanStack Virtual 3.14.19, Arrow 17.0.0, DuckDB-Wasm 1.32.0. `page --check` only (reference, median of 10). Load average 5.49 → 8.25 over the run: the first paint below is a gate's reading, not a comparison.

Raw: none written (`--check` writes no file); the readings below are its output.

## What changed

- **The routes:** one per scenario from `contenders/scenarios.ts`: `/observatory/tall-table/big-file/` (the base level, the session the page showed at `/observatory/tall-table/` until now) and `/observatory/tall-table/wide-sheet/`. `/observatory/tall-table/` is the component's page: its name, its line, and a card per scenario, no frame, any parameter ignored.
- **The navigation:** the sidebar's TallTable row links its page, a chevron beside it opening its scenarios' list (open on its own routes, closed on the Overview), one item filled and the rest muted; the crumb "TallTable / Wide sheet", the scenario's name a menu of every scenario (its use under it, the names alone on a phone). A scenario's link carries the route's `?side=`, never `?stop=`.
- **The window:** each frame at its scenario's level with `?scenario=`, `?stop=`, and `?theme=`; the dial ("Cells on screen", ~380, ~570, ~710, ~940), each stop's parameters in its title and the dial's explainer with this window's count; on a phone a menu button. A stop destroys the frame and makes it again; the toggle and the dial stay disabled until it fills or fails. The state starts at "Loading the app…" (before `contender:usable`); its explainer lists this load's parts.
- **Size:** the page's first-paint JS 79.7 → 82.9 KiB brotli (the routes' copy, the crumb, the dial, the sub-list, two icons). Base UI's Collapsible, tried for the sidebar's list first, put it at 85.3 KiB: replaced by a disclosure of the page's own (`docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Page", 2026-11-03). No contender's build changed.

## After the code review

Fixed and checked again (`page --check` passes): a phone's dial menu closed before its pick (the pick disabled its trigger and replaced the frame its press-outside listened on); a scenario's link clearing a side an earlier press left on it; the load's explainer reading the frame only once its app is past loading (the switch's own render still holds the old frame), claiming nothing past the app on a failure (now checked: GPUI and TanStack with the data missing read "not read"), and saying why a load is warm (the cache, the frame's HTML counted as the state's rule now counts it, or a frame before it in this tab); one `hasCold` for the cold rows, Compare, and the description; the stops' labels built from their counts; Big file's route from `scenarioPath` in `burst` and `readout-cost`; the manifest read once; the old route's name gone from the justfile, `readout-cost`'s header, and the page's first-paint budget's label.

## Experiments and controls

- **Aborted reads on a switch:** a stop, a flip, or a navigation destroys a frame whose engine may be mid-read; its worker logs `FAIL WITH: Error: Range request … failed` to the console. A probe that changed the stop right after the fill logged it; one that waited 4 s didn't. The check allows one per frame torn down (9 in its scenario section) and fails on anything else.

## Reproducing it

```sh
just observatory build
```
