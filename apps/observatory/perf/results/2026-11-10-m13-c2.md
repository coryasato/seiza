# M13 part C2: the same on both sides at the wall level (2026-11-10)

Measured on Apple M1, Darwin 25.6.0 arm64, machine `local`, Chromium headless=new (Firefox and WebKit for `tanstack-parity`). These are checks, not the measurement protocol: `perf/wall.ts` runs at 1440×900 and DPR 1 (2560×2600 for the fold), and `tanstack-contract` at the protocol's page (1440×900, DPR 2). Shown in and the rest are part D's to measure.

Raw: `2026-11-10-m13-c2-wall.json` (`perf/wall.ts`, both sides and the same on both), `2026-11-10-m13-c2-tanstack-contract.json`, `2026-11-10-m13-c2-tanstack-parity.json`. All three are the runs after the code review.

## What changed

- **Where each wall table is, on both sides, with `?cells`:**
  - `__contender.tables[t].layout` is `{ left, top, rowHeight }`: its cells' left edge right of the row numbers, their top under the header, and the row height. On GPUI those three numbers come from the tall table's `TallTableLayout` (`wall.rs`, at `TallTableEvent::Layout`).
  - `__contender.tables[t].box` is the table's frame, `[x, y, width, height]`. On GPUI it's the table element's bounds at prepaint (`wall.rs`'s `probed`, gpui's `on_children_prepainted`, attached only with `?cells`).
  - On TanStack both are read from the DOM each time (`wall.ts`'s `publishWallCells`, getters, since a phone's column scrolls with no render): the scroller for `layout`, the `.frame` for `box`.
- **`perf/wall-grid.ts`, one home for what "the same grid" means:**
  - `gridDiffers`: each table's frame (its four edges within 1 px; GPUI lays out in whole device pixels), its cells' left and top (within 1 px: the gutter and the header), its row height, and its columns' x and width (within 0.5 px).
  - The grid's control (`controlHolds`): the same tables moved 2 px, which must differ in every table.
  - The wall's sign and flash rules (`signWrong`, `flashOff`).
  - `perf/wall.ts` and `tanstack-parity` both import it.
- **`perf/wall.ts`, the same on both sides:** each side's tables are read after the fold (light) and at each grid: the three stops at 1440×900 and a phone's column at 390×844, 4 tables. Then they're compared: every table's cells' exact and shown text, and `gridDiffers` after the fold and at each grid. The controls: TanStack's fold one tick short against GPUI's, and at each grid TanStack's tables moved 2 px.
- **`tanstack-contract` at the wall level** (port 4390):
  - The first screen is prerendered and hydrated in place; usable ≤ open < filled; the probe key.
  - `filled` is presented across the tables: every table's visible rows (`row:<50t + r>`) are painted, through the reader the other levels use (`cellsPainted` now takes a list of rows).
  - With `?measure`: the state (`rows` 50, `tables` the stop's), the batches in order with a generation a table, the refreshed marks, and each table's generation drawn, none past its last.
  - The replay's `open()` refills every table and ends the feed; `fling()` and `jump()` are refused.
  - None of it is published unmeasured, and the marks are relayed from a frame.
- **`tanstack-parity` at the wall level** (ports 4391–4392), in Chromium, Firefox, and WebKit:
  - The grid against GPUI's (its wall build in Chromium) at each stop and on a phone, with the control. Both sides' grids are read in each browser at its defaults: headless, none of them has a scrollbar with width.
  - Then at 6 tables, light and dark: Chg %'s sign colors, Last alone flashing in every table at the mix, and a 200-tick feed lighting cells whose fade ends.
  - Market watch's fade check now also requires the short feed to light a cell.
- All three were already in `check` (`wall`, `tanstack-contract`, `tanstack-parity`), so their wall sections now run there.

## After the code review

- **The grid's control could not fail.** It compared GPUI at 6 tables with TanStack at 8, so it differed on the table count alone. It's now the same tables moved 2 px, which must differ in every table, at every grid, in `wall.ts` and in `tanstack-parity`.
- **Table widths and heights weren't compared.** The last table in a row or in a phone's column could be mis-sized and pass. Both sides now publish each table's frame, and `gridDiffers` compares its four edges.
- **The layout probe published GPUI's whole `TallTableLayout`** (its cells' width, the columns again, and `format`'s advances), and TanStack only three fields. Both now publish the same three numbers, as the contract says.
  - The size the first version was charged with (+1.5 KiB) was measured against B2's baseline. Against HEAD built today, the first version was +0.7 KiB and this one is +1.2 KiB: the frame's box costs a little more than leaving out the bridge's layout saved.
- **Two sides, two scrollbar settings:** the parity script read GPUI's reference in a browser at its defaults and TanStack's in its `launch`, which shows classic scrollbars. Both now read in each browser at its defaults.
- **A fade check that couldn't fail:** a short feed that lit nothing passed "the fade ends". It now requires a lit cell first, at the wall level and market watch's.
- **Copies:** the grid rules live in `wall-grid.ts` alone. The wall's contract reuses `cellsPainted` and `presented`, which now take a list of rows.
- **Left as it was:** "more than 50 batches in 1.5 s" in the contract's wall and market sections, a margin of about 28 on this machine.
