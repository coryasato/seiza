# M7: `trim-paths`, no home paths in the shipped wasm (2026-11-01)

Measured on Apple M1, Darwin 25.6.0 arm64, machine `local`. Sizes are exact bytes of one build each; TTFP is `cold --check`'s reference run, Chromium headless=new, 1440×900 at DPR 2, median of 10 (root CLAUDE.md, "Measurement protocol").

Raw: `2026-11-01-m7-wasm.json` (the GPUI contender's wasm before and after, with its home paths counted), `2026-11-01-m7-trim-paths.json` (`sizes`: the shipped wasm, the untrimmed control, the panel's variants).

## What changed

- The root release profile remaps build paths in the compiled output (`trim-paths = "object"`; root `docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Toolchain and build"). Registry crates' panic locations become `<crate>-<version>/src/…`, the standard library's `/rustc/<commit>/…`.
- `sizes` (and so `check`, through `sizes --check`) fails if the shipped wasm holds the builder's `$HOME`, `/Users/`, or `/home/` (`shared-web/perf/home-paths.ts`).

| The GPUI contender's wasm | Before | After | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Raw (cargo's output) | 13,173.9 KiB | 13,136.5 KiB | −37.4 KiB |
| After wasm-bindgen and wasm-opt (shipped) | 9,743.7 KiB | 9,702.8 KiB | −40.9 KiB |
| Brotli 11 | 2,708.0 KiB | 2,707.2 KiB | −0.8 KiB |
| Home-directory paths | 979 | 0 | |

The paths compress to almost nothing: 41 KiB before brotli is under 1 KiB after it.

## Against the budgets

| Check | Budget | Reference | Pass |
|---|---|---|---|
| TTFP (`cold --check`) | 187.6 ms (baseline 170.5 + 10%) | 173.3 ms | yes |
| Wasm, brotli | 3,099.7 KiB (old baseline + 15%) | 2,707.2 KiB | yes |

`perf/baseline.json`'s `wasmBrotliKiB` rewritten to 2,707.2 (was 2,695.4, from 2026-10-16; the contender has grown since without a rewrite). Its TTFP was kept: the check passed, and a change of 0.8 KiB brotli can't move first paint. The file's `date` stays the TTFP's.

## Experiments and controls

- **The setting on the pinned nightly:** `cargo-features = ["trim-paths"]` in the root manifest, no `-Z` flag needed; `"object"` covers the panic locations (`file!()` in `Location`), so `"all"` and the `RUSTFLAGS` route weren't needed.
- **Control:** the contender built without the setting (`CARGO_PROFILE_RELEASE_TRIM_PATHS=none`, in `target/untrimmed/`, then wasm-bindgen and wasm-opt as shipped) holds 987 home paths, and the check fails it (`sizes` without `--check`). (979 in the build before the change; the control builds in another target directory, the likely cause of the 8 more, not checked).
- **No other absolute path:** after the change the shipped wasm holds no `/Users/` or `/home/` at all.

## Reproducing it

```sh
just observatory gpui-build
just observatory sizes --label m7-trim-paths
just observatory cold --check
```
