## The checks (`page --check`)

Every existing item passed, plus:
- **The Overview:** the rule "Use the DOM for small tables. Use GPUI past ~380 cells on screen." (as the review's fixes left it, below). N ~380 is re-derived from the shares: the last stop where TanStack's slower steady scroll draws new cells in ≥ 90 % of the display's frames on the fast device. The rule is in the prerendered HTML. With no numbers, `galleryOf` gives no rule (the control). The card ends at 612.6 px (the window is 900) with two tiles, and "More components coming" isn't a link. The galleries' chunk loads, and it isn't in the first-paint set.
- **The component's page:** the cards' verdicts are "TanStack ready 3.4× sooner; GPUI smoother on a slow device" and "TanStack keeps up to ~380 cells; GPUI keeps up at every stop, to ~940", the latter the panel's fast-device verdict. The wide sheet's reach is fast GPUI ~940 cells, TanStack ~380 cells; slow none, none (re-derived). Its row links `wide-sheet/#compare`, and Big file's no-dial line is there. Each scenario's mechanisms match its copy, and the five definitions are anchored.
- **`#compare`:** it opened "How they compare: Wide sheet" and cleared the fragment, and a reload showed the table. The wide sheet's "Why" links `/observatory/tall-table/#why`.
- **A phone:** neither page is wider than 390 px (before the fix, the component page was 296 px wider: the mechanisms' screen-reader text escaped its scroller). Hydration logged no errors on either page, desktop or phone.

## After the code review

`/code-review high` found ten issues, and all are fixed. `page --check` passes again: first paint 79.5 ms (60.0–136.8, load average 5.4), first-paint JS 84.2 KiB. The run before it read 87.4 ms and failed on first paint alone, at a load average of 5.3, with every check passing and the JS unchanged.
- **The check's rule followed one branch of the page's three:** it now re-derives each one. GPUI's clause is "past" the DOM's last stop, or "from" the first where the DOM keeps up at none. The DOM's half appears where it keeps up. With neither half, the rule waits. Before the fix, a DOM that keeps up nowhere would have crashed the run.
- **The rule's DOM half was fixed text:** it's now said only where the DOM keeps up at a scenario's smaller stops, as "Use the DOM for small tables." M8's "text-heavy" waits for the admin table.
- **One rule for every component's card:** each component now has its own, from its own scenarios (`ruleOf`, `Gallery.rules`).
- **A `#compare` that never opened its panel would have thrown on the next read** and aborted the run. The check now records the failure and goes on.
- **The rest, with no effect on what the page shows:**
  - `cold.ts`'s comments now say `virtual:gallery` is the galleries' chunk's, not first-paint JS.
  - The numbers are resolved once per build for both modules (and again after a source changes), and once in the check.
  - The series dot and the series classes are in one place, `components.ts` (`seriesDot`, `seriesClass`, written out whole for Tailwind's scan); the window re-exports the dot.
  - The side names come from each component's contenders.
  - The drawings' widths are rounded, so Node's markup and a browser's match.
