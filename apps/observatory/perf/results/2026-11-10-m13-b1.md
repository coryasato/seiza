# M13 part B1: the monitoring wall's data, scenario, and feed fixture (2026-11-10)

No timing runs in this part: its checks are the fixtures, the data's build, and what the change adds to the TanStack contender's JS. The JS sizes are brotli at quality 11 of each chunk as built, deterministic, so no machine label is needed. They were measured with a one-off script over `dist/assets/`, at HEAD (the change stashed) and with the change, each level built by `just observatory tanstack-build [level]`.

## What changed

- **The data:** `wall-instruments.parquet`, by `prep.ts --only wall` (native DuckDB v1.4.5). It holds 400 rows (8 desks of 50, in the feed's order), 3,716 B, one row group, SHA-256 `5cbf2407…`, with the columns `Desk`, `Symbol`, `Last`, `Change %`, `Volume`. NASDAQ Trader's directories of 2026-11-10 (5,607 + 7,674 securities, AAPL and IBM the control) list none of the 400, and none repeats market watch's 50. Market watch's file rebuilt to its SHA-256 of record (`cb49e007…`).
- **The feed:** `Feed` takes its instruments' prices (market watch's 50 by default). Market watch's fixture regenerated byte-identical (`c824a33a…`, `write.ts --check`).
- **The wall's feed fixture** (`contenders/wall/fixtures/`): seed `0x3a11`, 2,000 ticks over the 400 instruments in 10 batches, SHA-256 `975414de…`. 384 instruments have a volume after them, and the dropped tick is index 1000. Each stop's feed is pinned by its bytes' SHA-256 too: 4 tables `ed3547cc…`, 6 `6cb23d78…`, 8 the fixture's own.
- **No wasm changes:** the GPUI crate changed only in its tests.

## The contenders' JS, with the change against HEAD (brotli, bytes)

| Build | Chunk | HEAD | With the change | Δ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| TanStack base | first-paint (`index`) | 75,564 | 75,749 | +185 |
| TanStack market | first-paint (`index`) | 77,805 | 77,946 | +141 |
| TanStack market | the feed's worker | 1,711 | 1,744 | +33 |
| either | every other chunk | | | 0 (±1 B of hash) |

The first-paint bytes are `MONITORING_WALL` (and its desks' names) in `ALL_SCENARIOS`, which every contender build's `pickScenario` carries. The worker's are `Feed`'s new parameter and its check: the worker's ticks are unchanged (the fixture). The GPUI contender's JS host imports the same two modules, so its levels change the same way, and its wasm doesn't. `tanstack-sizes --check` passes at both levels: base first-paint 74 KiB against 79.3, data layer 34.8 against 38.2; market 76.1 against 83.1, and 40.9 against 44.8.

## Experiments and controls

- **The fixture's controls**, on both stores, over the wall's fixture as over market watch's: a store skipping the dropped trade, one keeping only each instrument's last tick in a batch, and one computing the change % as `(last / open − 1) × 100` each fail it. A store sized for market watch's 50 refuses the wall's batches, on both sides. Each stop's feed regenerates its pinned bytes, and 4 desks' bytes don't match 6's. One volume in the wall's `feed.json` raised by 100 failed GPUI's `feed_fixture` with "monitoring wall", restored after.
- **`write.ts --check`** before the wall's fixture existed failed it and passed market watch's.

## Reproducing it

```sh
just observatory data --only wall
node apps/observatory/contenders/market/fixtures/write.ts --check
just observatory fixtures
just observatory tanstack-build && just observatory tanstack-build market
node apps/observatory/perf/tanstack-sizes.ts --check
node apps/observatory/perf/tanstack-sizes.ts --check --level market
```
