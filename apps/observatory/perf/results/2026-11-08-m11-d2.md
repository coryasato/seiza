# M11 part D2: live tail's numbers (2026-11-08)

Measured on Apple M1, Darwin 25.6.0 arm64, machine `local`, Chromium 153.0.8010.12 headless=new, 1440×900 at DPR 2, cold (a fresh browser per run), both contenders alternated, reference and throttled (CPU 4×, Fast 4G); **median of 10 runs** per side, stop, and profile, the soak 3. gpui-kit 0.9.0, React 19.3.1, TanStack Virtual 3.14.19, Arrow 17.0.0, DuckDB-Wasm 1.32.0 (each file's `versions`). The tail level's release builds of both contenders, built right before the runs from the reviewed code (part D1's review). One sitting, 13:46 to 17:42 local time: the tail at every stop (1 h 27 min), the soak (2 h 4 min), then `usable`, `data`, and `memory` (25 min). Load average 1.2–4.4 over the sitting (2.0–2.5 most of it). Quiet by the spread gate ((max − min) / median under 6%): the busy loop, CPU alone, spread at most 5.1% in any tail setting (10k throttled; ≤ 2.3% in 13 of 16), 114.9–117.6 ms at the reference and 451.0–479.6 throttled over every tail and soak run. Every run passed: no failures in any file.

The scripts date their files in UTC, so the once-a-scenario files, written after 16:00 local time, are dated 2026-11-09.

Raw (each labeled `live-tail-<stop>`):
- `2026-11-08-m11-d2-tail-live-tail-1k.json`, `-10k`, `-50k`, `-100k`: the tail at each stop (`perf/metric-tail.ts`): each run's Row shown in, keeps up, the frame share, stamp → mark and mark → presentation, the rows behind, late frames, work, busy time, collections, the scroll back and its control, and the busy loop.
- `2026-11-08-m11-d2-soak-live-tail-100k.json`: the soak (`perf/metric-soak.ts`), 10 minutes, each run's start and end windows and its memory after each, and after a forced collection.
- `2026-11-09-m11-d2-{usable,data,memory}-live-tail-10k.json`: the once-a-scenario metrics at the default stop.

- **At the reference both sides hold steady for 10 minutes:** Row shown in, late frames, and the rows behind don't move. Memory, less the canvas, grows 21 MiB on GPUI (10.8 of it the wasm's linear memory, which never shrinks) and 10.2 on TanStack, and a forced collection frees none of it on either side. GPUI's JavaScript outside the wasm grows by about the same 10 MiB as TanStack's, the same on every run, so that part is common to both pages (the page's code or the harness under `?measure`) rather than either view's. Not taken apart here.
- **On a slow device at 100k, the trailing is mostly the replay's first seconds.** In the window 1 s after the first tail mark, both sides trail by 0.4–1.3 s (the tail metric's window, which opens at the same moment, reads the same). After 10 minutes both run 69–111 ms behind at p50, within 6–21k rows of the tail. **Neither keeps up even then** (p95 118–167 ms on GPUI, 154–215 on TanStack), so 100k's verdict on a slow device stands either way. Where between the two windows each side catches up isn't measured: the soak reads only its two windows.
- **Collections halve by the end on both sides** (GPUI 13–14 → 5–7, TanStack 29–37 → 15–18): fewer as the page warms, the ratio between the sides unchanged.

## GPUI's second canvas backing store

GPUI's page counts its canvas twice (39.6 MiB against 19.8) in some readings. Across this part's runs:
- **The soak:** never at the reference (0 of 9 readings); throttled in 3 of 6 readings before a collection (one run's start and end, another's end) and 0 of 3 after a forced collection.
- **`memory`'s equal work:** never at the open on either device (40 of 40 readings at 19.8). At the end, 4 of 10 reference runs and 9 of 10 throttled, and **a forced collection didn't free it** in any of them.

So it comes with continuous drawing, more often on a slow device, and isn't always garbage. The page's Memory card already leaves GPUI's canvas out (`readouts.ts`: "less GPUI's canvas, which holds one window-sized image or more"), so no card changes. What holds the second backing store isn't looked into here.

## The throttle's control

The busy loop after each window took 114.9–117.6 ms at the reference and 451.0–479.6 throttled over every tail and soak run: each setting's median **4.1×** on both sides. D1's check (a throttled run under 3.5× fails) passed in all 86 throttled runs, so every throttled time is real.

## Against D1's smoke runs and A2's spike

**D1's finding holds on the full protocol:** at 100k on a slow device both sides trail by about half a second or more (the medians' p50 GPUI 699 ms, TanStack 757; D1: p50 GPUI 0.76–1.24 s, TanStack 1.00–1.26 s; here 0.49–0.88 and 0.65–0.93). At 50k on a slow device TanStack misses by a little and GPUI keeps up (D1's three runs: TanStack 109–118, GPUI 80–106; here 98.9–109.0 and 77.6–81.9). D1's one reference miss at 100k was noise: GPUI's p95 is 43.1–46.7 ms in all 10 runs here.

Against the spike (`2026-11-07-m11-a2.md`; the contenders here):

| Stop | Profile | Spike: GPUI p95 | Contender: GPUI | Spike: TanStack p95 | Contender: TanStack |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1k | reference | 28.7 | 26.1 | 27.1 | 27.7 |
| 100k | reference | 49.1 | 44.8 | 28.8 | 49.2 |
| 10k | throttled | 59.3 | 59.3 | 55.0 | 75.4 |
| 50k | throttled | 83.7 | 79.8 | 54.5 | 101.7 |
| 100k | throttled | 1,734 | 816 | 53.0 | 843 |

- **GPUI's contender matches its spike or beats it,** the 100k slow-device lag about half the spike's.
- **TanStack's contender costs more than its spike as the rate grows:** even at 1k, 1.7× at 100k at the reference, and from keeping up at every stop to missing at 50k and trailing at 100k on a slow device. A2 attributed the spike's lead to a lighter data path: the spike ported `follow` in brief (no re-anchor, no thumb mapping) over its own page reads, and the contender runs the table's whole paging, `follow`, and `live` (D1-4, closed with the user). Busy time agrees: the spike's TanStack 504–590 ms/s throttled, the contender's 661–754.
- So **"TanStack's limit is above the dial" (`docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Scenarios", 2026-11-07) doesn't hold on the contenders:** both sides' edge on a slow device lies inside the dial, TanStack's between 10k and 50k, GPUI's between 50k and 100k.

## Reproducing it

```sh
just observatory gpui-build tail && just observatory tanstack-build tail
just observatory metric tail --scenario live-tail --stop all --runs 10 --label m11-d2-tail
just observatory metric soak --scenario live-tail --label m11-d2-soak
just observatory metric usable --scenario live-tail --runs 10 --label m11-d2-usable
just observatory metric data --scenario live-tail --runs 10 --label m11-d2-data
just observatory metric memory --scenario live-tail --runs 10 --label m11-d2-memory
```
