# M13 part C1: the TanStack contender at the wall level (2026-11-10)

Measured on Apple M1, Darwin 25.6.0 arm64, machine `local`, Chromium headless=new. The sizes are brotli at quality 11, deterministic. `perf/wall.ts` runs at 1440×900 and DPR 1 (2560×2600 for the fold), not the measurement protocol: its times say each batch was paired with a near frame, and Shown in is part D's to measure.

Raw: `2026-11-10-m13-c1-tanstack-sizes-wall.json` (the level's JS, its baseline), `2026-11-10-m13-c1-wall.json` (`perf/wall.ts`, both sides).

## What changed

- **The level:** `wall` in `BUILT.tanstack`. Its code sits behind `__LEVEL__ === 'wall'`, and `live`'s branches in `pages.ts`, `paging.ts`, and `live.ts` are widened to it (`'market' || 'tail' || 'wall'`).
- **The data layer:** `data/wall.ts`, the GPUI side's `scene/wall.rs` and `wall.rs` on the DOM side. It checks the file (its five columns, 400 rows), reads the stop's desks' rows once through the engine to seed market watch's `QuoteStore`, then builds a `TablePaging` a desk over a `DeskSource`, every read in hand. Then the formats at 12 px, Last's flash, and the feed: one worker, each batch folded and each desk's ticked runs invalidated in its task, with `__contender.batches`' generations a table and visible mask. With `?readout`, `WallRing`, pooled across the tables.
- **The view:** `wall.ts` and `view.ts`'s `WallGrid`. Each desk is the contender's own `Table` (its scroller, its TanStack Virtual, market watch's `MarketRow`) under a title. One `WallFrame` an open does three things: commits every table whose pages landed in one `flushSync` in the frame's callback, marks `filled` across the tables (each table's `TableContract` reports to it, `wallContract`), and sets one `refreshed` in a microtask after each commit that raised any table's generation. The first table holds the focus and publishes the state (with `tables`) and the targets.
- **The styles:** `wall.css` (the grid, the density's rows, market watch's `market.css`), `wallCss` (the grid's numbers from the scenario, and the one column under `oneColumnBelow`), and `wall-theme.css`, the sidebar color, generated by `theme_css.rs` beside `theme.css`.
- **`main.ts`:** the stop and `?ticks` parsed, the open (`openWall`), the feed started at the first `filled` across the tables, and the replay `open()` alone (`wallReplay`).

## Experiments and controls

- **The view beside the GPUI side's**, screenshots at DPR 1: 6 tables light, 8 dark, the phone at 4. They match in the grid, the titles, the gutter, the columns, and the cells. Two differences:
  - The first build's rows and header stopped at the last column, where the tall table draws them to the frame's edge. `.wall .head, .wall .spacer { min-width: 100% }` fixed it.
  - A phone's DOM table shows a seventh row's top in the 16 px the GPUI table keeps for its scrollbar. This is expected: the DOM has no horizontal scrollbar when nothing overflows, and the height is the GPUI side's, as decided.
  - (A first try at DPR 2 drew GPUI's grid as a phone's column: the headless DPR sizing gotcha, which the harness's `hideDevicePixelContentBox` avoids and a throwaway script didn't.)
- **The data layer's control:** `test/wall.test.ts`'s visible mask fails with the mask computed as every ticked desk.

## After the code review

- **Element Timing names:** each table's cells had carried `row:<r>`, so 8 tables' names collided. They now carry the instrument's row, `row:<50t + r>`. At 8 tables with `?measure`: 705 entries over 176 distinct rows, 22 a table (16 on screen and the overscan), every name a number the metrics parse. Market watch's bundle stays byte-identical with the branch in `MarketRow`.
- **Only the tables with a landed page render** in the frame's one commit, not all N.
- **The replay's `open()`** waits for a `filled` from after the reopen, so the last open's tables can't resolve it. Run ad hoc at 6 tables: resolved 35 ms later on the new open's `filled`, all 6 tables back; `jump` rejected.
- **The rest:** the phone table's border named (`BORDERS`) and the copied scrollbar room pointed at its GPUI source and the cross-side check; `theme_css.rs`'s color-scheme and radius as two flags, both files unchanged (`theme-css --check`).
- Afterwards: the base, wide, market, and tail builds byte-identical to HEAD's again; `npm test` (96, none failing); `tanstack-sizes --level wall --check`; `perf/wall.ts` on both sides (the table above is this run).
