Measured on Apple M1, Darwin 25.6.0 arm64, machine `local`, Chromium 153 headless=new, 1440×900 at DPR 2. `burst` is one run per use, unthrottled (a peak, not a timing: `perf/burst.ts`). Raw: `2026-11-14-m14-f-burst.json` (the requests one visitor sends in the busiest 10 s, fourteen uses, counted at the server).

## What changed

- **The README:** the wide sheet's intro says that a visitor resizes, selects, and copies in the same frame, and a section of its own, "Resizing, selecting, copying", says what the tools do on both sides (the key's 100,000-cell limit and the confirming key, the 64 MiB cap, the copy's reads beside the view's and its turns over frames). It says where each side wins. GPUI wins a drag's smoothness (the resize on both devices, the selection's slowest frames and its slow device), the week and the sheet copied sooner on a slow device, and the scroll during the sheet's copy. TanStack wins a selection's typical frame on a fast device, the visible block copied sooner, and the sheet copied sooner on a fast device, with twice GPUI's main-thread time. Even: the week on a fast device, and the same cells and text on both. Two generated tables follow: the drags at each stop, and the copies at the default stop. "Canvas tradeoffs" (root rule 4) gains one bullet: GPUI selects and copies whole cells only, and neither side resizes by touch or a key. The top paragraph names the tools.
- **`perf/budgets.json`:** the 62 metrics part E2 recorded with `show: []` show in the README's `readme:wide-sheet-drags` (48: drawn share, work p95, and late frames for both drags at every stop, both devices) and `readme:wide-sheet-copies` (14: the three copies' times, the scroll during the sheet's copy and without it, the cells and the sheet's bytes).
- **`burst` covers the copy:** `copy` (the wide sheet's route; on each side every cell selected, the window's Copy pressed, a fling while it copies, the clipboard written).

No build changed in this part: `burst.ts`, the README, and `budgets.json` run in none. Every level was rebuilt from HEAD (`just observatory builds`) before the burst and the deploy.

## The deploy

`just observatory deploy` from this session (2026-11-14): the builds staged with a quality-11 `.br` beside each build file (109 files; DuckDB's two oversize wasm left out, served as `.br` and `.gz`), `wrangler deploy` (118 assets, 93 already uploaded), then `worker-check --wait`. **PASS:** 103 build files, 27.2 MiB on the wire as br, each exactly the staged `.br`, among them the grown wide level's builds on both sides; the two oversize wasm as `.gz` without br; a 304, the redirects, a 404; each of the six data files a ranged HEAD and `bytes=0-0` → 206, its closed ranges byte-exact, no Content-Encoding, its SHA-256 the `MANIFEST.json` pin.
