## Experiments and controls

- **The kept build is not M9's build.** It's `fe36e4c`'s, which had the text change and gpui-kit 0.9.1; M9 measured before both. The A/B measures parts B and C (and M15's shared code), not what the page has shown since M9. The reruns caught it (GPUI ~half of M9's work at every stop), and rows-26 ran again.
- **Every reading the A/B paired was whole:** no failures, 10 rounds a reading at every stop.
- **Drags and copies:** no control runs here; D1's ran their controls (`2026-11-13-m14-d1.md`) on code this build doesn't change.
- **TanStack's sideways at ~380:** in the reruns its runs read 80.7–99.2%, under the line in 5 of 10. It isn't re-run for a better median: the rule is the median of 10.

## Reproducing it

```sh
just observatory gpui-build wide && just observatory tanstack-build wide
# the kept build: as in 2026-11-13-m14-d1.md, in target/ab/m9-wide/
just observatory ab --build target/ab/m9-wide --label m14-d2-ab --rounds 10
just observatory metric drag --scenario wide-sheet --stop all --runs 10 --label m14-d2-drag
just observatory metric copy --scenario wide-sheet --runs 10 --label m14-d2-copy
just observatory metric scroll --scenario wide-sheet --label m14-d2-scroll          # every stop
for m in usable data response reach cost memory; do
  just observatory metric $m --scenario wide-sheet --label m14-d2-$m                # the default stop
done
just observatory report
```
