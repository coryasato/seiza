## Reach and the contract

`just observatory reach`, the protocol's page:

- **Reach:** native DuckDB (data/'s) counts 1,567,523 rows and gives the last row's spkid, 54657680. With `?measure`, `state.rows` is 1,567,523; End shows rows 1,567,495–1,567,523, all loaded, `lastCell` 54657680. The filled mark after End came 43.4 ms after the key, 0.24 ms after the presenting task's last GPU call, in that task (`presentedMark`); the first filled mark after the open 0.41 ms after its own.
- **Marks:** first-pixels (the placeholder's paint) ≤ usable (the first frame) ≤ open < filled, set with and without `?measure`.
- **State and targets only with `?measure`:** `table`, `table-scroll-track`, `table-scroll-thumb` inside the viewport with it; without it `state` is null and there are no targets.
- **Replay:** `jump(0.9)` → first row 1,410,770 (⌊0.9 × 1,567,523⌋), filled, 42 ms, and again (onto the rows already loaded, which sets no filled mark) 39 ms; `open()` → a new `contender:open`, filled at row 0, 40 ms; `fling()` (180 equal wheel events on the canvas, one per frame) → the last row, filled, 3,029 ms.
- **Relay:** framed in a same-origin page, the four contract marks reached the parent by `postMessage`, each `at` the mark's epoch time.

## The placeholder

Light and dark, DPR 2 and 1: every surface pixel within the limits (0.0000% differ) and the status line 0 device px across from GPUI's, 1 (DPR 2) and 2 (DPR 1) up or down, ink mass 0.85–1.02×: a match. `index.html` 20,296 B raw, 11,208 B brotli (the placeholder's font inlined).

## Experiments and controls

- **The placeholder's negative controls** (light, DPR 2), each must fail the match, and did: the frame 1 px left (0.100% of surface pixels differ), 1 px shorter (0.162%), the border's color (0.351%), the frame's fill (93.6%), the text 1 px up (3 device px off), in the foreground color (ink 1.71×), in a fallback font (10 device px across). Two first controls didn't fail and were replaced: the text 1 px *down* (the placeholder's text sits 1 device px above GPUI's, so 2 device px down lands 1 below, inside the ±1 tolerance) and no rounded corners (~120 device px differ, under the surface share's limit of ~2,000).
- **The panel-leak check's controls:** Cargo enables `seiza`'s `panel` for the contender built with Tycho (unified), and not built alone; the build with `--features seiza/panel` holds `__seizaPerfOverlay`, the shipped one doesn't.

## After the code review

A review of the first version found, and this one fixes: `check` ran the size script's slow path (two extra release builds); the replay hooks waited for a `contender:filled` mark that a view landing already filled never sets (a second `jump(0.9)` waited 30 s and failed; `reach` now runs it, 39 ms); a reopen left a still-running open's queries in the engine (now cancelled); `cacheBytes` was read on every notify of the table (now at most every 100 ms, in a timer's task); `first-pixels` and `usable` followed the first of their source marks rather than the last; `cold` waited a fixed 50 ms for them (now waits for the marks); and the perf scripts redefined `waitMark` and the contract's mark names. `seiza` without `panel` is now linted. The fixes add 2 KiB raw to the wasm (2,695.3 KiB brotli). `just observatory check` after them: TTFP 180.8 ms (159.9–337.1, load 3.3–4.8) vs the 187.6 ms limit, the rest as above: PASS. The reach numbers above are from the run after the fixes.
