# M10 part D2: market watch's numbers (2026-11-06)

Measured on Apple M1, Darwin 25.6.0 arm64, machine `local`, Chromium 153.0.8010.12 headless=new, 1440×900 at DPR 2, cold (a fresh browser per run), **median of 10 runs** per side, stop, and profile, both contenders alternated; reference and throttled (CPU 4×, Fast 4G). gpui-kit 0.9.0, React 19.3.1, TanStack Virtual 3.14.19, Arrow 17.0.0, DuckDB-Wasm 1.32.0 (each file's `versions`). The market level's release builds of both contenders, built right before the runs from the reviewed code (part D1's review). Two sittings: the feed 10:23 to 11:18, then `usable`, `data`, and `memory` 11:18 to 11:42. Load average 4.2 at the start (the builds), 1.6–2.5 during both sittings. Quiet by the spread gate ((max − min) / median under 6%): the busy loop, CPU alone, spread at most 2.8% in any setting (2.1% throttled); time to data's runs 3.5% / 4.0% at the reference, 0.6% / 0.4% throttled. Every run passed: no failures in any file.

Raw (each labeled `market-watch-<stop>`):
- `2026-11-06-m10-d2-feed-market-watch-100.json`, `-500`, `-1k`, `-5k`: the feed at each stop (`perf/metric-feed.ts`), each run's keeps up, Shown in, send → mark and mark → presentation, collections, late frames, work, busy time, and the busy loop.

## Shown in's run states (D2-3)

Each run settles in a frame-scheduling state (B1, `docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Measurement"), and here the states were much steadier than B1's three runs suggested:
- **TanStack was in one state in every setting,** its mark → presentation within 0.4 ms across all 10 runs (18.2 ms at 100, 5.2 at 500, 6.0 at 1k, 7.8 at 5k at the reference; 15.4, 13.4, 12.6, 19.4 throttled). Its state depends on the stop, not on the run: at 100 its commit sits a frame from its presentation, most of its 2.1× Shown in gap.
- **GPUI was in one state at the reference** (mark → presentation 1.1–1.2 ms in all 40 runs) and in two throttled: a fast one at ~0.75 ms, or a slow one whose value follows the stop (~10.1 ms at 100, ~9.5 at 500, ~9.2 at 1k, ~7.9 at 5k). 5 of 10 runs drew the fast one at 100 (so its median, 20.3, falls between the two), 1 at 500 and at 1k, none at 5k. So GPUI's throttled medians at 100–1k lean on how many runs drew the fast state.
- The open question, whether a cold load's long tasks put the DOM side in one state more often than GPUI: no. The DOM side never moved; GPUI's slow-device runs did.

**Decided with the user:** a side is called faster on Shown in only where its median beats the other's by more than 5% (the page's `EVEN`) and the two sides' runs don't overlap; elsewhere "even". Each side's range goes in the results files (it's here, from `shownInP50MsRuns`) and a README footnote (part F). The page's words (part E) take the rule.

- TanStack's collections run ~1.4–1.8× the spike's at every rate, with its keeps up unchanged. Not looked into here: the contender reaches its rows through the table's paging and `live`'s re-reads, which the spike's view didn't have.
