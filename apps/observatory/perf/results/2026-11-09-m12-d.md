# M12 part D: the admin table on the page (2026-11-09)

Measured on Apple M1, Darwin 25.6.0 arm64, machine `local`, Chromium 153.0.8010.12 headless=new, 1440×900 at DPR 2. The page's run is cold, median of 10, reference and throttled (root CLAUDE.md, "Measurement protocol"). Load average 2.3 before the page's run, 3.0 after it. The scripts date their files in UTC, so they read 2026-11-10.

Raw:
- `2026-11-10-m12-d-page.json`: `page --write-baseline`, every check of the page (the admin table's route among them) and its first paint, both devices.
- `2026-11-10-m12-d-memory-admin-table.json`, `-fonts.json`: C2's two memory files (`2026-11-10-m12-c2-memory-admin-table*.json`, unchanged), summarized again from their own runs into new files (`metric memory --summarize --label m12-d-memory`), never measured again.

## What changed

- **The admin table on the page**, as M8's canvas's admin boards draw it (approved by the user at the part's start): its route, the fonts toggle where a dial would be, the status bar's four (a phone's two), the panel "What each side can do", the description and its credits, the gallery's card and drawing, the component's page's line, and the Overview's rule's DOM half, "small, text-heavy" ([`docs/DECISIONS.md`](../../docs/DECISIONS.md), "Page", 2026-11-09).
- **Memory's summary** gains the page less every canvas buffer and the runs holding a second one; C2's two memory files were summarized again into new files (`--summarize`). Every earlier field kept its value; the new ones:

| | Reference: GPUI | Reference: TanStack | Throttled: GPUI | Throttled: TanStack |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Less every canvas buffer, at the end (MiB) | 11.8 | 4.8 | 11.8 | 4.9 |
| A second buffer at the end, runs | 2 of 10 | 0 | 10 of 10 | 0 |
| With fonts, less every canvas buffer (MiB) | 14.1 | — | 14.2 | — |
| With fonts, a second buffer, runs | 10 of 10 | — | 10 of 10 | — |

- **The page's servers serve the script faces** at `/observatory/fonts/` in dev and preview, as the deploy stages them; before, only the GPUI contender's own servers did.
- **No contender changed:** both builds are C2's, byte for byte.

## Against the budgets

| Check | Budget | Reference | Throttled | Pass |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Page: first paint, Big file's route | ≤ 85 ms (the old baseline 70.8 + 20%) | 60.4 ms | 439.3 ms (recorded) | yes |
| Page: first-paint JS (brotli) | ≤ 86.0 KiB (the old baseline 78.2 + 10%) | 86.8 KiB | | **no: re-baselined** |

HEAD's page was 86.0 KiB, at the limit. This part adds 0.8 KiB: about 0.5 for the window's fonts state (the toggle's state, its title bar text, its load row) and 0.3 for the route itself (its copy, its scenario in the list). The toggle's UI and explainer were moved into the numbers' chunk, after first paint, as live tail's follow toggle is; before that move the page was at 87.1. **Decided with the user:** `page --write-baseline` on this build (first paint 60.4 and 439.3 ms, first-paint JS 86.8 KiB), its fraction kept at 10% (limit 95.5 KiB). The first paint budget tightens with it, to 72.5 ms.

## Against HEAD

First paint on Big file's route, reference, 10 cold loads each, HEAD's page build and this one alternated (a scratch script on `perf/harness.ts`'s `servePage` and `protocolContext`), load average 2.5 → 3.1: **HEAD 65.3 ms, this build 65.4** (medians). The first `page --check` run read 154.9 ms (2 runs, 192 and 118) right after its checks, with the load at 10: the machine, not the change.

## The checks

`page` passed every check, the admin table's new ones among them (`2026-11-10-m12-d-page.json`, `readouts.admin`):
- GPUI's frame on the base build with no `?fonts`, and no face fetched (the control). No dial, no tag, the lead; the toggle off, its explainer giving the cold faces' 586 KiB.
- The toggle pressed: the frame with `?fonts`, the button disabled while the faces load. The title bar read "Loading the app…", "Loading the engine…", "Loading the data…", "Ready · warm · loading fonts for 13 scripts…", then "Ready · warm · Fonts loaded after first paint: 582 KiB. CJK drawn by the browser." (the warm label kept after the code review). The frame's marks came in order (`filled`, `fonts-in`, `fonts`), with 13 faces fetched once each. Pressed again, it reloads without them. With the data refused, the fonts frame fails and the toggle stays usable, saying the faces failed; pressed, a reload without them. TanStack's toggle is disabled, its explainer says why, and a flip back comes without the faces.
- The panel: its verdict; the six capabilities, GPUI's no, no, no, no (Partly with fonts), yes, no, TanStack's all yes; the 15 scripts, Arabic, Khmer, and Sinhala drawn wrong. Ready in reads "TanStack 3.4× faster · 7.4× on a slow device", Download "TanStack 15.8× smaller". Memory carries its second-buffer line, and the fonts card the faces' bytes against "none".
- The description's wins as `winsOf` says them; the credits of CLDR, Wikidata, and the Noto faces, whose `OFL.txt` is served. The gallery's card verdict and drawing; the component's page's line; the Overview's rule, "Use the DOM for small, text-heavy tables." On a phone, Ready in and Memory, the toggle "Load fonts", nothing wider than the screen.
- The four other scenarios' "Other scenarios" lists now include the admin table.

## After the code review

Fixed: the toggle stuck on "Loading fonts…" after a failed frame; the second-buffer lines' denominators (the runs each device measured, `runsMeasured`, not those asked for); the title bar's warm label kept once the fonts load; `--summarize` writing a new file rather than editing C2's, and failing on runs that lack what it reads; the scripts' verdicts in a pure module the build checks (a missing one no longer throws in every route's numbers); the page check's theme (the `.dark` class, three checks); the rule's HTML check by its halves; `wins` shared with the admin table; the footnote's count and version from the data. `page --check` after them passed every check: first paint 71.1 ms against the new limit of 72.5 (measured right after the checks, whose load the runs inherit), first-paint JS 86.7 KiB against 95.5.

## Experiments and controls

- The control for the fonts path: the default frame fetched no face.
- `--summarize`'s control: an older memory file (`2026-10-20-m5-d1-memory.json`, whose runs predate the window's canvas reading) fails and writes nothing.
- The 154.9 ms first paint, tested against HEAD above, was the machine's load.
