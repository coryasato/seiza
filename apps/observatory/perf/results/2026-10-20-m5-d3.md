# M5 part D3: the meter (2026-10-20)

The meter part C designed (https://claude.ai/artifact/6TUq4g7KJ3jrFdKVUGRY67) over the contender's frame: the frame-time graph over 10 s and the memory graph over a minute, one canvas each, FPS and the late count in its corner; open, a pill, or hidden, in one of four corners. Measured on Apple M1, Darwin 25.6.0 arm64, machine `local`, Playwright's Chromium headless=new.

No JSON of its own: `check` writes none. The page's numbers below are `page --check`'s run.

## What changed

- `web/src/meter.ts` (new): the meter, its state (`seiza-observatory-meter`), the drag, the picker, the canvases. In the numbers' chunk (`numbers.ts` re-exports it): loaded on its own, it shared `readouts.ts` with the numbers, and Rollup hoisted the shared code into a chunk the entry imports, +2.2 KiB of first-paint JS.
- `metrics/metrics.ts`: `meterReadings` (the bars in display frames, the late count by Smooth's rule, FPS or idle, the minute's memory); `LiveRecord.memory` is now the last minute's measurements, not only the latest.
- `web/src/live.ts`: `subscribeMeter` and `meterSnapshot`; no memory measurement during a scroll (part C's rule, not yet applied in D2); no refresh while the page is hidden.
- `numbers.ts`: the status bar's Meter and the phone's overflow item work. `slot.ts` mounts the meter in the stage after first paint, not while the panel is open. `explainer.ts`: an `above-start` placement (the FPS note above a bottom-corner meter). `page.css`: the meter's styles.
- `perf/page.ts`: the meter's checks; the screenshots scroll first so the meter has bars.
- The code review's fixes: the meter's clock is the frames' (`Date.now()` drifts from `timeOrigin` + `performance.now()`, by a sleep's length after one, which would have read every frame as outside the window); a scroll just begun (no frames yet) no longer ends the walk back through the scrolls; a bar's amber is judged on the unrounded interval, as the late count is; the refresh period from the latest 600 intervals only, not the session's; the canvases redraw on a resize; the memory slots and interval are the metrics module's constants; the drag moves the meter by a transform.

## The checks (`perf/page.ts`, in `check`)

- **Where it starts:** open at the bottom right on a desktop, 12 px from the frame's corner; the status bar's Meter pressed.
- **Under it, the frame:** at the frame graph's center the page's hit test is the iframe, and a click and a wheel there reach the contender's window (`pointerdown`, `wheel`), on GPUI and TanStack. Its buttons take the pointer. Control: the meter set to take the pointer, the frame got nothing, FAIL as it should.
- **Graphs:** a scroll with one 80 ms task in it: FPS 57–58 mid-scroll, "idle" at rest; "1 late" and amber bars (468 amber pixels of 3,432 drawn on GPUI, of 2,808 on TanStack), the on-time bars gray.
- **Drag:** the header dragged across the frame to its top left shows four outlines, the nearest the top left, and snaps there (12 px in); dragged back, bottom right. Control: `setPointerCapture` stubbed, a drag across the frame loses its release to the frame and the meter stays where it was (bottom left), FAIL as it should.
- **Picker and keys:** the picker opens with focus on its pressed corner; ArrowUp moves it there; Esc closes it with focus on its button; ArrowLeft on the header moves it; picking the bottom left closes the picker, and its label says "Meter position: bottom left".
- **Kept per visitor:** a reload keeps the corner and the mode (open, then the pill); hidden stays hidden over a reload, Meter unpressed, and Meter shows it again.
- **The panel:** Compare hides the meter; Esc brings it back.
- **After a flip:** TanStack's meter tagged "warm", in GPUI's corner.
- **Phone:** the pill at first load, and also with the meter stored open; the overflow's "Hide the meter" hides it.
